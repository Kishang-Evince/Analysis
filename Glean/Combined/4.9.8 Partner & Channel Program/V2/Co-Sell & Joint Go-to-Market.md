## Headline

All three cloud marketplaces (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) are reconfirmed as real listings with committed-spend drawdown - but none of the three partner pages discloses formal ACE-style co-sell mechanics (registration through the cloud provider's own systems, co-sell incentive structures). The Google Cloud partnership page is dated January 26, 2023 - genuinely old, and should be treated as potentially stale rather than assumed current. This pass also identified the Glean:GO 2026 Partner Summit (San Francisco, August 25, 2026) as the actual named launch event for the Partner Network, and found that the accreditation-count discrepancy flagged in the Partner Program Details field is more precisely a same-day reporting discrepancy - two outlets covering the identical August 25, 2026 event reported different figures (1,000+ vs. 2,000+), not a two-week growth trend as initially hypothesized. Target-account mapping and MDF dollar thresholds both remain confirmed absent from public sources.

## Independent read

- Claim 2's dated-source finding is worth flagging prominently: a January 2023 blog post being the primary public source for a specific cloud partnership's co-sell mechanics, in a September 2026 evaluation, is a real currency risk - this relationship may have evolved substantially since then, and the page shouldn't be treated as reflecting today's actual co-sell terms without confirmation.
- Claim 6's refinement matters for how the earlier discrepancy gets framed in any summary: "same-day conflicting press reports" is a meaningfully different (and arguably more concerning) finding than "the program grew fast," and changes what question should be asked of Glean directly.
- Claim 9 is a small but genuine piece of cross-validation - when the same specific number appears independently in two different research passes from two different source types (a vendor's own marketplace page and a third-party TCO analysis), that's a real signal the figure is a stable, intentional claim rather than noise.
