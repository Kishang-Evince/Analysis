## Headline

The core program structure (four pathways, no join fee, 1-2 week review) is reconfirmed directly. This pass adds real, previously undocumented benefits — Market Development Funds (MDF)/co-marketing and an automated deal-registration process — plus a five-category "Build, Sell, Deliver, Operate, Innovate" framework for how partners contribute. Two genuine discrepancies surfaced worth flagging rather than smoothing over: an accreditation count that appears to have roughly doubled (1,000+ at the program's August 25, 2026 public launch, vs. 2,000+ in Glean's own later blog content) within a short window, and inconsistent partner-category counts (4 "ecosystem classifications" on the official page vs. 6 named categories in trade press coverage of the same launch). Tier ladder names (Silver/Gold/Platinum or equivalent) remain confirmed absent from every public source checked.

## Independent read

- Claims 6-7 are this pass's most useful additions: MDF/co-marketing funding and automated deal registration are concrete, decision-relevant partner benefits that weren't previously captured, and both are directly relevant to how favorably a Services & Solutions or Referral pathway choice should be evaluated.
- Claim 8's discrepancy deserves a direct question in the verification stage rather than quiet resolution — a genuine near-doubling of accreditations in roughly two weeks would itself be a notable growth signal worth mentioning, but it's equally possible one of the two figures is simply stale or imprecise, and picking one without checking would be a real accuracy risk.
- Claim 9 matters less for its exact resolution and more as a caution: this project should describe partner categories using the language of whichever specific source is being cited, rather than blending "four ecosystem classifications" and "six partner categories" into a single number that neither source actually states.
- Claim 1's content-location nuance (pathway names living on the blog/press, not the main partners page) is a small but real detail worth keeping in mind if anyone cites "the partners page says four pathways" — that specific page, checked directly, does not use that exact framing.
