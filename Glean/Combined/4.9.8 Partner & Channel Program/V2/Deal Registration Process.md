## Headline

The ~1-business-day eligibility response and the "defined validity window + confirmation" language are both reconfirmed directly, with real added precision on what happens after registration: Glean's own sales team fully owns qualification, demos, negotiation, and close - the partner's role ends at the warm introduction. The field's central open question - how many days the protection window actually lasts - remains genuinely unpublished anywhere Glean-authored. This pass specifically checked whether the "90-day" figure cited in the evaluation's own SOW methodology could be traced to a Glean source, and it could not: that number appears to trace to generic channel-sales industry guidance (commonly citing 30-90 or 90-180 day ranges across vendors generally), not anything Glean-specific - a fifth instance of this project's recurring "generic industry content mistaken for a vendor-specific claim" pattern, this time embedded in the evaluation's own assumptions rather than found on Glean's site.
