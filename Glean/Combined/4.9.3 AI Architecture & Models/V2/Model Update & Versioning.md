## Headline

Taking the SOW's three sub-questions in turn: **how often** models update has no published fixed cadence anywhere found - updates appear event-driven, tied to each provider's own release/retirement schedule, not a periodic internal cycle. **Is the customer notified** - yes, via release notes and a dedicated sunset/retirement page, though not proactively per-agent (the customer must self-audit which of their own agents are affected). **Can updates be deferred** - no; three separate, dated, real-world precedents confirm the platform actively migrates tenants off older models on its own schedule, with no admin control found for locking or pinning a specific model version indefinitely.

## Independent read

- Claim 4 is worth flagging as a genuine correction-in-progress: earlier research (Model Selection/Flexibility field) concluded model deprecation tracking was scattered across release notes with no dedicated tracker. This pass found a dedicated sunset/retirement page does exist - it just isn't the "deprecations" page that was checked before. The overall conclusion (no single unified *version-freeze* mechanism, and no *deferral* option) still holds, but the retirement-*notice* story is better than previously stated.
- Claim 5 needs a live re-check before being treated as fully settled - flagged honestly rather than presented as equally solid as the directly-fetched claims.
- Claim 8 is the field's most direct answer to "how often" - and the honest answer is "no published cadence; changes happen reactively, tied to provider timelines." This should be stated plainly to a client rather than guessed at (e.g., do not assume "quarterly" or "monthly" without this being confirmed by Glean directly).
- The overall pattern (claims 1, 5-6, 8) matters most for a buyer: "Model Update & Versioning" control, as literally asked by the SOW, resolves to "no dated-snapshot pinning, no deferral option, no published update cadence; instead, Glean manages model transitions on its own event-driven schedule and provides advance notice via release notes/the sunset page, but migration is not optional and timing is not predictable in advance."
