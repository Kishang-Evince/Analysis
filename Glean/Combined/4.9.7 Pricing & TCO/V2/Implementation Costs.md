## Headline

The one clean, contractually-binding line confirmed directly from Glean's own website terms: native cloud-app connector setup is free (one-time), while any custom connector requires a separately-scoped, paid professional-services SOW - a real, legally-worded cost boundary, not a vague estimate. Everything past that boundary - partner PS pricing, actual implementation dollar figures, POC fees - is either genuinely unpublished or only available from third-party estimate sites with no confirmed accuracy. This pass also caught a fourth instance of this project's recurring "generic industry content mistaken for a vendor-specific claim" pattern: Glean's own "Comparing Costs" blog article cites broad AI-search industry cost bands ($15K-$500K+), not Glean-specific implementation pricing - a real risk of being misread as Glean's own quote.

## Independent read

- Claims 1-2 are this field's most solid, load-bearing finding precisely because they come from binding contract language (Exhibit A), not a blog post or a search summary - this is about as close to "confirmed" as pricing-adjacent claims get for a vendor that otherwise discloses very little.
- Claim 5 deserves particular care in any client-facing summary: it would be very easy to misread "Glean's own blog says AI search deployments cost $15K-$500K+" as Glean's own implementation pricing, when the article is actually discussing the broader market. This is exactly the trap this project has now caught four separate times, and it's worth naming explicitly rather than letting a plausible-looking, on-domain number slip through as if it were vendor-specific.
- Claims 7-9 exist to give a realistic sense of the range circulating in the market without overstating confidence in any single number - claim 9's cross-source repetition is a genuinely useful signal (independent sources converging on the same figure is more meaningful than one source repeating itself), while claim 8's single, hedged ("reportedly") source is exactly the kind of number that should not survive into a confident-sounding summary without direct confirmation.

## Confidence

**Doc-Verified, contract-tier** for claims 1, 2 (binding legal terms, not marketing copy). **Doc-Verified** for claims 3, 4, 5, 6 (direct fresh fetches, verbatim quotes). **Search-corroborated, explicit competitor/aggregator caveat** for claim 7. **Search-corroborated, single-source, explicitly low-confidence** for claim 8. **Search-corroborated, cross-source repetition, still vendor-unconfirmed** for claim 9.
