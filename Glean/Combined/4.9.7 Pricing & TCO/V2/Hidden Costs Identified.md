## Headline

The single most consequential finding this pass came from reading Glean's own FlexCredit Terms PDF directly: Glean contractually committed to **not charging for usage-based Services through March 31, 2026** - a real, dated free-usage grace period. Because today's evaluation date is September 2026, **that grace period has already expired**, meaning any tenant now on Enterprise Flex is genuinely being billed for FlexCredit usage that may have been free during an earlier evaluation or pilot. Separately, FlexCredits expire one year after purchase if unused (a real "use it or lose it" cost) and are non-refundable with all sales final. The Customer Support SLA, also read directly, confirms Premium Support carries a real, contractual fee "as specified in the Order Form" - but the document itself does not state a percentage, meaning the "10-12% of license" figure circulating in third-party sources remains unconfirmed by this direct, primary-source check.

## Independent read

- Claim 2 is this pass's standout finding and should be flagged prominently in any summary: a specific, dated, contractually-binding free-usage period that has already lapsed by the time of this evaluation is exactly the kind of fact that changes a cost estimate materially - an organization that piloted Glean before April 2026 and is now scoping a full rollout needs to budget for FlexCredit usage as a real cost line, not assume the free period still applies.
- Claim 5's honest "not confirmed here" finding matters because it directly tests a specific number (10-12%) that has appeared repeatedly in this project's other pricing fields - reading the actual primary SLA document and finding it silent on the percentage is a meaningful downgrade in confidence for that specific figure, even though the fee's *existence* is confirmed.
- Claim 6 is a genuinely new discovery for this project - the tiered uptime/Service-Credit structure had not been surfaced in any prior field's research, and it materially changes what "Premium Support" actually buys (a real financial remedy for downtime, not just faster ticket response).
- Claim 8 is worth highlighting because it's a rare example of Glean's own documentation naming a hidden cost the customer can affirmatively avoid, rather than one that's simply unavoidable overhead - worth calling out as a concrete, actionable recommendation in any Cloud-Prem cost-planning summary.
