## Headline

Glean itself never frames its own pricing tier - a direct fetch of Glean's own "Comparing Costs" perspectives article, the single most on-topic page Glean has published on this exact question, explicitly avoids any premium/mid-market/value self-description or head-to-head price comparison. Every specific positioning claim in this field therefore comes from third-party procurement-data sources, not Glean, and is graded Search-corroborated or Third-party accordingly - never Doc-Verified, per this field's own definition. That said, the third-party picture is consistent and multi-sourced: Glean is repeatedly and independently described as a premium, enterprise-tier product (median contract value roughly $97.5K/year, ~$40-80/user/month, ~100-seat/~$60K minimum entry) positioned above Microsoft Viva Topics and Guru (lower per-user cost, less AI depth or smaller-team focus) and roughly in the same tier as Moveworks and Coveo (comparable or higher enterprise spend, different pricing mechanics). This lines up with, and is reinforced by, this project's own already-confirmed finding that "pricing opacity" is a cataloged weakness for Glean (`Honest Weaknesses.md`) - a vendor that competes on capability breadth and platform consolidation rather than transparent low pricing, consistent with premium-tier positioning by inference, not vendor admission.

## Independent read

- Claim 1 matters as a methodological anchor for the whole field: checking the one Glean-authored page most likely to answer this question directly, and confirming it deliberately does not, is what justifies grading every other claim here as Search-corroborated/Third-party rather than reaching for a false Doc-Verified label just because the underlying question is important.
- Claims 2-3's convergence across independent, differently-motivated third-party sources (a procurement marketplace, independent pricing aggregators) is worth weighting more heavily than any single source alone - this project's own `License Structure Details.md` (V2) already cautioned that some pricing-estimate sites are themselves Glean's competitors with an incentive to inflate cost claims; a procurement-data marketplace like Vendr has a different, more neutral business model (helping buyers benchmark actual spend across many vendors), which is worth noting as a modest confidence upgrade relative to that earlier caution, without overstating it to Doc-Verified.
- Claims 5-6 are included specifically to prevent this field from collapsing "Glean's own cost-related marketing claims" into "evidence of value-tier pricing" - Glean does make cost arguments, but they're about switching-cost flexibility and AI-task efficiency, not sticker-price tier, and conflating the two would misrepresent both this field and the sibling fields it draws from.
- Claim 7 is written as the field's actual answer to the SOW's question, stated as an inference explicitly, not a vendor-confirmed classification - "premium enterprise, by consistent third-party inference" is the honest finding, not "premium, confirmed."
