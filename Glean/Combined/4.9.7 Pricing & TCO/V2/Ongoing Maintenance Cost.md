## Headline

This pass's standout finding directly resolves a gap this project's own `Hidden Costs Identified.md` (V2) left open: that field read Glean's Customer Support SLA PDF directly and found it confirmed a Premium Support fee *exists* but never states *how much* - leaving the commonly-cited "10-12% of license" figure explicitly unconfirmed. This pass found a different Glean-authored primary source - the website-terms contract, Exhibit A, Section 3 - that states the number outright: *"Service Provider offers a free level of business support or Customer can choose to pay an additional 12% of license fee to have premium support."* That closes the gap with a real, Glean-authored, contractual figure, not a third-party estimate. The same contract section also confirms standard support is free, platform updates/monitoring are bundled into the base "fully managed" service with no separate upgrade fee named anywhere, and a dedicated CSM/Solutions Architect is included at no extra charge - a materially more generous inclusion set than typical enterprise SaaS. Renewal itself auto-continues unless the customer gives 30 days' notice, and while the contract doesn't state a renewal price-increase percentage, third-party procurement data consistently converges on a 7-12% default annual escalation, negotiable down with an upfront cap.

## Independent read

- Claim 1 is this field's headline for a reason: it takes a specific number this project had already flagged, twice, as "circulating but unconfirmed by primary source" and closes it with a second, different Glean-authored contractual document. This is a meaningfully stronger evidentiary position than the earlier "the SLA PDF doesn't say" finding, and any future citation of the 12% figure in this project should point to this contract clause, not the earlier third-party estimates.
- Claims 3 and 4 matter because they turn "no fee found" from a research gap into an actual finding: the same contract that is precise about the 12% Premium Support surcharge is conspicuously silent on any upgrade fee and explicitly generous about dedicated CSM/SA inclusion - that pattern (specific where it charges, silent/generous where it doesn't) is itself informative about Glean's commercial structure.
- Claim 6's explicit warning against conflating the 12% Premium Support fee with the separately-estimated 7-12% renewal escalation is included deliberately - these two numbers landing in the same rough range, on two different cost lines, from two different source tiers (confirmed contract vs. third-party estimate), is exactly the kind of thing that gets garbled in a client-facing summary if not flagged plainly here.
- Claim 8's absence-check is worth stating explicitly rather than leaving implicit: a client evaluating TCO should know that "always-on vendor-managed services" is not a forced cost add-on for the standard SaaS path - only Premium Support (claim 1) is an optional, quantified upcharge.
