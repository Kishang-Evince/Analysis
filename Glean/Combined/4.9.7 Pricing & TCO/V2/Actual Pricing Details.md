## Headline

Glean's own channels (direct sales, Carahsoft, cloud marketplaces) confirm zero public list pricing — even the AWS Marketplace listing displays an obvious placeholder figure ($100,000,000.00) rather than a real price, directing buyers to "Request private offer." The most credible non-Glean data point found this pass comes from Vendr.com, a SaaS-pricing benchmarking firm that aggregates real, anonymized buyer transaction data rather than marketing copy: a median annual spend of $98,890 with buyers achieving roughly 20% average savings through negotiation, and typical minimum commitments of 100-250 users. Referral partner economics are now confirmed more precisely: 5% of first-year ARR standard, up to 10% with tier approval, paid ~45 days after the customer pays, and subject to clawback.

## Independent read

- Claim 2's placeholder AWS price is a small but telling detail — it visually confirms, rather than just asserts, that self-service marketplace pricing doesn't exist for this product, and is worth keeping in a summary as concrete evidence rather than just repeating "no public pricing."
- Claims 4-5 are this field's most useful addition: distinguishing Vendr's real-transaction-data methodology from the marketing-blog estimate sites cited elsewhere in this section is an important credibility distinction that should carry through into how confidently any of these numbers get presented to the client.
- Claim 7 is offered specifically to prevent an easy mistake: because several pricing fields in this section cite superficially similar dollar figures, it would be easy to read that as five independent confirmations of the same number, when it's more accurately a handful of the same underlying sources being repeatedly surfaced by search.
