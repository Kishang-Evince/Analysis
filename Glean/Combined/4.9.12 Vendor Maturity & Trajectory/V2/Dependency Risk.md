## Headline

Glean's multi-provider Model Hub genuinely reduces single-vendor LLM dependency risk at the architecture level - this project's own prior research already confirmed 40+ models spanning OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Amazon, and smaller providers, with per-step/per-agent model selection. But "architecturally diversified" is not the same as "operationally insulated from provider changes," and this project's own prior research also already documented a real, dated case proving the opposite: Anthropic's April 30, 2026 retirement of the 1M-token context beta broke any Glean workflow depending on it, with no proactive tooling to identify which agents were at risk. This pass adds two further dependency angles not previously covered: key-type-gated provider access (a real, structural single-point dependency, not a theoretical one) and Glean's own company/funding durability as the ultimate dependency a buyer is making.

## Independent read

- The headline finding for a healthcare buyer is genuinely two-layered: Glean's *architecture* is well-hedged against single-provider dependency (claim 1), but its *deployment configuration choices* (which key type, which cloud) can silently re-introduce single-provider dependency (claims 4-5), and its *operational tooling* provides no early warning when a dependency actually breaks something (claim 3, proven real by claim 2's dated precedent).
- Claim 6 is the most concrete, checkable risk item for a pre-pilot technical review: before committing to specific Glean features (Deep Research, Agent Sandbox, real-time voice), a buyer should ask Glean directly what happens operationally if that feature's pinned minimum model is deprecated - this project found no public answer to that specific operational question.
- Claim 7's company-durability angle is included because "dependency risk" as framed in the SOW extends beyond just model/cloud providers to the vendor relationship itself - the evidence here is reassuring but should be presented with the appropriate epistemic humility of any forward-looking financial read on a private company.
