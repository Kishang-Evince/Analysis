## Headline

Glean documents a real, named friction diagnostic framework — but confirms no benchmark thresholds for what counts as healthy vs. failing. Its own docs admit that default SSO-tile/direct-URL access "lead to poor adoption," and require the browser extension as mitigation. Two published flagship case studies (Nextdoor 82% stickiness, Zillow 80% adoption) look strong — but Glean's own 2026 platform-wide average (40-45% wDAU/wMAU, a related but not identical ratio) is far below those outliers, meaning the case studies are not representative of a typical deployment. Independent third-party sources add friction signals Glean's own docs don't surface: G2 reviewer complaints about response-time inconsistency, and a third-party monitoring-tool market that exists specifically because customers can't easily see Glean adoption/friction themselves.

## Independent read

- Claim 1 is the field's cleanest, most load-bearing finding: Glean's own documentation, unprompted, admits its default deployment method causes poor adoption. This is a rare case of a vendor being direct about a real limitation rather than something inferred or found via a sourcing trap.
- Claims 8-9 together are this pass's most important addition beyond what a single-page docs read would surface: the gap between marquee case-study numbers and the company-wide average is a real, citable finding, and the metric-definition distinction (wDAU/wMAU vs. WAU/MAU) is a genuine accuracy trap worth flagging explicitly rather than treating the two ratios as interchangeable.
- Claim 6 (feedback doesn't auto-retrain) is a small but consequential detail: it means the "downvote rate" friction signal, once identified, still requires manual admin follow-up to actually close the loop — it is not a signal the system resolves on its own.
- Claim 7's legacy-support-migration detail (prior logins not carried over) is a new, concrete friction point for any organization that was already a Glean customer under an older support system — worth flagging specifically for change-management planning.
- Claims 10-11 exist to counterbalance an over-reliance on Glean's own documentation for a field that is fundamentally about *resistance* — independent, non-vendor sources are the more credible place to look for friction Glean's own materials wouldn't be inclined to volunteer.
