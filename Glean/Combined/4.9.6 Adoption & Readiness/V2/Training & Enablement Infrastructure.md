## Headline

Every component the SOW asks about exists in some form, but none of them match a "comprehensive enablement program" bar. "Glean Academy" is real but is a guest-lecture business/leadership speaker series, not hands-on technical training — and carries no certificate. No certification program of any kind was found anywhere. Developer documentation is static prose with copy-paste code snippets, not an interactive quickstart. A modest self-service video component does exist (Community Resources' "videos"), and recurring monthly instructor-led sessions exist (support-team troubleshooting, product-team updates) — but neither cost/inclusion status nor a train-the-trainer program could be confirmed. No in-app guidance or product-tour layer was found documented anywhere. A fifth instance of this project's recurring "similarly-named unrelated company" trap also surfaced this pass: **Glean UDS** (training.gleanuds.com), an unrelated organization's training portal.

## Independent read

- The most important structural finding here is the mismatch between what "Glean Academy" sounds like it should be (a hands-on technical training academy) and what it actually is: a short guest-lecture series aimed at business/technology leaders discussing AI adoption strategy, not a developer skills program. Both seasons follow the identical pattern — outside executives, organizational/strategic topics, no certificate.
- Claim 5 is a real, direct answer to the "interactive tutorials" question for the one representative page checked — it is not interactive. This should not be over-generalized to every page on `developers.glean.com` without further spot-checks, but the one checked is unambiguous.
- Claims 6-8 are this field's honesty-critical rows: the community forum's actual health (activity level, response speed) is the single most evaluation-relevant fact for this field, and automated fetch could not deliver it. Rather than inferring an answer from the small member count in claim 7 alone, this should be explicitly handed to the test guide for a live-browser, logged-in check.
- Claims 10-13 are this pass's direct answers to the SOW's four added sub-questions, and the honest pattern across all four is "exists narrowly, or not found, with real gaps on cost/comprehensiveness." A video library and monthly instructor-led sessions exist (claims 10-11), but neither their depth nor their cost is documented; train-the-trainer and in-app guidance (claims 12-13) were not found at all. None of these four should be presented to a client as either "strong" or definitively "absent" beyond what's stated — cost/inclusion status especially should be confirmed directly with the account team, tracked in the companion test guide.
- Claims 9 and 14 keep this project's now-well-established "similarly-named company" pattern consistent — worth a one-line mention in any summary, not a deep dive, since they are sourcing-hygiene notes rather than Glean product findings.

**Doc-Verified** for claims 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 11 (direct fetches). **Search-corroborated only, direct fetch failed** for claims 7, 8 — explicitly flagged, not treated as equally reliable. **Search-corroborated** for claims 9, 14 (different companies' own marketing/about pages, straightforward to verify).
