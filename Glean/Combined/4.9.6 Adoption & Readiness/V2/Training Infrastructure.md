## Independent read

- The most important structural finding here is the mismatch between what "Glean Academy" sounds like it should be (a hands-on technical training academy, given the eval methodology's framing) and what it actually is: a short guest-lecture series aimed at business/technology leaders discussing AI adoption strategy, not a developer skills program. Both seasons follow the identical pattern — outside executives, organizational/strategic topics, no certificate.
- Claim 5 is a real, direct answer to the "interactive quickstarts" question for the one representative page checked — it is not interactive. This should not be over-generalized to every page on `developers.glean.com` without further spot-checks, but the one checked is unambiguous.
- Claims 6-8 are this field's honesty-critical rows: the community forum's actual health (activity level, response speed) is the single most evaluation-relevant fact for this field, and automated fetch could not deliver it. Rather than inferring an answer from the small member count in claim 7 alone, this should be explicitly handed to the test guide for a live-browser, logged-in check.
- Claim 9 keeps this project's now-well-established "similarly-named company" pattern consistent — worth a one-line mention in any summary, not a deep dive, since it's a sourcing-hygiene note rather than a Glean product finding.

## Confidence

**Doc-Verified** for claims 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 (direct fetches). **Search-corroborated only, direct fetch failed** for claims 7, 8 — explicitly flagged, not treated as equally reliable. **Search-corroborated** for claim 9 (a different company's own marketing/about pages, straightforward to verify).
