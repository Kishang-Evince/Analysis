## Independent read

- The evidentiary strength genuinely varies: named-customer, named-individual, quantified full case studies (Confluent, McCarthy Holdings, Covered California, SafetyCulture) are meaningfully stronger signal than logo-only placements (Rivian, Samsung, Vanta, etc.) with zero published detail - the SOW's "active/satisfied vs. legacy/minimal usage" question is best answered by this published/logo-only split as an inference, since Glean does not publicly rate its own logos by deployment depth.
- For this healthcare client specifically, Covered California is the strongest available reference despite not being a direct-care provider - it is regulated, government, health-insurance-adjacent, and fully named with on-record executives. The actual direct-care healthcare case study (WWT/Glean, 3,000+ outpatient centers) is real but anonymized, which is a meaningfully weaker evidentiary standing than a named reference.
- The "willing to speak with prospects" sub-question is honestly unanswerable from public web research and is flagged as an Absence-check in the companion test guide - it requires a direct AE ask, ideally for a named healthcare or health-insurance-adjacent reference given the thin public bench in that specific sector.
