## Headline

Glean's own pricing documentation directly answers half this field's question: the 275+ native connector catalog, standard Platform/Admin/Search APIs, and MCP host are all confirmed *included* in the per-seat Core Suite license, not billed per-connector or per-call - with an explicit "human-scale usage is free, machine-scale/abusive volume is billed" carve-out (already documented in this project's `License Structure Details.md` claim 7, re-confirmed here in its integration-specific context). What is NOT answered anywhere in Glean's own docs is a per-connector or per-API-call dollar figure - none is published, and the marketing connector-catalog page (`glean.com/platform/connectors`) discloses zero commercial terms. The one genuine, quantified cost signal this pass found sits outside Glean's own docs: third-party/reseller sources describe custom (non-native, Push-API-built) connector development as a professional-services line item running roughly **$15,000-$75,000 per integration** - unconfirmed by Glean directly and should be treated as an estimate, not a quote.

## Independent read

- This field turns out to be mostly *already answered* by this project's existing Pricing & TCO research (`License Structure Details.md` claims 5 and 7), just never framed through the integration-specific lens the SOW asks for. Claims 2 and 3 here are best read as "same underlying fact, re-applied to this field's exact question" rather than new discoveries - flagged explicitly rather than silently re-deriving them as if new.
- Claims 4 and 5 are the genuinely new, load-bearing findings for this field: two independent direct checks of Glean's own documentation (the custom-connector page and the connector-catalog marketing page) both confirm zero published per-connector or per-API-call dollar figures exist. This is a legitimate Absence-check finding, not a research shortfall - Glean's commercial model is confirmed quote-based for anything beyond the bundled native-connector/standard-API layer.
- Claim 6's dollar range is worth surfacing to the client because it is the only concrete number found anywhere for the "what does a non-native integration actually cost" question, but it must travel with its caveat: it comes from deal-intelligence/consulting sources, not Glean, and should be sanity-checked against this tenant's own quote before being used in any TCO model.
