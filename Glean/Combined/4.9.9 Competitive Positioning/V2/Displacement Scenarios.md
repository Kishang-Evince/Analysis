## Headline

The most important finding this pass is a **second, independent instance** of the audit-trail overstatement already flagged in this project's Best-Fit Client Profile V2 field: Glean's ChatGPT-Enterprise-compliance comparison article claims audit logging *"covers every Glean Search query, Glean Assistant interaction, and Glean Agents action across all connected systems"* - the same category of claim, on a different page, using even stronger language ("every"). Finding this exact overstatement pattern twice, independently, on two separate comparison pages materially raises confidence that this is a consistent marketing pattern, not an isolated blog imprecision. Separately, both of Glean's major comparison articles explicitly frame Glean as complementary to, not a replacement for, Copilot/ChatGPT Enterprise/Workspace AI - confirming the base research's "does not displace" table with precise, quotable, deliberate positioning language from Glean itself.

## Independent read

- Claim 2 is the standout finding of this pass, and arguably of this entire competitive-positioning section: finding the exact same audit-trail overstatement independently on two separate Glean comparison pages (this one, and the life-sciences blog in the Best-Fit Client Profile field) turns what could have been dismissed as one imprecise sentence into a documented, repeated pattern across Glean's own marketing. This deserves to be flagged prominently and consistently wherever audit/compliance claims are discussed across this evaluation.
- Claim 3's third independent instance of the real-time-sync overstatement (after the platform page and the life-sciences blog) further reinforces that this, too, is a consistent marketing pattern rather than an isolated statement - worth treating as a standing caveat whenever any Glean permission or sync claim is quoted.
- Claim 4 is worth highlighting as a genuine positive counterpoint in the same source that also contains claims 2-3 - this project's honesty convention cuts both ways, and a real, defensible differentiator (ZDR) shouldn't be discounted just because it appears alongside overstated claims elsewhere on the same page.
- Claim 6 is the field's practical takeaway: Glean's own restraint about not claiming to replace Copilot or ChatGPT Enterprise is useful cover for Stratos to adopt the same honest framing, rather than overselling displacement potential beyond what the vendor itself claims.
