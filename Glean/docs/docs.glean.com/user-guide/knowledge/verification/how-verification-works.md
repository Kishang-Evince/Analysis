On this page

When you're using Glean to search for documents across different tools in your company, Glean's Verification feature makes it easy to know if a document is **verified**: personally approved by one of your users that it's accurate and up to date.

You can also track which of your documents your users want you to verify and set proactive reminders to help you keep your documents up to date.

## See verified search results[​](#see-verified-search-results "Direct link to See verified search results")

A **verified search result** appears with a **green badge** after a result title. Hover over the badge to see who verified the document and when. This way, before you even click on a document, you can check if it's up to date or not.

## Track what documents need your verification[​](#track-what-documents-need-your-verification "Direct link to Track what documents need your verification")

### Verification tasks[​](#verification-tasks "Direct link to Verification tasks")

Open the [Verification tasks page](http://app.glean.com/verification) to see which documents your users have requested you to verify and your own reminders to reverify documents.

### Your content[​](#your-content "Direct link to Your content")

You can also open [Your content](https://app.glean.com/verification/content) to see the content you own across apps. Use the filters at the top of the page to find content that is unverified, verified, or deprecated. Verify and deprecate documents from there to help your users work from current information.

## Verifying a document[​](#verifying-a-document "Direct link to Verifying a document")

### Verifying from the web app[​](#verifying-from-the-web-app "Direct link to Verifying from the web app")

Verify a document via your [Tasks](http://app.glean.com/verification) list. After verifying, set a reminder to reverify the document to ensure that your content stays fresh. When the reminder is up, you'll see this document pop back into your Tasks list.

## Ask for verification on your users' documents[​](#ask-for-verification-on-your-users-documents "Direct link to Ask for verification on your users' documents")

### Verifying from the web app[​](#verifying-from-the-web-app-1 "Direct link to Verifying from the web app")

Hover over a search result and under the "more" menu (three dots), click "Request verification". You'll be able to select a verification reason as well to provide more details.

### Verifying from the Chrome extension[​](#verifying-from-the-chrome-extension "Direct link to Verifying from the Chrome extension")

When you visit a teammate's document (if you have the Chrome extension enabled), you can see whether the doc is verified, unverified, or deprecated in the Glean sticky tabs on the bottom right.

Click on the tab and you can see more details about the verification status and request verification from the teammate.
