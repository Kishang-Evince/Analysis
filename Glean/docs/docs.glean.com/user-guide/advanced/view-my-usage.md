On this page

The **Usage** page in your profile settings shows your personal Glean usage for the current calendar month, including the models you use, agents you run, and product surfaces that contribute to your total.

note

This page is available for all users on Enterprise Flex and Glean Core Suite plans.

## Open your usage page[​](#open-your-usage-page "Direct link to Open your usage page")

Navigate to **Profile** → **Your settings** → **Usage**.

## Usage summary[​](#usage-summary "Direct link to Usage summary")

The top of the page shows how much you've used during the current calendar month. If your admin enables an applicable hard cap for you, this section also shows your current progress toward that limit.

## Usage details[​](#usage-details "Direct link to Usage details")

The personal **Usage** page is limited to your own usage. The usage details show the models you use, agents you run, and product surfaces that contribute to your total usage. Use this information to learn more about your personal usage and understand what drives your cost.
