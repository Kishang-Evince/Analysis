On this page

Clear your browser cache in Glean to troubleshoot a bug or performance issue. The Glean backend domain is cached in your browser's local storage, so clearing it resolves many display and session problems.

note

This guide applies to **Google Chrome**. Other Chromium-based browsers (such as Microsoft Edge) follow a similar flow, but menu labels may differ.

## Prerequisites[​](#prerequisites "Direct link to Prerequisites")

Do the following before you begin:

-   You need to be on a Glean page (for example, your Glean web app) so the site settings apply to the Glean domain.
-   Clearing the cache signs you out, so you will **Sign in to Glean again** afterward. Clear the cache when you are troubleshooting a bug, stale content, or a performance issue.

## Steps to clear the browser cache[​](#steps-to-clear-the-browser-cache "Direct link to Steps to clear the browser cache")

1.  Click the lock icon in the left of the URL bar and choose **Site settings**.

2.  In the window that opens, click the **Clear data** button.

3.  Sign in to Glean again.
