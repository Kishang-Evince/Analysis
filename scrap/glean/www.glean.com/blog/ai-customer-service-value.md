Customer service teams today are under pressure to deliver faster, more accurate support while navigating fragmented systems, high employee turnover, and long onboarding cycles. Turnover rates for call center agents are hovering at a concerning [30-45%](https://www.nextiva.com/blog/call-center-turnover-rates.html#:~:text=See%20Nextiva%20in%20action.&text=The%20average%20call%20center%20turnover,and%20impacting%20customer%20service%20quality.), while onboarding takes months, resulting in a third of onboarding investments going to waste. With budgets tight, and the perception of customer service as a cost center, teams need to maximize efficiency and productivity while maintaining customer satisfaction. 

AI customer service tools are helping support teams resolve issues with full context, recommend the right next step, and improve both the agent and customer experience. In this blog, we’ll look at real examples of how customer service teams are using AI to reduce resolution times, shorten onboarding, and strengthen support outcomes. Learn how the right AI platform can help support staff move from faster time-to-resolution (TTR) to better support escalation handling, grounded handoffs, and more consistent service.

## Better prioritize tasks and time by reducing escalations 

Empowering frontline support staff with the full support context they need increases the percentage of tickets resolved at first contact and helps minimize unnecessary escalations.

When agents can quickly surface accurate answers and recommended next steps, organizations see measurable improvements in First Contact Resolution (FCR) and reductions in Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR), improving the customer experience while lowering operational costs. This approach keeps cases moving and lets senior staff focus on the most complex issues and strategic initiatives instead of routine support escalation.

Organizations across industries are using Glean to achieve measurable gains in their support operations. Hospitality teams have increased first call resolution by 17%, telecom companies report 24–47% efficiency improvements in information search for agents, and technology sector support teams have seen a 13% rise in positive employee satisfaction. Glean equips frontline staff with instant answers, enabling faster, more effective service and higher satisfaction for both employees and customers. 

## Quicker onboarding and support for new hires improves retention

Providing instant access to the right knowledge, prior cases, and process guidance accelerates onboarding for new support staff, helping them ramp faster and perform more like experienced team members.

By reducing the time required for new hires to become effective, AI can minimize the burden on senior staff for training and support, easing workplace stress and helping new employees feel more confident in their roles. This streamlined approach to onboarding not only lowers associated costs but also fosters a more positive experience for new team members, helping improve retention and strengthening overall support operations.

Glean provides this instant knowledge discovery and access, enabling companies like a leading fintech enterprise to reduce new agent ramp-up time from four months to one, cutting onboarding time by 75%. Other companies also saw up to an 11% increase in support staff retention, thanks to new hires becoming productive faster, succeeding in a more informed and streamlined workplace.

## Improving customer satisfaction through quick, omnichannel support 

Effectively reducing churn and building stronger customer loyalty starts with meeting the high expectations of today’s customers. They expect fast, accurate, always-available support across channels-a challenge when critical context is scattered across systems and teams. By streamlining this flow of information, your team can resolve issues faster and ensure a superior support experience.

Leading organizations across industries are turning to Glean for customer service to seamlessly connect information from tools like Zendesk, Service Cloud, and ServiceNow CSM. This empowers support agents with actionable recommendations-such as next steps for open tickets, draft customer communications, and suggested experts for quick escalation. This unified approach not only helps agents resolve issues faster, but also delivers measurable impact: teams using Glean have seen up to a 20% reduction in ticket resolution times and save over an hour per day per agent. 

## Delivering better service through improved customer and employee experiences

Customer service teams require an AI solution that can tackle the complexities of today's omnichannel support strategy effectively. The best platforms bring together enterprise context, secure governance, and action-oriented assistance so support teams can deliver better resolutions, reduce burnout, and improve customer satisfaction.
