## How to write effective AI prompts for operations

A strong operations prompt gives the AI a role, real data, and a clear output format. Generic prompts fail because they lack company context, so the model guesses instead of answering from your numbers. These four practices sharpen every prompt above.

-   **Give the AI a role and business context.** Start with who the AI is acting as and what it is working on, such as "You are an operations manager reviewing last month's warehouse throughput."
-   **Specify the output format.** Ask for a ranked list, a table, a standard operating procedure, or a one-paragraph summary, so the response fits how you will use it.
-   **Feed it real operational data.** Point the prompt at actual figures from your ERP, tickets, or schedules rather than hypotheticals, and the analysis reflects your business.
-   **Iterate and ask follow-ups.** Treat the first answer as a draft. Refine it with questions like "now break this down by region" or "flag the three highest-cost items."

The last point is where most value hides. When your prompts run against real company data, follow-ups compound. Glean grounds prompts in the Enterprise Graph, which maps relationships across documents, messages, tools, and people, so each refinement pulls from the same connected knowledge rather than starting cold.
