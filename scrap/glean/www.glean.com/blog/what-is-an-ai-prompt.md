An AI prompt is the instruction, question, or input you give an AI system to get a specific response. The clearer and more specific the prompt, the more useful the output.

A prompt bridges what you want and what the AI produces. Prompt engineering, the practice of refining prompts, turns a vague request into a precise one that returns relevant results.

Prompt quality drives output quality. Ask a general-purpose model a business question and it guesses at the details. Ask [Glean Assistant](https://www.glean.com/ai-assistant), grounded in your company's knowledge, and you get cited, permission-aware answers instead of generic text.

## Understanding AI prompts

AI prompts guide AI systems to generate the response you want. They carry context, intent, and specificity, which shape the clarity and precision of the AI's output.

### Defining prompts and AI prompts

Prompts are instructions or inputs given to an AI system to elicit a specific response. An AI prompt contains details that provide context and guide the AI to perform the desired task accurately.

These instructions range from simple commands to complex queries with multiple constraints.

In an AI prompt, specificity and clarity are crucial. They determine how well the AI understands and executes the prompt. The more precise the instructions, the better the results.

### Roles and importance in AI systems

AI prompts shape what an AI system does. They act as a bridge between the user's intent and the AI's output.

Prompts help the AI understand the context and provide relevant, accurate responses. Prompt quality tracks closely with output accuracy: a specific, well-scoped prompt narrows what the model has to guess, so the answer lands closer to what you asked for.

Effective AI prompts improve the usability, functionality, and reliability of AI systems.

### Prompt engineering basics

Prompt engineering is the practice of designing precise and effective prompts. It means understanding the requirements and constraints of the task and adapting written instructions to get the best result.

Key factors for successful prompt engineering include:

-   Defining clear intent
-   Being specific and clear
-   Including relevant context

These factors help the AI understand and perform the desired actions. In practice, prompt engineering takes iterative testing and refinement to get the inputs right. Where the model matters most is context: Glean Assistant grounds each response in your company's knowledge, so a business prompt returns cited, permission-aware answers rather than plausible-sounding but generic text.

## How to write good AI prompts

Effective AI prompts require specificity, clarity, context, and tone. They also use relevant keywords and subjects so the AI returns accurate responses.

### Crafting with specificity and clarity

Specificity in AI prompts directs the AI to provide the exact information required. Vague instructions often lead to irrelevant or unfocused answers. For example, if you ask for market trends in the tech industry, adding details like the timeframe, specific technology type, and geographical region can improve the accuracy of the response.

Clarity is equally important. Clear, concise language prevents misinterpretation. Simple sentences and precise wording help the AI understand the request. This approach keeps the responses relevant and useful.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

-   **Weak prompt:** "Write about our product."
-   **Strong prompt:** "Write a 150-word product description for our project-management app, aimed at IT directors, that highlights security and admin controls, in a professional tone."

The strong prompt names the length, audience, focus, and tone, so the AI has less to guess.

### Incorporating context and tone

Adding context to prompts helps the AI generate more personalized and relevant responses. If the AI understands the background or the situation, it can tailor its output more effectively. For example, when drafting a business email, stating the recipient's role and the purpose of the email guides the AI toward an appropriate tone and style.

Tone aligns the AI's output with your expectations. Whether you want a formal, conversational, or technical style, specifying it in the prompt produces responses that fit. Clear indicators about the tone help the AI stay consistent in its output.

### Using keywords and subjects

Keywords guide the AI toward the core aspects of the prompt. Including industry-specific terms or relevant subjects narrows the context and makes the responses more accurate. For instance, a prompt about "blockchain technology in healthcare" should specify keywords like "data security," "patient records," and "decentralized systems."

Subjects specify the main focus areas of the query. By identifying key themes and adding them to the prompt, you help the AI cover the necessary topics in full. This use of keywords and subjects leads to more detailed and pertinent answers.

## AI prompt examples

The fastest way to write better AI prompts is to compare a weak version with a strong one. Below are three common workplace tasks, each with a vague prompt and a specific rewrite that gives the AI enough to work with.

### Drafting a customer email

-   **Weak prompt:** "Write an email to a customer about the outage."
-   **Strong prompt:** "Draft a 120-word apology email to an enterprise customer about yesterday's 45-minute login outage. Acknowledge the impact, explain the fix, and offer a follow-up call. Keep the tone calm and professional."

### Summarizing a support ticket

-   **Weak prompt:** "Summarize this ticket."
-   **Strong prompt:** "Summarize this support ticket in three bullet points: the reported issue, the steps already tried, and the recommended next action for a Tier 2 engineer."

### Researching a sales account

-   **Weak prompt:** "Tell me about this account."
-   **Strong prompt:** "Summarize our relationship with Acme Corp: open opportunities, recent support issues, the main contacts, and any renewal risks. Pull from our CRM and past email threads."

## Optimization and best practices

To write effective AI prompts, it helps to know how to avoid common mistakes, follow best practices, and keep improving over time.

### Avoiding common mistakes

One frequent error is using overly complex or vague language. This can confuse the model and degrade output quality. Simplicity and clarity are key to effective prompts.

Another pitfall is failing to define the scope and constraints. Be specific about what you need the AI to accomplish, and outline any limits so the responses stay accurate and precise.

Biases can also creep into prompts. Recognizing and reducing them helps keep outputs fair and balanced. Double-checking prompts for unintended biases improves the overall result.

### Adhering to best practices

Best practices start with prompts that are clear, concise, and unambiguous. Adding contextual details helps the AI perform more accurately.

Using lists or bullet points for complex queries breaks tasks into manageable parts. This keeps the work precise and lowers the risk of errors.

Provide examples when you can. This helps the AI understand the desired format and style, which makes the generated content more usable.

### Continual learning and improvement

Reviewing and iterating on your prompts leads to real improvements. Analyzing the AI's responses shows where adjustments are needed.

Feedback loops let you refine prompts over time and get more from each one.

Grounding matters as much as wording. When an AI answers from open-ended training data, it can hallucinate. Glean returns cited, permission-aware answers grounded in your company's knowledge, so you can check each response against its source and trust what you act on.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between an AI prompt and prompt engineering?

An AI prompt is the input you give an AI system. Prompt engineering is the practice of designing and refining those prompts so the AI returns accurate, relevant responses.

### What are the main types of AI prompts?

Common types include text generation, code generation, classification, and creative or multi-step prompts. Each type shapes the input to match the task and the model's capabilities.

### What makes a high-quality AI prompt?

A high-quality AI prompt is clear, specific, and provides enough context. It avoids ambiguity and is tailored to the outcome you want, which leads to more accurate and useful responses.

### What are common mistakes when writing AI prompts?

Common mistakes include ambiguity, missing context, and unintended bias. Prompts that are too complex or too simple can also produce irrelevant results, so balancing detail and simplicity matters.

### How do AI prompts work in an enterprise setting?

In the enterprise, prompts work best when they're grounded in company data. Glean Assistant returns cited, permission-aware answers from your own knowledge, so a prompt about a customer, project, or policy pulls from real sources rather than generic text.
