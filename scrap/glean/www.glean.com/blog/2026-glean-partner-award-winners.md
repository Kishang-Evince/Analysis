Today at Partner Day during Glean:GO 2026, we’re celebrating the partners who are helping organizations move from AI ambition to measurable outcomes.

The first annual Glean Partner Awards recognize standout work across transformation, collaboration, delivery, customer impact, innovation, and regional growth. These partners have helped customers adopt Glean, expand what’s possible with work AI, and turn strong ideas into results.

We’re proud to recognize this year’s winners.

## The 2026 Glean Partner Award winners

### AI Transformation Partner of the Year: Bain

### Customer Impact & Advocacy Partner of the Year: Deloitte

### Collaboration Partner of the Year: AHEAD

### Delivery Excellence Partner of the Year: Alchemy Tech Group

### Emerging Partner of the Year: EggNest

### Architecture Partner of the Year: Caylent

### Public Sector Partner of the Year: Carahsoft

### Regional Partners of the Year

-   **APAC:** KK Ashisuto
-   **LATAM:** Inco
-   **EMEA:** Softcat

## Celebrating the work behind the awards

Partner success is built through the work that happens every day: aligning on customer needs, bringing the right expertise to the table, creating repeatable solutions, and staying close from the first conversation through deployment and adoption.

That work is becoming even more important as organizations look for practical ways to put AI to work across the enterprise. Our partners bring the industry expertise, technical depth, services capabilities, and local relationships that help customers make progress with confidence.

The Glean Partner Awards are one way we recognize that impact. They also reflect the kind of ecosystem we’re building through the Glean Partner Network, our official global partner program. The network brings together the pathways, expertise, and resources partners need to help customers move from AI strategy to real-world implementation and adoption.

Congratulations to Bain, Deloitte, AHEAD, Alchemy Tech Group, EggNest, Caylent, Carahsoft, KK Ashisuto, Inco, and Softcat. Thank you for the work you do with Glean and for the outcomes you help create for our shared customers.

We’re excited to keep building what’s next together.
