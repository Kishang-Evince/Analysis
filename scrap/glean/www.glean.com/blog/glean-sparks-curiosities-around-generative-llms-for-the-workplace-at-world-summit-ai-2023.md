Glean recently attended [World Summit AI Americas 2023](https://americas.worldsummit.ai/) in Montreal – the world’s leading AI convention in the Americas. We were able to share with the curious crowd a deeper look into how AI and LLMs factor into knowledge management, and how Glean can help deliver on the promise of AI-powered search and discovery through trusted knowledge models.

-   Why it’s essential to anchor generative LLMs in domain-adapted semantic search
