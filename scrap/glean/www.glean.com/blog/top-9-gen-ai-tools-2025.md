Generative AI tools have rapidly become essential in the enterprise landscape. They transform the way businesses operate by automating workflows, offering data-driven insights, and optimizing productivity across various departments, from customer support to development and finance. As we look to 2025, here are nine top generative AI tools designed to elevate enterprise efficiency and provide a competitive edge.

## 9 best enterprise generative AI tools

[1\. Glean - Enterprise AI for knowledge management](#glean)

[2\. GitHub Copilot - AI for developer productivity](#github-copilot)

[3\. Superhuman - AI-enhanced email management](#superhuman)

[4\. Stampli - AI for accounts payable automation](#stampli)

[5\. Gong - AI for sales and revenue insights](#gong)

[6\. ChurnZero - AI for customer success management](#churnzero)

[7\. Helpshift - AI-driven customer support](#helpshift)

[8\. Runway AI - AI tools for creative teams](#runaway-ai)

[9\. Paradox AI - HR automation for recruitment](#paradox-ai)

<h3 id="glean">1. Glean - Enterprise AI for knowledge management</h3>

-   **Description:** Glean acts as a digital workplace assistant, enhancing productivity by providing precise answers from a company’s internal data while ensuring robust security.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **Tailored enterprise knowledge:** Customized answers based on internal data and user role.
    -   **Connectivity across 100+ apps:** Integration with multiple enterprise applications.
    -   **Generative AI summaries:** Provides concise document summaries to save time.
    -   **Real-time indexing:** Ensures users have access to up-to-date, permission-based search results.

<h3 id="github-copilot">2. GitHub Copilot - AI for developer productivity</h3>

-   **Description:** GitHub Copilot uses OpenAI’s Codex to streamline coding by offering intelligent code suggestions and completion.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **Smart code suggestions:** Offers real-time snippets and boilerplate code.
    -   **Integration with popular editors:** Compatible with Visual Studio Code and more.
    -   **AI-driven code generation:** Helps developers tackle complex coding challenges.

<h3 id="superhuman">3. Superhuman - AI-enhanced email management</h3>

-   **Description:** Superhuman is an AI-powered email client that organizes inboxes and prioritizes important messages.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **Smart email categorization:** Uses AI to highlight priority emails.
    -   **Keyboard shortcuts:** Efficient navigation through an inbox.
    -   **Insights & analytics:** Offers data on open rates and response times.

<h3 id="stampli">4. Stampli - AI for accounts payable automation</h3>

-   **Description:** Stampli leverages AI to automate invoice processing, reducing manual work in finance.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **Automated invoice processing:** Reduces time spent on data entry.
    -   **Customizable approval workflows:** Aligns with company policies for smooth approvals.
    -   **ERP integration:** Works with major ERP systems for seamless data flow.

<h3 id="gong">5. Gong - AI for sales and revenue insights</h3>

-   **Description:** Gong analyzes sales interactions, providing insights to improve performance and increase revenue.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **Automated call analysis:** AI breakdown of sales calls to reveal interaction patterns.
    -   **Sales coaching tools:** Suggests targeted coaching to enhance skills.
    -   **Revenue insights:** Identifies growth opportunities through data analysis.

<h3 id="churnzero">6. ChurnZero - AI for customer success management</h3>

-   **Description:** ChurnZero aids in reducing churn by offering AI-driven insights on customer engagement.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **Customer health scores:** Identifies at-risk customers for proactive intervention.
    -   **Automated workflows:** Ensures timely follow-ups with personalized communications.
    -   **CRM integration:** Connects seamlessly with popular CRM systems.

<h3 id="helpshift">7. Helpshift - AI-driven customer support</h3>

-   **Description:** Helpshift enhances customer support with AI, offering solutions that reduce response times and streamline ticket management.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **AI-powered chatbots:** Handles routine inquiries to free up agents.
    -   **Smart ticket routing:** Automatically assigns tickets to the correct team.
    -   **Performance analytics:** Provides insights into customer service metrics.

<h3 id="runaway-ai">8. Runway AI - AI tools for creative teams</h3>

-   **Description:** Runway AI supports creative teams with AI tools for video editing, graphic design, and animation.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **AI-powered video editing:** Simplifies complex video tasks, like adding effects.
    -   **Design automation:** Uses AI to create graphics and animations efficiently.
    -   **Collaboration tools:** Allows creative teams to work together seamlessly.

<h3 id="paradox-ai">9. Paradox AI - HR automation for recruitment</h3>

-   **Description:** Paradox AI leverages conversational AI to streamline HR functions, from recruitment to onboarding.
-   **Key features:**
    -   **AI-powered recruiting:** Manages applicant screening and interview scheduling.
    -   **Automated onboarding:** Guides new hires through orientation.
    -   **Workflow automation:** Reduces repetitive HR tasks to free up time for strategy.

## Conclusion

Generative AI is reshaping the enterprise landscape, helping businesses enhance workflows, automate repetitive tasks, and gain actionable insights. Integrating AI tools like [Glean](https://www.glean.com/get-a-demo), GitHub Copilot, and others can provide substantial boosts to productivity and maintain a competitive advantage in the ever-evolving digital space.
