Retail is moving faster than ever - but the real challenge is no longer just speed. Its keeping stores, ecommerce, customer service, and merchandising aligned as conditions change.

Between changing tariffs, shifting customer expectations, economic pressures, and increasingly complex tech stacks, retailers need a faster way to act on what's happening across the business. That’s where AI comes in. At its best, AI helps retailers work from shared context - so teams can prepare the next step, coordinate faster, and keep execution consistent across every channel.

In this blog, we’ll explore five key ways AI is helping modern retailers stay aligned across channels and act with more confidence.

## 1\. Deliver faster, more personalized customer service

Today’s customers expect fast, personalized, and consistent support across every touchpoint. But for retailers, meeting those expectations is challenging when order history, return policies, delivery status, and customer context is spread across different systems and teams.

With the necessary context, support agents can move faster and resolve issues more efficiently to meet these expectations.

AI can help support teams:

-   Work from full order, policy, and case context
-   Prepare clearer responses and next steps
-   Resolve issues with speed and consistency

With Glean, support reps don’t have to manually dig through documents, systems, or emails. They simply ask a question - like “What’s our return policy for international orders?” - and get clear, trusted answers in seconds to keep service moving during high-volume moments.

## 2\. Keep omnichannel execution in sync

Omnichannel retail only works when every team is operating from the same context. Promotions, pricing, PDP updates, inventory, and store guidance need to stay aligned - especially when conditions change quickly.

Without shared context, even simple changes create friction across channels. Online promotions launch before store teams are ready, product details differ across touchpoints, and support teams are left reacting to fulfillment issues after customers already feel the impact.

AI helps omnichannel teams:

-   Unify customer data from ecommerce, POS, CRM, loyalty programs, and support systems
-   Power real-time personalization across channels
-   Maintain consistent product and inventory info across platforms
-   Enable seamless fulfillment - from BOPIS to curbside pickup

With Glean, retailers can connect signals across channels, quickly analyze performance, and ensure that store & digital teams are working from the same operating context.

## 3\. Ramp new and seasonal employees faster

Retailers face constant turnover and seasonal hiring, which makes fast ramp to productivity critical. Because store policies and training resources are scattered across systems, traditional onboarding processes are slow, manual, and resource-heavy.

AI helps frontline teams get the guidance they need in the flow of work, so managers spend less time answering the same questions and more time coaching.

AI allows store managers to:

-   Enable new hires with on-demand guidance to company policies
-   Build customizable, role-specific learning paths
-   Drive consistent execution across locations & shifts  
    

With Glean, employees get access to the right guidance in the moment - whether they're learning a returns workflow, checking store policies, or preparing for a high-volume shift.

## 4\. Turn retail signals into synchronized decisions

In a fast-moving market, retailers can’t wait for the next reporting cycle to understand what changed. The opportunity isn't just faster analysis - it is faster action.

AI helps merchandising, planning, and marketing teams work across performance signals, assortment data, campaign context, and demand shifts in one place instead of rebuilding the same story by hand.

Retailers are using AI to:

-   Synthesize insights from sales data, customer feedback, and industry trends
-   Prepare merchant-ready briefs for business reviews
-   Make assortment and pricing decisions

With Glean, teams can move quickly from signal to follow-through without waiting for manual analyses or cross-functional back-and-forth.

## 5\. Surface operational risks sooner and keep work moving

Retail operations get harder when inventory, fulfillment, supplier updates, pricing decisions, and store execution all live in separate systems. Teams end up spending time reconciling the story instead of acting on it.

AI helps retailers surface what needs attention earlier, connect the right context across systems, and prepare the next steps before disruptions result in revenue or margin issues.

AI helps operations teams:

-   Spot inventory, fulfillment, and supplier issues sooner
-   Connect operational context across systems without manual stitching
-   Prepare the next steps before delays spread across channels

With Glean, teams can work across contracts, pricing sheets, warehouse updates, incident notes, and operating guidance in one place - so they spend less time reconciling systems and more time acting on what matters.

## **Move faster with every channel working from the same context**

Retailers don't need more disconnected AI tools. They need a way for stores, digital teams, merchandising, support, and operations to act on shared context across every channel.

That is where AI becomes most valuable: not just by saving time, but by helping teams prepare the next action, stay aligned as conditions shift, and deliver more consistent customer experiences.

Glean builds that shared context and makes it usable across the business - helping retailers move faster, coordinate better, and keep every channel in sync.
