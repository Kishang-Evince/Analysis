We’re proud to announce today that Glean has won the 2025 Google Cloud Technology Partner of the Year Award for Marketplace-Business Applications. With a growing partnership and increased customer adoption through Google Cloud Marketplace, working with Google Cloud has helped us deliver transformative work AI and agents to companies like Deutsche Telecom, Instacart, Reddit, and many more. 

Glean's work AI platform enables businesses to unlock their productivity potential and reshape core businesses for efficiency. With enterprise-ready workplace search, permissions-aware AI assistance, and agents requiring only natural language to build, Glean enables enterprises of any scale to kickstart the AI transformation process with real impact on both top and bottom-line results. 

## Delivering work AI for all

Our founder, Arvind Jain, one of the original search engineers at Google, wanted to make enterprise search as easy as consumer search with Google. That’s why he set out to create Glean and enterprise search that understands every company's unique language, respects permissions, and connects to every business application where data lives. 

This award highlights both the accomplishment of delivering the workplace search solution our founder set out to build, along with our latest advancements in AI agents and achievements in the Google Cloud ecosystem. 

"Google Cloud’s Partner Awards recognize partners who have created outsized value for customers through the delivery of innovative solutions and a high level of expertise,” said Kevin Ichhpurani, President, Global Partner Ecosystem, Google Cloud. "We're proud to announce Glean as a 2025 Google Cloud Partner Award winner and celebrate their impact enabling customer success over the past year."

## Our partnership and journey so far

2025 has been a milestone year for Glean, marked by significant achievements:

-   Expanded our customer base, welcoming many new enterprises to the Glean family - including customers like Deutsche Telecom, Reddit, Instacart, and Booking.com
-   Strengthened our integration with Google Cloud while extending the choice of LLMs on our platform - we’re excited to support Google’s Gemini models as one of our native models on Glean
-   Launched innovative features that further enhance our platform’s capabilities:  
    -   **Glean Agents** enable users to build, deploy, orchestrate, and govern AI agents across the enterprise using natural language
    -   **Universal knowledge** enables users to seamlessly integrate and leverage both real-time web and internal enterprise data in their queries
    -   Glean now makes **data analysis** on structured and unstructured data possible. Use natural language to run queries, explore data trends, and generate insights across structured files

“Following up on an incredible year of advancements and achievements for Glean, we’re honored to receive this award from our fantastic partners at Google Cloud i,” said MP Eisen, Vice President of Partnerships from Glean. “Our partnership with Google Cloud has been essential to Glean achieving $100 million in ARR this year and bringing AI to the enterprise."
