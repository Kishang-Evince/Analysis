# LinkedIn post draft

Turn internal wins, launches, and ideas into on-brand, ready to share LinkedIn posts.

## Overview

Employees often want to share launches, work wins, and learnings, but writing a strong LinkedIn post takes time. The LinkedIn post draft agent acts like an in-house social partner: it uses your message as the topic prompt, pulls in any URLs you include, and drafts a concise, engaging post that matches your company’s tone and LinkedIn formatting best practices. When it detects a blog link, it automatically adds a standardized, UTM-tagged CTA.

## See it in action

## Capabilities

-   Turns rough ideas and internal wins into polished LinkedIn posts that sound like your brand and your people.
-   Keeps posts accurate and on-message by grounding them in the links and source content you provide.
-   Helps employees share confidently by giving them copy that feels natural and audience-ready.
-   Standardizes CTAs and tracking links so marketing gets clean, consistent attribution on every post.

## Example output

Here’s a concise, high-engagement LinkedIn post draft about Globex’s latest product launch:

Big day at @Globex. We’re rolling out new updates that help revenue, product, and operations teams work with more clarity and speed.

These changes came from months of customer conversations, internal testing, and iteration. Teams are already seeing faster onboarding, clearer reporting, and fewer blockers between ideas and execution.

If you’re navigating shifting priorities or scaling quickly, this is worth a look.

Learn more: globex.com/blog/product-updates?utm\_source=linkedin&utm\_medium=organic-social&utm\_campaign=employee-share&utm\_content=blog

## Agent workflow

### Step 1: Trigger the workflow

The user submits a prompt with the topic for the post and, optionally, one or more URLs.

### Step 2: Read linked sources

The agent fetches and parses content from any URLs included in your message to supply context for the post.

### Step 3: Draft the post

It writes a concise, high-engagement LinkedIn post based on your message and the retrieved content, paraphrasing instead of copying.

### Step 4: Add tracking when needed

If a blog URL is detected, the agent rewrites it with standardized UTM parameters and inserts a clear CTA.

### Step 5: Generate share link

It produces a LinkedIn share URL with the full draft pre-filled and returns both the draft and a “Draft post to LinkedIn” link.
