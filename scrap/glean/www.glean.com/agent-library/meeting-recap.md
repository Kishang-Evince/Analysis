# Meeting recap

Get instant meeting summaries with key takeaways, action items, and stakeholder context.

## Overview

The single meeting summarizer helps you stay aligned without the hassle of manual notes. It pulls from meeting transcripts to summarize key points, decisions, and action items - then surfaces suggested owners and related content to keep work moving. Whether you missed the meeting or need a quick refresh, this agent delivers the context you need, fast.

## See it in action

Watch how the single meeting summarizer automatically pulls insights from your meetings and generates a clear, structured summary to speed up next steps.

## Capabilities

Quickly understand what was discussed and decided with a clear, structured summary.Move projects forward faster by turning conversation into action items.Boost cross-team clarity with surfaced experts and supporting context.

## Agent workflow

### Step 1: Trigger recap request

The workflow begins when you ask Glean for a summary of your most recent meeting with a specific person.

### Step 2: Find the meeting transcript

The agent checks your calendar to locate the right meeting, then fetches the corresponding transcript from Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams.

### Step 3: Analyze the conversation

It reads the transcript to identify the meeting’s objective, key discussion points, and outcomes.

### Step 4: Extract action items

The agent lists all follow-up tasks, assigns likely owners, and clearly defines what needs to happen next.

### Step 5: Identify supporting context

It surfaces relevant documents and internal experts who can help move the work forward, based on the topics discussed.

### Step 6: Generate and share summary

The final summary is compiled into a clean, structured format and sent to your preferred destination.
