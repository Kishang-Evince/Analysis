# Ghostwriter

Drafts content in your voice or someone else’s using past work and shared context.

## Overview

Need to send a follow-up from your VP? Draft a proposal in your manager’s style? The ghostwriter agent makes it easy. Just say what you need written and whose voice you want it in — and the agent will take it from there. It pulls from past messages to emulate tone, adds relevant context, and delivers a polished draft you can send or edit.

## See it in action

## Capabilities

Drafts polished content in the voice of any teammate or executive.Saves time on common writing tasks like emails, briefs, or proposals.Automatically adds context from past conversations and shared documents.Reduces the need for constant rewrites and approvals.

## Example output

Sure! Here's a draft follow-up email recapping the product strategy call and proposing next steps in the voice of your VP of Product, Jane Doe.

\---

Hi Michael,

Thanks for the thoughtful conversation today. It was great to hear more about your roadmap and where you're seeing traction.

As you continue exploring solutions to support scale and cross-functional alignment, we’d be happy to walk you through how Acme Corp is helping similar teams streamline product planning and decision-making. I’ll coordinate with Sam to find time next week.

Talk soon,

Jane

## Agent workflow

### Step 1: Receive user request

The user inputs what they want written and whose voice it should be in.

### Step 2: Retrieve writing samples

The agent searches email, chat, or documents to understand the person’s tone and style.

### Step 3: Search for context

It finds relevant details from past meetings, documents, or shared notes to inform the message.

### Step 4: Generate draft

The agent writes a clear, tailored draft in the selected voice, incorporating the right tone and information.

### Step 5: Deliver response

The draft is sent to the user in chat, ready to copy, tweak, or send.
