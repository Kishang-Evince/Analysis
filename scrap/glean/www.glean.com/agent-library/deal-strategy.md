# Deal strategy

Turn scattered deal context into clear next steps.

## Overview

Get a concise, accurate deal readout and actionable recommendations to move forward. Deal strategy pulls from your internal systems and playbooks to synthesize what matters, then collaborates with you on positioning and next steps. Designed for sales teams that want faster, better-informed deal execution without manual digging.

## See it in action

## Capabilities

-   Produces a crisp deal summary with stakeholders, timelines, and key factors that matter now.
-   Surfaces concrete action items tied to success criteria and checkpoints so reps can maintain momentum.
-   Combines CRM activity, call insights, and competitive context to inform positioning and next steps.
-   Reflects learnings from prior wins and losses to help AEs avoid pitfalls and double down on what works.

## Example output

### Deal basics 

-   500 seats in play; TCV $380,000; ARR $96,000; 2,100 employees; renewal target Nov 15, 2025. 
-   **Quick win:** finalize Jira and Confluence connector setup this week to unlock ML-powered search.
-   **Differentiators to anchor on:** trusted partner references and measurable productivity gains in support.

### Stakeholders: 

-   **Customer**: Priya Shah, CTO; Chris Alvarez, Sr. Director IT; Morgan Tate, VP Finance
-   **Acme**: Jamie Rivera, account owner; Dana Wu, solutions engineer

### Action items:

-   Complete Jira and Confluence connector setup; confirm full indexing by end of week. 
-   Enable customer success and support teams on Acme; track reduction in average handle time weekly. 
-   Schedule on-site working session with cross-functional stakeholders.

## Agent workflow

### Step 1: Receive user request

Search internal documentation and build an understanding of sales best practices for winning deals. 

### Step 2: Understand context

Is the request for a simple deal overview, competitive positioning, or drill-down of an overview item? 

### Step 3: Retrieve deal parameters

Search internal data to identify deal basics like timeline, stakeholders, and deal size, as well as action items. 

### Step 4: Identify next steps

Find immediate wins or actions that address the context of the query. 

### Step 5: Generate deal summary

Generate a structured deal readout and recommendations for the rep.
