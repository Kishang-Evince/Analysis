# SEO keyword research from sales calls

Identify the high-intent keywords hidden inside your sales call transcripts.

## Overview

The SEO keyword research from sales calls agent analyzes sales call transcripts for a specific industry to surface the recurring challenges, themes, and language prospects use most. It helps marketing teams validate content ideas, refine messaging, and build keyword strategies grounded in real customer conversations - not guesswork. By blending qualitative insights with SEO direction, it ensures teams focus on topics that truly matter to buyers.

## See it in action

## Capabilities

-   Highlights common pain points prospects mention across sales calls, revealing what audiences care about most.
-   Surfaces industry-specific language and terminology you can translate directly into keyword themes.
-   Identifies early patterns in objections and questions to help shape future content strategy.
-   Strengthens SEO prioritization by aligning content ideas with real buyer intent.

## Example output

#### Common pain points in the manufacturing sales call transcripts 

After reviewing a broad set of sales call transcripts from the manufacturing industry, several recurring pain points, keywords, and phrases appear across conversations. Below is a synthesized list of the most common themes prospects raised:

#### Cost and operational efficiency

-   “Lower production costs” / “cut waste”
-   “Budget freeze” / “capital constraints”
-   “Improve margins” / “tightening cost controls”

#### Data and system fragmentation

-   “Data lives in too many places”
-   “No single source of truth”
-   “Manual processes slow everything down”

#### Quality, compliance, and risk

-   “Regulatory pressure” / “audit readiness”
-   “Traceability challenges” / “documentation gaps”
-   “Security concerns” around shared files

#### Productivity and workflow bottlenecks

-   “Too much manual data entry”
-   “Slow handoffs between teams”
-   “Hard to onboard new engineers quickly”

These themes reflect consistent patterns across manufacturers - from industrial equipment makers to specialty goods producers - highlighting where prospects face friction and what language they use when describing their challenges.

## Agent workflow

### Step 1: Trigger the workflow

The user enters an industry and runs the agent, prompting it to begin the research process.

### Step 2: Identify relevant transcripts

The agent searches internal sales call recordings for conversations related to the selected industry.

### Step 3: Extract key themes

It scans the transcripts to extract recurring pain points, keywords, and the language buyers repeat.

### Step 4: Validate themes with web search

The agent checks top themes and phrases against live web search signals to confirm which terms show meaningful interest or demand.

### Step 5: Map insights to SEO direction

It clusters themes into keyword ideas and content angles aligned with real buyer language.

### Step 6: Create the deliverable

The agent compiles a Google Doc summarizing the pain points, keyword opportunities, and topic recommendations in a structured, easy-to-share format.
