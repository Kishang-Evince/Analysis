# Intelligent reminders

Finds your open action items across meetings, messages, and docs.

## Overview

The intelligent reminder agent reviews your recent activity across meetings, messages, emails, and docs to surface the tasks and requests you may have missed. It extracts action items based on conversations and context - then returns a clear, organized list so you can stay on top of commitments.

## See it in action

## Capabilities

Surfaces open action items from recent meetings, chats, and emails.Helps you follow through on asks from others - even ones you didn’t write down.Reduces the risk of missed deadlines or overlooked tasks.Saves time by scanning all your tools for relevant follow-ups.Prioritizes high-confidence items so you can focus where it matters most.

## Agent workflow

### Step 1: Search recent work

The agent looks across your chats, emails, docs, and more to find potential action items.

### Step 2: Review meeting transcripts

It searches your calendar and reads through notes and transcripts from your latest meetings.

### Step 3: Extract open action items

The agent identifies requests, follow-ups, and to-dos - even ones buried in long threads or conversations.

### Step 4: Generate a prioritized list

The agent groups action items by source and urgency, distinguishing between clear asks and ambiguous mentions.

### Step 5: Present the summary

It displays your action items in a clean, readable format - ready to check off or delegate.
