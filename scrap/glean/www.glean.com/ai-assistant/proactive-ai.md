## Give every employee and every team a head start.

### Know what needs attention

Automatically see the updates, blockers, requests, and decisions most relevant to you, without digging through every app, doc, or thread.

### Get work started automatically

Independent agents (beta) monitor issues, investigate what’s happening, bring in the right people, and carry out the next steps.

Independent agents

## Automate complex work.

Cross-functional work depends on smooth handoffs. Independent agents act as autonomous AI coworkers for teams, monitoring and investigating issues, bringing in the right people, and carrying out next steps.

![Product screenshot](http://assets.glean.com/marketing/Proactive%20Intelligence/independent-agents-new.webp)

Meeting Coach

## Learn from every conversation.

Meeting Coach learns what great looks like across your organization. After each conversation, it gives you personalized, actionable feedback you can use in the next one.

![Product screenshot](http://assets.glean.com/marketing/Proactive%20Intelligence/meeting-coach.webp)

## Find what matters in your inbox.
