## FAQs

What is Glean Companion?

Glean Companion is an AI writing and understanding assistant that brings editing, explanations, and company context into the documents and web pages where employees already work.

What can users do with Glean Companion?

Users can highlight text to explain, rewrite, improve, or summarize it without leaving the page.

Where does Glean Companion work?

Glean Companion works in web-based spaces such as Google Docs, Jira tickets, and industry websites, bringing company context with users as they work.

How does Glean Companion improve writing quality?

It turns rough drafts into polished content using company-specific context, voice, and standards, rather than producing generic AI output.

Can Glean Companion help users understand complex content?

Yes. It can explain policies, product docs, and other complex material in plain language, helping users understand content faster.

Can Glean Companion help users find experts and related work?

Yes. Based on what the user is viewing, it can identify colleagues with relevant expertise and surface related projects or content.
