## Keep the work moving, together

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8c4422d2335e3a81528003_toys_and_games_24dp_1F1F1F_FILL0_wght300_GRAD0_opsz24%201.svg)

### Keep context through handoffs

Hand off a shared chat without starting over. Teammates can review prior work, ask follow up questions, and continue from the same context.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8c4422645799f253bfa425_group_add_24dp_1F1F1F_FILL0_wght400_GRAD0_opsz24%201.svg)

### Bring in the right expertise

Invite reviewers, decision-makers, and subject-matter experts when their input is needed. Each person can prompt Glean directly and build on shared context.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8c4422085ee5221dea5cc0_edit_24dp_1F1F1F_FILL0_wght300_GRAD0_opsz24%201.svg)

### Turn comments into stronger drafts

Use a reviewer’s comment to ask Glean for an in-line edit or response. The feedback reaches the next version with fewer handoffs and less room for misinterpretation.

Team chat

## Find answers together from the start.

Invite teammates into a team chat. They can review the history, ask follow-ups with their existing permissions, and continue the conversation without starting over.

Co-creation

## Build together.

Create in on shared thread, where each teammate brings their own context and shapes the same artifact without passing drafts back and forth.

![Product screenshot](https://assets.glean.com/marketing/Multiplayer/Co-creation.webp)

Artifact review

## Leave a comment and let Glean make the change.

Reviewers can comment directly on a Glean artifact and @-mention teammates. Then, the artifact owner can ask Glean to make the requested change from the comment itself.

![Product screenshot](https://assets.glean.com/marketing/Multiplayer/artifact-review.webp)

## How Glean Multiplayer works

01

Share

Invite teammates to an existing chat and choose who can participate.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8e98cf62da2619b8349d41_multiplayer-share.png)

02

Contribute

Each person can prompt Glean, add their context, and build on what the group has already surfaced.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8e98cfa677ae87f7a57f19_multiplayer-contribute.png)

03

Get answers

Each user can ask Glean questions and get responses based on their own permissions, right in the team chat.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8e98d03b7c8bea659bbd5d_multiplayer-get-answers.png)

04

Comment

Leave comments directly on shared artifacts, @-mention individuals, and ask Glean to turn feedback into edits.
