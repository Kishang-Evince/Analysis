### Knowledge grounded in your actions

Glean gathers personal activity from 100+ data sources, drilling down to granular details like edits, replies, pings, and more. It layers up this data into tasks and projects to truly understand how you get work done. This approach allows Glean to capture nuanced relationships, like prioritization inferred from turnaround times, personal commitments to collaborators, and connections between tasks and strategic goals.

### Content-specific personal style

Work happens dynamically depending on the task at hand— especially the way we write. Glean’s personal graph categorizes your communication styles in up to 5 different profiles depending on content type and audience. The best profile is automatically fetched during any creative work, ensuring that the right rendition of your personal voice is included in any writing task.

### Enterprise intelligence at any resolution

Glean’s personal graph and Enterprise Graph work together to imbue AI with both high level strategic awareness and practical expertise on how you and your company work best. AI on Glean can freely ladder up or down in knowledge abstraction to accomplish deep, intricate tasks with full supporting context.

## FAQs

What is Glean Personal Graph?

Glean Personal Graph is a personalization layer that understands a user’s goals, commitments, priorities, writing style, and ways of working.

How does Personal Graph personalize AI assistance?

It helps Glean prioritize what matters, tailor responses to the user’s context, and provide support that aligns with their role and goals.

Can Glean write in a user’s own voice?

Yes. Glean learns from a user’s past work and can adapt writing for different content types and audiences, so the output feels more natural and personal.

What data does Personal Graph learn from?

It learns from personal activity across connected data sources, including edits, replies, messages, and other work signals that roll up into tasks and projects.

How does Personal Graph connect work to company priorities?

It links a user’s day-to-day activities to broader company initiatives, helping employees see how their work contributes to larger goals.

How does Personal Graph work with Enterprise Graph?

Together, Personal Graph and Enterprise Graph give Glean strategic company-wide context and practical knowledge of how each person works.
