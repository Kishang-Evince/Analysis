## Prepare recruiter intake brief

## prompt

You are helping a recruiter prepare for a hiring intake. Review the following materials: \[\[Job description\]\] \[\[Hiring manager notes\]\] \[\[Optional calibration notes\]\] Summarize the role in a way a recruiter can immediately act on. Include: 1. Core mission of the role 2. Must-have qualifications 3. Nice-to-have qualifications 4. Likely talent backgrounds to target 5. Top selling points for candidates 6. Risks or ambiguities that should be clarified with the hiring manager 7. A concise kickoff summary the recruiter can reuse in sourcing or alignment conversations.
