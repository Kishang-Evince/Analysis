## Become an AI-native enterprise with Glean on AWS

## **Securely scale AI agents** 

Glean expands on the safeguards built into AWS, ensuring safe AI adoption with permission-based data access, protection against prompt injection and malicious content, plus guardrails that govern how agents operate at work.

## **Put AI agents to work today**

Glean works out-of-the-box with native connectors, dozens of quickstart agents, and a model hub that features Amazon Bedrock models.

## **Build and run agents anywhere**

Glean is an open, horizontal AI platform that grounds assistants and agents built anywhere in enterprise knowledge and security. Build agents directly on the Glean app or use MCP servers to develop on Bedrock Agents.

### **Your data, your AWS account, your control.**

Get enterprise-grade security, compliance, and data privacy built in. Your data stays within your own AWS account and resides in your preferred region for complete control.

### **Choose the best Amazon Bedrock models for your agents.**

Choose the best models from Amazon Bedrock, including Nova, Claude, Deepseek, Llama families, for any agent or step in an agentic workflow. Optimize your agents based on price, performance, or expertise. 

![Interoperable by design so you can build on Glean or Bedrock Agents.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/688b9f2079e932cadc90e4ad_Interoperable%20by%20design%20so%20you%20can%20build%20on%20Glean%20or%20Bedrock%20Agents..webp)

### **Interoperable by design so you can build on Glean or Bedrock Agents.**

Build agents directly on the Glean app or use MCP servers to develop on Bedrock Agents. Index and secure your data once, and run Glean agents on any environment.

### **Get started quickly with Glean on Amazon Marketplace.**

Streamline procurement and burn down your enterprise commitments with Glean on Amazon Marketplace. Go live on AI agents in less than 3 weeks and realize the benefit - up to 110 hours saved per employee per year.
