## FAQs

Who is eligible to join the Glean Referral Program?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

The program is designed for external referrers such as fractional CTOs/CDOs, AI and data consultants, independent advisors, and trusted CSP/PE or broader ecosystem contacts who influence AI, data, or digital workplace initiatives at mid-market and enterprise companies.  

What kinds of customers and deals are in scope?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

We focus on net-new private-sector opportunities that fit Glean’s ICP: mid-market and enterprise companies with fragmented knowledge across tools and strong AI/productivity initiatives. The account must not be an existing Glean customer, there must be no active qualified opportunity, and no recent meaningful sales engagement with the buying center before your referral.  

What counts as a qualified referral?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

A qualified referral is when you introduce Glean to a net-new account in our ICP and your intro directly leads to a meaningful sales engagement with the buying center (e.g., CIO, CDO, VP-level sponsor) that was not already in motion via Glean.  

How much can I earn for a successful referral?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

For eligible referrals that turn into a net-new, private-sector closed-won deal, you earn 5% of first-year ARR on the initial order.  

When do I get paid?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

Referral fees are earned only after Glean receives payment from the customer and are paid in line with our standard payment cycles (typically within ~45 days of customer payment), under the terms of our legal-approved referral agreement. If a customer fails to pay, receives a refund, or terminates early, we may reduce, withhold, or claw back fees on a pro-rated basis as defined in the agreement.  

Do I need to sell Glean or stay involved in the opportunity?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

No. Your job is to make a warm introduction; the Glean team will run the sales process (qualification, demos, negotiation, close). You still earn 5% of first-year ARR if the deal closes and the referral meets the program rules.  

How will I know what’s happening with my referrals?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

The referral team handles eligibility checks, logging in our referral tracker/CRM, and routing to SDRs/AEs. We aim to respond quickly (within about one business day) to confirm eligibility, and we’ll provide periodic updates as the opportunity progresses or closes.  

Do I need to sign an agreement?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

Yes. Qualified referrers will sign a short, legal-approved referral agreement that covers payment terms, confidentiality, and standard commercial protections. This is required before any referral fees can be paid out.  

Which industries and use cases are the best fit?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

Glean is a leading Work AI platform used by mid-market and enterprise companies across industries such as technology & startups, financial services, telecom, travel & airlines, and oil & gas. Common use cases include developer productivity, contact center optimization, employee onboarding, customer onboarding, and customer support.  

Who can I contact if I’m not sure a referral qualifies?

![Down arrow](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/63fcbe1dc4d91b2c368e403d_Chevron%20Down%20Black.svg)

If you’re unsure whether a specific referrer or referral is in scope, start by emailing referrals@glean.com with the details you have. Internally, questions are handled by the Referral/Partner team and relevant sales leaders, who will confirm eligibility and next steps and ensure your referral is treated consistently with program rules.
