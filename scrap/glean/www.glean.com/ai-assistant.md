## How Glean Assistant works

01

Find

Assistant searches across company tools, docs, conversations, and the web to collect the most relevant context.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8d9b5b62ee0e705e760ce3_assistant-find.webp)

02

Plan

By reasoning through your request, Assistant creates a multi-step strategy and coordinates sub-agents to work in parallel.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8d9b55561058ed9fc353b8_assistant-plan.webp)

03

Research

Going beyond the initial scope, Assistant sends scouts to uncover missing context and close knowledge gaps.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8d9b58db03f56aa5c06e65_assistant-research.webp)

04

Iterate

It evaluates progress at each step, refining the plan and asking questions until the output is accurate and complete.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8d9b52aaa3f72c291cf10f_assistant-iterate.webp)

05

Deliver

Finally, it consolidates insights, outputs, and next steps into a polished result tailored to you.
