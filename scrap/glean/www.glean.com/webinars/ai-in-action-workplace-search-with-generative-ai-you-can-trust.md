## Overview

Many experts say that AI will disrupt one vertical after the next. It’s already disrupting the technology space. Whether it’s generating code, testing code or creating malware, AI has made big waves in a short time. Contrary to some predictions, AI will not eliminate the need for developers or make DevOps jobs obsolete; instead, it will increase developer productivity and accelerate the entire SDLC.  

In this AI in Action session, we focus on current AI use cases and explore how AI is revolutionizing the business world and the technology industry.
