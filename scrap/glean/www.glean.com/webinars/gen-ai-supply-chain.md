## Overview

Supply chain operations are evolving and GenAI is helping businesses move from reactive decisions to proactive, co-ordinated strategies.

By closing gaps between teams, systems and decisions, AI is helping supply chain leaders unlock speed, visibility and resilience at scale.

Watch this episode of AI Talk hosted by Kevin Craine to explore:

-   Why fragmented systems slow down decision-making across teams
-   How AI solves co-ordination challenges across the lifecycle
-   How AI is closing the gap between knowledge and execution in supply chain operations

Watch this panel discussion to discover how AI is transforming supply chain operations from insight to execution.
