## Overview

AI assistants, or "agents," are changing how work happens. These tools can retrieve information, support multi-step workflows and automate repetitive tasks. By stepping in where needed, AI agents are becoming an essential part of how we get things done.  

You'll hear real-world examples and practical strategies from Mark Christianson, Sr. Manager, Digital Workplace & AI Strategy at ezCater and Kat Crichton, Customer Success Manager at Glean for integrating these tools into your organization.  

**During this webinar, you will:**  

\- See how organizations are using AI agents to support daily tasks and boost productivity.

\- Learn about practical use cases for custom generative AI solutions in the workplace.

\- Gain tips for building and adopting AI agents that fit your team's needs.
