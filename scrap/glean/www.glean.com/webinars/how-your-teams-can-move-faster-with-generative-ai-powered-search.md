## Overview

In 2023, there's been a lot of talk of generative AI. But how are you businesses actually thinking about tapping into the transformative power of generative AI in the modern workplace this year and beyond? Al Linke, AVP of Enterprise Applications & PMO at SentinelOne, and Lauren Kennedy, Head of Customer Success at Glean, discuss how enterprise-grade generative AI search capabilities can help knowledge workers worldwide. They share how these capabilities can help remove obstacles like knowledge silos and busywork, empowering people to excel in their roles. Then they explore how the latest advancements in technology can provide users with accurate and secure information, making it easier to integrate genuinely useful generative AI into their workflows.
