## Overview

Automate everyday work by deploying AI assistants and agents at scale.  

This webinar is a unique opportunity to hear from experts and peers on how to adopt a platform approach to building AI applications with enterprise security and scalability, including a deep dive into our latest AI advancements. Glean is on a mission to enable every employee to use AI meaningfully in their work, as we believe that only AI-centric employees can create AI-centric companies.

The keynote by Will Hiebert, Regional Vice President - APAC at Glean, is followed by a fireside chat on AI trends in the workplace (the shift into the agentic era) and a spotlight session by Nathan Catania.
