## Overview

Building at scale starts with building for yourself. See how Glean teams use our AI coworker to do higher-value work, make sharper decisions, and drive greater impact, faster

Watch this session to discover three concrete examples of how we’re putting it to work day to day: 

-   **Product Design Explorer**: An internal tool for testing product directions and gathering feedback early in the design process.
-   **Debugging Agent**: A specialized agent for tackling connector escalations and speeding up technical issue resolution.
-   **Daily AI Newsbrief**: A daily briefing turning competitor news into clear and context-grounded takeaways for the Solutions Engineering team.
