### Speakers

## Overview

Join us for a conversation with Henry Shi, Co-founder of Super.com, as he discusses how they streamlined the information discovery process, saving their employees an average of twenty minutes daily, which compounded to over 1,500+ hours saved monthly across their workforce.

In this on-demand webinar, we begin by introducing Glean with Deedy Das, Software Engineer at Glean, followed by a conversation with Henry. During the chat, you’ll learn how the Super.com team:  

-   Implemented a written culture and streamlined their knowledge discovery process during a period of rapid growth
-   Paired their written culture with an AI-powered knowledge hub to maximize productivity
-   Harnessed the power of Glean Chat to optimize their knowledge management process further
