## Overview

AI agents are reshaping how organizations operate – accelerating productivity, enhancing knowledge access, and streamlining workflows across departments. Yet many companies struggle to build and deploy agents across the enterprise, and those who have begun deploying agents are grappling with how to scale agent strategies beyond isolated use cases.  

Join 451 Research Analyst Chris Marsh and Glean Head of Product Emrecan Dogan as we explore how organizations can unlock the full potential of AI agents by investing in scalable, horizontal infrastructure.  

This webinar will dive into real-world departmental use cases, recent research insights, and early adopters' lessons. Attendees will gain a deeper understanding of the agent-building learning curve, organizational pain points, and the strategic decisions that drive adoption across functions like sales, support, engineering, HR, and beyond.  

Whether you're an IT leader, innovation strategist, or business executive, this session will equip you with actionable guidance to design, deploy, and scale intelligent agents across your enterprise.
