## Overview

Join SentinelOne CIO Sandy Venugopal for a conversation with Glean about the opportunities and challenges with adopting generative AI in the enterprise. She'll share her firsthand insights on this pivotal moment in technology, alongside a deep dive into Glean's recent survey of IT decision makers.  
  
We'll answer questions including:  

-   How much are CIOs planning to invest in generative AI, and where should they focus?
-   How can IT drive effective adoption of generative AI?
-   What can CIOs do to measure the effectiveness of their generative AI initiatives?
