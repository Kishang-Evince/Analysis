## Overview

Today’s retail and e-commerce organizations are navigating increasing complexity – siloed information, disconnected tools, and manual workflows can slow teams down and hinder innovation. But with the right AI tools in place, teams can break through the noise to streamline workflows and focus on more high-impact work.

In this webinar, you'll hear two avid Glean users at Etsy share how Glean’s AI-powered enterprise search is boosting their productivity. They’ll share firsthand examples of how they leverage Glean on a day-to-day basis to work more efficiently, reduce time spent searching for information, and make faster, more informed decisions.

You’ll learn:

-   How Etsy power users leverage Glean to stay organized, focused, and efficient across their teams’ operations
-   Tips for driving adoption and real value from AI tools
-   Examples of impactful use cases that are leading to productivity gains at both the individual and teamwide level.
