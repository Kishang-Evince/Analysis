## Overview

AI agents are reshaping how work gets done, but most employees need clear guidance to start building agents in their business environments. In this live webinar, we’ll show you how to automate work at scale by building agents tailored to complex workflows, cross-functional teams, and real organizational challenges.  

This session will walk you through the key steps to build AI agents for real, scalable impact. Don’t miss out on your chance to see live demos of high-impact agents that drive significant ROI and speed up workflow completion by up to 97%.

Whether it’s accelerating security reviews, streamlining support operations, or helping go-to-market teams move faster, these agents are designed to solve real problems and deliver measurable impact.  

What you’ll learn:

-   What business AI agents are and how they can be leveraged and scaled across entire organizations
-   A practical framework for identifying use cases, designing effective agents, and scaling adoption across your organization
-   The most common pitfalls teams face when building agents, and how to avoid them
-   How forward-thinking teams are already using Glean Agents to automate security review workflows, accelerate support resolution, improve internal search, and more.
