## Overview

AI-enabled decision-making is transforming how financial services organisations improve efficiency, accuracy, and operational performance at scale.

As organisations move beyond isolated AI pilots, many are rethinking workflows and operating models to unlock measurable impact across complex business processes.

Watch this episode of AI Talk with Kevin Craine, who hosts a panel discussion where we’ll explore:

-   Transforming decision-making in core banking workflows and insurance operations
-   Lessons from organisations who struggle to scale - moving from AI pilots to real operational impact
-   Building AI native workflows that improve efficiency, accuracy and decision making at scale

Watch this panel discussion to discover how AI-native workflows are reshaping decision-making and driving scalable transformation across financial services.
