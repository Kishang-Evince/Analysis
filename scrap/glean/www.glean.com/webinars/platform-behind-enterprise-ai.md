## Overview

Every new assistant, agent, and AI application needs access to company context, models, tools, and permissions. 

Rebuilding that foundation for every use case slows teams down and makes AI harder to govern.

Join us for a closer look at the Glean platform and the technology behind secure, enterprise-wide AI. We’ll show how Glean connects company knowledge once, then makes that trusted context available across the AI experiences your teams use and build.

You’ll learn how to:

-   Ground AI in permission-aware company knowledge
-   Give teams access to models and tools through a governed platform
-   Manage security, usage, and agent activity from one place

Leave with a clear view of the foundation enterprises need to build, deploy, and govern AI at scale.
