## Overview

Security leaders are under pressure to move fast on AI while keeping firm control over risk. A recent IBM study found that [97% of organizations](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/webfiles/Publications/20250822_Cost-of-a-Data-Breach-Report-2025.pdf) experiencing AI-related security incidents lacked adequate AI access controls, underscoring the need for AI security guardrails. 

In this candid, live conversation, security leaders at Palo Alto Networks and Glean dig into what’s working, where teams get tripped up, and how to *Deploy Bravely* with the right controls in place. 

What you’ll walk away with:  

-   What to secure across the AI lifecycle, including model posture, runtime protections against prompt injection and data leaks, and agent security for tool misuse and identity abuse.
-   Practical guardrails Security should own, such as least‑privilege access, governed data from prompt to response, policy‑driven detection and blocking of risky prompts in production.
-   How Glean and Palo Alto Networks work together by pairing a secure AI platform with comprehensive AI security to standardize controls, prove compliance, and enable the business to adopt AI safely.
