### Speakers

## Overview

What if every employee at your company had their own personal assistant? Register to hear conversation with Glean and Confluent on how to take a platform approach to building and deploying AI chatbots, assistants, and apps with enterprise security and scale, including real-life use cases across functions from Matt Mangia, Staff Partner Solutions Engineer at Confluent.

### What You'll Learn

-   Benefits of generative AI agents that are tailored to specific use cases (e.g. HR FAQs, IT helpdesk, documentation assistant).
-   Combat concerns about AI’s security, governance, and other problems within the workplace
-   How to automate everyday work by connecting data, people, and AI
