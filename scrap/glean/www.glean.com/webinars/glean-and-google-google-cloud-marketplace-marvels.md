### Speakers

## Overview

In this special CUBE presentation of the Google Cloud Marketplace Marvels Series, John Furrier, executive analyst at theCUBE Research, welcomes Steven Orban, VP of migrations, ISVs and marketplace at Google Cloud, and Tamar Yehoshua, president of product and technology at Glean Technologies.

They discuss the success of Google Cloud Marketplace in empowering ISVs such as Glean to scale operations and enhance customer experiences. Glean, a work AI platform, utilizes Google Cloud's tools to break down data silos and enable enterprises to find and utilize data more effectively, integrating advanced search and generative AI.
