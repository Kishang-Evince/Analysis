## Overview

The era of AI experimentation is over. Today’s focus is on delivering real business value. While many early AI pilots have stalled, a new, value-focused approach is helping organizations drive real results.

Join Juliet Bramwell, Glean's VP of EMEA, and Jonathan Eley, long-form editor at The Financial Times, for a practical discussion on how to make workplace AI deliver tangible outcomes. Drawing on real-world examples from Glean customers like Time Magazine and Deutsche Telekom, this session will provide a blueprint for a successful AI transformation.

**In this webinar, you'll learn:**

-   **How to define, measure, and scale AI use cases that matter.** Move past the hype and focus on initiatives that deliver clear ROI for business leaders in sales, marketing, HR, and R&D.
-   **Why culture and leadership are the real make-or-break factors for adoption.** Learn how to build a culture of innovation and psychological safety, with executive role models who champion AI literacy and experimentation.
-   **The playbook for a successful rollout.** Discover the importance of having a clear sponsor, identifying internal champions, and starting with narrow use cases that can be scaled quickly.
-   **How to balance automation with the human touch.** Hear how AI is augmenting employees and freeing them from low-value work, not replacing them, and why humans are still essential for making critical decisions.

Whether you're sponsoring your first AI initiative or scaling across departments, this session will show you how to move from "what can AI do?" to "what can AI do for *us*?"
