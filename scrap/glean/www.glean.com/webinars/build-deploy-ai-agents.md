## Overview

What if the work you repeat every week could keep moving without starting from scratch each time?

Join us for a live look at Glean Agents and see how people can turn their expertise and recurring processes into AI coworkers. We’ll show how agents can prepare meeting briefs, monitor important changes, coordinate work across systems, and take action with the right company context and permissions.

You’ll see what agents can do for an individual employee, how teams can share and improve successful workflows, and how IT can support adoption without losing visibility or control.

You’ll learn how to:

-   Build agents that understand your company and how work gets done
-   Turn recurring tasks and processes into agents that continue working on your behalf
-   Move from a useful personal workflow to agents shared across a team
-   Give IT visibility into adoption, quality, access, and ROI

See what it takes to move from one useful agent to measurable impact across the organization.
