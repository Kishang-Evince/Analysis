## Overview

Conversations around AI and the future of software engineering often revolve around faster code, smarter tools, and more automation. But what happens when engineering leaders challenge conventional metrics and rethink productivity from the ground up?

Join us for a webinar featuring Abdullah Haydar, Director of Engineering at LinkedIn and a renowned leader in Enterprise Productivity. With experience at Intel, Amazon, AAA, and major startups, Abdullah brings a unique front-line perspective on the hidden shifts reshaping engineering work.

Whether you're an engineering leader or a developer passionate about the future, you’ll walk away with actionable insights on: 

-   Why are the biggest bottlenecks in engineering not about writing code, and how can we tackle the overlooked “hidden tax” of collaboration, integration, and bureaucracy that come with extra productivity?
-   Why top engineering teams are measuring productivity in radically new ways, beyond code lines and pull requests.
-   How code generation tools redistribute, not eliminate, work, requiring fresh skills in design, integration, and communication that most engineers never expected.
-   The surprising role communication and language play, and how AI-powered documentation is quietly leveling the global playing field.
-   Real stories from LinkedIn and others about bleeding-edge adoption and early mistakes.

Don’t miss this opportunity to future-proof your engineering practices and stay ahead in an AI-driven world.
