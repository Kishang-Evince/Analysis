## Overview

Transform how your whole company uses AI at work. In this live session, Glean’s Senior Solutions Engineer, Justin Swadling, shows exactly how he uses Glean every day to find, create, and automate work across all his tools.

What you’ll learn:

-   How Glean connects to tools like Slack, Google Drive, Jira, Confluence, Salesforce, Snowflake, and more to index all your structured and unstructured data into a single, secure knowledge graph.
-   How retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with Glean dramatically improves LLM performance, reduces token spend, and lets you use any model you like.
-   How permission-aware search and governance work in Glean so every employee only sees what they’re authorized to see, while IT and security teams stay in full control.
-   How teams in mid-market and large enterprises use Glean as a context layer and Work AI platform to summarize incidents, prep renewals, evaluate candidates, and generate content faster.
