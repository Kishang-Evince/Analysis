### Speakers

## Overview

Glean introduced enterprise search using AI, leveraging deep learning techniques for enterprise knowledge understanding and personalization to provide relevant answers to user questions. In this talk, T.R. Vishwanath and James Simonsen discuss how they built an enterprise knowledge graph that indexes content, people and activity to serve fast search results. They’ll talk through the challenges they faced making AI-based search real time and privacy aware at scale.
