## Overview

Most enterprise AI programs begin team by team. Each investment may solve a local problem, but companies end up with fragmented context, duplicated effort, uneven adoption, and inconsistent controls.

Glean gives every team an AI coworker built on the same company knowledge. A seller preparing for a customer meeting can draw on CRM data, past calls, new product announcements, and open support issues. A support agent can bring together the customer history, relevant help content, and the latest guidance from engineering. Product and engineering teams can use feedback from sales and support to understand customers' needs and make better decisions.

Join us to see how Glean brings company knowledge, people, and workflows into one enterprise AI platform. You’ll see how employees get the context they need for their role while leaders create a more connected, consistent way of working across the company.

You’ll learn how to:

-   Give every team a tailored AI coworker grounded in the same trusted company knowledge
-   Connect insights across sales, support, product, engineering, and other business teams
-   Help employees use knowledge from across the company without searching through disconnected systems
-   Expand successful workflows and AI adoption on one enterprise-wide foundation

See what enterprise AI looks like when every team can contribute to and benefit from the company’s collective knowledge.
