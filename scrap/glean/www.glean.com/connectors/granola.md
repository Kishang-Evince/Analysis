### Overview

Granola is an AI note-taking tool that captures meeting notes, summaries, transcripts, and follow-ups. Glean's integration lets users search team and personal notes, folders, and transcript-backed context to quickly locate decisions and next steps.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

• Team space notes  
• Personal notes for connected users  
• AI-generated meeting summaries  
• Full meeting transcripts and event metadata

### Benefits

Recover meeting context fast — no replaying calls, no digging through folders. Understand how positions shifted, what was agreed, and where threads were left open by searching Granola notes and transcripts alongside docs, tickets, CRM data, and chats.
