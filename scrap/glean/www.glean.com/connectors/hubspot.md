### Overview

Hubspot is a software company that builds a suite of products designed for inbound marketing, sales, and customer support teams. With Glean’s Hubspot connector, sales and services teams can get the insight to progress deals forward and resolve customer support issues.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Contacts
-   Deals
-   Companies
-   Tickets

Glean executes actions across:

-   Create and update CRM data

### Benefits

With Glean, organizations access a unified knowledge base for AI across their enterprise. The HubSpot connector supports both sales and support use cases end to end: sales teams can quickly find the most relevant contacts, deals, and companies to streamline prospecting, while customer support teams can aggregate and search across tickets to resolve issues faster. Glean can also take action back in HubSpot – updating and creating CRM fields and records – so your sales and support data stays accurate and up to date as things change.
