### Overview

Mixpanel's connector for Glean helps teams access dashboards, reports, events, funnels, flows, retention insights, and session analytics without leaving their workflow. With live MCP-powered queries and secure, permission-aware access, teams can get fast answers on product performance, user behavior, and feature adoption right in Glean.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Mixpanel dashboards and saved reports
-   Live insights, funnel, flow, and retention queries
-   Events, properties, and analytics schema
-   Cohorts and key product metrics in context

Glean executes actions across:

-   Analyze session replays alongside enterprise knowledge
-   Surface Mixpanel data in Glean Assistant answers
-   Keep access permission-aware by Mixpanel project permissions
-   Combine analytics with docs, tickets, and conversations

### Benefits

Give product, engineering, marketing, and data teams one place to understand adoption, retention, and conversion so they can make faster, data-backed decisions without switching tools.
