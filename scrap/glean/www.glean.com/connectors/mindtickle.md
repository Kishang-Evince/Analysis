### Overview

Mindtickle is the leading AI-powered revenue enablement platform to help revenue teams sell smarter, coach more effectively, and engage modern buyers. With Glean and Mindtickle working together, your teams can quickly and easily find the training and collateral they need within the tools they already use.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Leverage federated enterprise search, making all training and collateral discoverable
-   Surface relevant content in tools your reps use, like Slack, Google Drive, and more
-   Track and report on content engagement accessed through Glean in Mindtickle

### Benefits

Increase the ROI of your content by making it easy for reps to find in the flow of work. Enforce content governance by only surfacing relevant content that each individual has access to.
