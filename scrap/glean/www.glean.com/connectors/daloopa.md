### Overview

Daloopa structures and source-links financial data for 5,500+ public companies — from fundamentals and operating KPIs to SEC filings and earnings materials. The Daloopa MCP connector gives analysts direct access to that data layer in Glean, so every figure in a model or answer traces back to the original filing.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

• Company fundamentals, financial statements, and operating KPIs  
• SEC filings, earnings transcripts, and investor presentations  
• Source-linked financial data points with full provenance• Historical financials across reporting periods and geographies

### Benefits

Daloopa in Glean puts sourced financial data in the same workflow as the analysis it supports, making it faster to move from filings to usable answers with figures that stay tied to their original source.
