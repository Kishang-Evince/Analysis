### Overview

Salesforce is a leading customer relationship managament platform. Connecting Salesforce with Glean brings live CRM data into your daily workflow. Look up records and prep accounts with full context, update deals or cases directly, and automate GTM and support routines — all from Glean, while Salesforce remains your system of record.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Accounts
-   Contacts
-   Opportunities
-   Leads

Glean executes actions across:

-   Create campaigns, opportunities, accounts, leads, contacts, and notes
-   Update accounts and leads
-   Log activity data like emails and calls

### Benefits

Glean gives sales and GTM teams a single, context-rich workspace where live CRM data powers faster deal cycles, informed prospecting, and more consistent deal and customer management.
