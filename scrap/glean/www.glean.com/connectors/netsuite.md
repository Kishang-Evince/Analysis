### Overview

NetSuite is a cloud ERP for finance, ops, and revenue teams. Connecting NetSuite with Glean lets you securely search invoices, purchase orders, sales orders, payment histories, and customer accounts, while mirroring NetSuite permissions so users only see data they’re allowed to access.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Vendors
-   Billing data
-   Purchase orders
-   Customer data
-   Invoices
-   Sales orders

### Benefits

Turn NetSuite’s system‑of‑record data into discoverable, usable context for everyone at work—so you get faster answers to finance questions, better visibility into revenue, and streamlined back‑office workflows.
