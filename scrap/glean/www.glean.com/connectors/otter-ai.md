### Overview

Otter.ai is an AI meeting agent that captures transcripts, notes, summaries, and action items across Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. Glean's integration lets teams search past meeting content to recover key moments, commitments, and next steps.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

• Meeting transcripts and conversation text  
• AI-generated meeting notes and summaries  
• Action items and follow-ups from past meetings  
• Decisions and key context captured in meeting records

### Benefits

Surface what was said, what was decided, and what needs to happen next — without digging through separate meeting tools. Otter.ai in Glean makes meeting knowledge searchable alongside the rest of your company's context.
