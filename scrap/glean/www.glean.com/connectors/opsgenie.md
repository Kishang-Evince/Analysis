### Overview

Opsgenie is modern incident management and alert notification software designed to help DevOps and IT operations teams manage critical issues efficiently. Glean connects to Opsgenie to enable IT teams to enhance readiness and response times by seamlessly reviewing previous notifications and actions taken in Opsgenie.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Alert management
-   Incident response
-   Reporting and analytics

### Benefits

Give incident response and SRE teams an advantage with broad visibility into past Opsgenie activity from within Glean.
