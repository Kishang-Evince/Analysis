### Overview

Enterpret helps teams understand customer feedback across sources using a structured model built for product and customer insights. Glean's integration lets users search themes, find insights with source links, and bring customer intelligence into Assistant and agent workflows.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Query customer feedback using natural language
-   Surface trends, patterns, and sentiment across feedback sources
-   Understand the relationship between the feedback and the product area

### Benefits

Validate product decisions with real customer evidence and spot emerging trends without manually stitching together data. With Glean, Enterpret feedback, roadmaps, support tickets, and internal docs are all usable alongside the rest of your organization's context.
