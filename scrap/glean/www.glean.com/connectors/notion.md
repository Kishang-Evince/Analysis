### Overview

Notion helps users schedule tasks, manage files, save documents, set reminders, keep agendas, and organize their work. Glean’s integration with Notion enables users to search all of their Notion files, workflows, and tasks to get everything they need, quickly.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Pages in Notion, including objects like text content documents
-   Databases in Notion, including objects like calendars, tables, task trackers, etc

Glean executes actions across:

-   Create and update pages
-   Organize pages
-   Leave comments

### Benefits

Find specs, runbooks, team wikis, and meeting notes in Notion alongside related Jira issues, Slack threads, or design docs. Quickly answer “how do we…?” questions by searching across Notion pages and databases. As work happens, you can update pages with weekly status reports, templates, and even meeting notes.
