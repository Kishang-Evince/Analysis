### Overview

Trello is a visual tool that helps teams manage any type of project, workflow, or task tracking. It allows users to add files, checklists, or even automation. Glean finds the users that have been given view access to the board and then resolves those permissions with any other rules and overrides, such as workspace-wide visibility, to determine the final list of all users that are allowed to search for that board within Glean. Glean is notified of create/update/delete actions regarding cards and update/delete actions regarding boards.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Workspaces (which the admin user is part of)
-   Boards
-   Cards

### Benefits

Increase your team’s productivity by keeping all Trello content easily accessible and in one place.

Easily keep track of project progress and never miss a due date by using Glean to search through Trello cards.

Filter Trello workspaces, boards, and cards to find the most relevant and up-to-date results using Glean’s powerful search capabilities.
