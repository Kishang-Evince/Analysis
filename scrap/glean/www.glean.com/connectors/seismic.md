### Overview

Seismic helps organizations engage customers, enable teams, and ignite revenue growth. Connecting Seismic with Glean allows Glean to fetch relevant Seismic files. Glean will understand which files a user has access to and will strictly enforce permissions for users, down to the record level at the time of the query, which will ensure that users are not able to see results that they do not have access to.
