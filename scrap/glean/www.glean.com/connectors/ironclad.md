### Overview

Ironclad is a digital contracting and CLM platform for legal and business teams. Connecting Ironclad with Glean lets you search contract records, workflows, and entities, while mirroring Ironclad permissions so users only see content they’re allowed to access.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Workflows
-   Records
-   Entities

### Benefits

Instantly find the right contracts, workflows, and entities, and get deep clause‑level insights—from Ironclad and all your other business applications—so you always have full context on every agreement and counterparty.
