### Overview

Confluence is a collaborative wiki and documentation platform used to create, organize, and share project knowledge. Connecting Confluence to Glean unifies your wiki with the rest of your company knowledge base. Instantly search wiki pages, spaces, and comments and keep your documentation up to date with full context without jumping between tools.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Spaces
-   Pages
-   Blogs
-   Comments

‍

Glean executes actions across:

-   Create and update pages, spaces, and blog posts
-   Add content labels

### Benefits

Answer IT and help desk questions, deflect tickets, and get debugging help by searching and updating your Confluence wiki content directly from Glean.
