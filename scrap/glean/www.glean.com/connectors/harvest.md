### Overview

Harvest makes it easy to track time and expenses, so you get access to critical data without driving yourself (or your team) crazy. The Harvest for Glean connector allows searching and categorizing of time spent on projects.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Time Entries
-   Projects

### Benefits

Get time-tracking insights in context with Glean-ask “How much time did I spend on Project X?” or combine time data with docs and tickets to understand effort for features or projects.
