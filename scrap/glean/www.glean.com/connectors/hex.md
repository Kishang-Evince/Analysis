### Overview

Hex is an AI analytics platform for business intelligence and visualizations. Glean connects to Hex, letting users ask data questions and access Hex charts, tables, and analysis without leaving Glean.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Projects, including titles, descriptions, summaries, and direct links
-   Published and unpublished projects in a user's workspace
-   Thread messages and results from Hex analysis workflows
-   Charts, tables, and visualizations generated in Hex Threads

Glean executes actions across:

-   Create new Hex Threads to explore data in natural language
-   Continue existing Threads with follow-up questions to refine or extend an analysis
-   Generate charts, tables, and visualizations through Hex Threads

### Benefits

Bring Hex analysis into everyday work so teams can ask data questions, visualize results, and move faster on decisions grounded in trusted, up-to-date context.
