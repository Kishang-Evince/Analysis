### Overview

Zoom is a video conferencing platform for meetings, webinars, and conferences. Glean indexes cloud recording transcripts and play links, providing permissions-aware search of past meeting context.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

• Cloud recording transcripts  
• Zoom recording play URLs  
• Meeting participant metadata  
• Recording access settings for permission mapping

### Benefits

Find decisions, status updates, and action items buried in past Zoom meetings. Use that context alongside the rest of your organization's knowledge to prep faster, follow up clearly, and keep work moving.
