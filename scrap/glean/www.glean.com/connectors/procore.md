### Overview

Procore is a construction project management platform that centralizes projects, RFIs, submittals, daily logs, drawings, and specs. Connecting Procore with Glean lets teams search project records and attachments in one place while Glean mirrors Procore’s project‑level permissions so users only see data they’re allowed to access.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Projects
-   RFIs
-   Submittals
-   Daily logs
-   Specifications
-   Drawings

### Benefits

Keep field, project, and back‑office teams aligned with a single, searchable view of current project status and decisions.
