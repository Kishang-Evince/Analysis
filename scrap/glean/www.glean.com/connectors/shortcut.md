### Overview

Glean connects seamlessly with your Shortcut instance to fetch relevant information that feeds directly into Glean’s enterprise search software. Glean’s Shortcut integration understands all user access permissions and strictly enforces them at the time of the query, which ensures that users are not able to see content they do not have access to. Most importantly, all data is stored in a GCP project inside your cloud account, and no data leaves your environment.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Stories
-   Epics

### Benefits

**All of your Shortcut content is organized in one place and easily accessible. Easily search through Shortcut stories to get information on status, type, assignees, and more.**‍

You can see more context around Shortcut content, including how popular it is, who has accessed it, when it was last accessed, etc.
