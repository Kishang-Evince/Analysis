### Overview

Panopto is a comprehensive video content management, recording, and sharing platform used in educational and corporate settings for learning and training. By connecting to Glean, Teams gain permissions-based search across their video content, including editing and distribution status and engagement metrics.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Video library content
-   Editing, streaming, and publishing history
-   Audience analytics

### Benefits

Accelerate video content creation workflows with full visibility across your Panopto account.
