### Overview

SalesHood is a sales enablement platform designed to help sales teams improve their performance, productivity, and effectiveness. Glean connects to SalesHood to enable teams with fast search of all SalesHood sales content, training, and coaching materials.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Digital Sales Rooms and Mutual Action Plans
-   Call summaries and content recommendations
-   Buyer engagement and pipeline metrics

### Benefits

Drive sales team efficiency with comprehensive search of SalesHood's enablement and marketing content.
