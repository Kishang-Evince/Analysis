### Overview

Lessonly helps new salespeople ramp up faster and practice essential skills through a cloud-based learning management system. Users can build, share, and track lesson plans to streamline sales learning and coaching. You can search through lessons and paths in Glean for easier access to learning content.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Lessons
-   Paths

### Benefits

Ramp up faster or refresh your memory by being able to easily search and findLessons and Paths within Glean

Get personalized and relevant search results across Lessonly content, as Glean’s system incorporates department info to provide personalized results for users.
