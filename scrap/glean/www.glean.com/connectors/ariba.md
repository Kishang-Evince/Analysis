### Overview

SAP Ariba delivers a unified view of enterprise spending that helps lower cost and risk while aligning to business goals. By connecting to Glean, team members gain visibility into an organization's spending priorities and contract management across categories.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Supplier profiles
-   Agreements dates
-   Contract details

### Benefits

Enable permissions-based search of enterprise procurement data directly from Glean. Surface insights around suppliers and budget outlays.
