### Overview

Interact provides an internal business communications platform that serves as a gateway to workspaces and other business tools. Connecting to Interact enables Glean users with permissions-aware search across your organization's communications, content, people, and apps.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Webpages, email, and directories
-   Communications and ideas
-   Usage data and analytics

### Benefits

Gain fast and easy visibility into events, trends, and team projects occuring on your Interact intranet. Find onboarding and other human resources content as well as details on workplace application integrations.
