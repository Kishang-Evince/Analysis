### Overview

Docusign is a leading electronic signature and digital transaction management platform. Integrating with Glean enables users to accelerate Docusign workflows with permissions-based search of previous contracts, signatures, and forms.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Past agreements
-   Agreement workflows
-   Agreement analytics

### Benefits

Power document management by providing users with immediate visibility over historical agreement activity from the Docusign platform.
