### Overview

Apollo is a sales intelligence and engagement platform that helps sales teams discover new leads, reach out through email campaigns, and maintain up-to-date contact information. With Glean, you can search for leads in Apollo just by describing your ideal customer profile (ICP) and pull them directly into outbound campaigns.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Discover leads that are relevant to your business
-   Reach out to leads by accessing their contact information
-   Create new contacts and update existing ones too

### Benefits

With Apollo and Glean, sales teams cultivate the right relationships, match every conversation with the content that resonates, and build stronger sales connections because its grounded in context from your CRM activity, emails, threads, and deal history.
