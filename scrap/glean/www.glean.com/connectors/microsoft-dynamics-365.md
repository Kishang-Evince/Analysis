### Overview

Glean’s integration with Microsoft Dynamics 365 speeds your account research and meeting prep with permissions-aware visibility across CRM data and everything else in your company knowledge base. Ask about open opportunities, recent activity, and more, and get structured results grounded in real Dynamics data.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Accounts
-   Contacts
-   Opportunities
-   Leads
-   Activities

### Benefits

Glean unites Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM data with the rest of your enterprise knowledge so you can immediately understand deals and customers in context.
