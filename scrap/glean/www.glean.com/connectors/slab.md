### Overview

Slab enables team members to collectively create, discover, and organize knowledge. Connecting to Slab enables Glean to understand and personalize this knowledge for every user, while maintaining permissions and access control.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Documents and other content
-   Topics and user activity
-   Comments and discussions

### Benefits

Enhance the accessibility and searchability of key organizational knowledge. Accelerate knowledge retrieval and collaborations with Glean's search capabilities.
