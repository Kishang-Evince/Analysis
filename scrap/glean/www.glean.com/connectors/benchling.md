### Overview

The Benchling R&D Cloud is a scalable platform that provides a central source of truth for biotech R&D. By connecting to Glean, team members can quickly search experiments, workflows, and end-to-end results. Glean maintains Benchling's robust permissions controls to ensure compliance and data security.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Research testing notes
-   Experiment status
-   Collaboration updates

### Benefits

Gain visibility into rich scientific data and track progress across your R&D teams. Enhance dissemination of organizational R&D with permissions-based access to research and results.
