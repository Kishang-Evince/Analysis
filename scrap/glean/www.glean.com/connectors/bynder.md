### Overview

Bynder is a digital asset management (DAM) platform designed to help organizations manage, distribute, and optimize their digital content across systems and channels. By connecting to Glean, Bynder users can instantly search for relevant assets and workflows in their enterprise DAM.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Digital asset workflows
-   Assets used in prior projects
-   Project analytics

### Benefits

Enable users to quickly find assets and analytics by searching their historical activity from across Bynder's digital asset management platform.
