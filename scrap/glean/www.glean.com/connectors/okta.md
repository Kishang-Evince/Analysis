### Overview

Okta is a customizable, secure, drop-in solution to add authentication and authorization services to your apps. As Okta is often the canonical source of people data in organizations, Glean connects to your Okta domain to enable search of colleagues, organizational hierarchy, contact information, and more.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Secure Client Credentials flow
-   Validity of OAuth refresh tokens
-   Other authentication data

### Benefits

Find coworkers more easily and surface experts on your team. Securely search for relevant documents with Okta and Glean’s authentication processes.
