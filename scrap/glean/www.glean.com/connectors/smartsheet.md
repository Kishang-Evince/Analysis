### Overview

Smartsheet is a cloud-based work execution platform designed to help businesses and teams manage projects, automate workflows, and collaborate more effectively. By connecting Smartsheet to Glean, team members can quickly search project Smartsheets and other project data to find the information they need, fast.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Sheets
-   Dashboards and reports
-   Workspaces

### Benefits

Elevate workflow productivity and team member effectiveness with comprehensive, permissions-based search of Smartsheet content.
