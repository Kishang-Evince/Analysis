### Overview

Ramp brings corporate cards, expense management, bill pay, and accounting automation into one finance platform. Connecting Ramp to Glean puts that functionality inside everyday workflows — so teams can access finance context and ask spend questions in natural language without a platform switch.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

• Corporate card, expense, and bill pay data from Ramp  
• Budget, vendor, and approval workflow data  
• Spend controls, accounting automation, and finance operations data

### Benefits

Finance operations slow down when spend data lives in a separate tool. Ramp in Glean means expense context, approvals, and vendor data are part of the same workflow as the rest of your company knowledge.
