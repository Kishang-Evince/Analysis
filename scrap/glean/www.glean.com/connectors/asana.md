### Overview

Asana is a work management platform for projects and tasks. Glean’s Asana connector lets users search tasks, comments, and projects alongside all their other work context. And, keep work moving forward by updating projects and tasks right from within Glean.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Increase your team’s productivity by keeping all Asana content easily accessible and straightforward.
-   Filter Asana tasks, comments, and projects to find the most relevant and up-to-date results using Glean’s powerful search capabilities.

Glean executes actions across:

-   Create and update tasks as part of agent workflows
-   Add task comments and move work through sections or statuses

‍

### Benefits

Stay up to date on the latest projects and tasks. As plans change or work gets done, keep Asana as the source of truth for projects without needing to switch apps.
