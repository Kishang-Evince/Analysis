### Overview

Zeplin is a collaboration tool designed for software designers and developers that streamlines the handoff of design assets and specifications. By connecting to Glean, users can search their workflows to quickly find existing assignments and reviews.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Assignments
-   Reviews
-   Approvals

### Benefits

Help streamline design-to-development workflows by enabling teams to search their Zeplin activities.
