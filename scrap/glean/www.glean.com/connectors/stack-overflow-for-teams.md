### Overview

Stack Overflow for Teams is a knowledge management and collaboration solution that helps companies and teams improve their overall efficiency. The Stack Overflow connector in Glean works for both public stackoverflow.com content and private Stack Overflow for Teams or Enterprise edition content. Glean understands all user access permissions and strictly enforces permissions for users at the time of the query, which ensures that users are not able to see results that they do not have access to.
