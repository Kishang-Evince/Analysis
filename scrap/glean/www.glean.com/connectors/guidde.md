### Overview

Guidde simplifies the process of creating and sharing step-by-step guides or tutorials, primarily for software applications and digital processes. By connecting to Glean, users can easily search all their Guidde content, from how-to's and feature notes to onboarding docs, FAQs, and more.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Guidde projects
-   Asset versions and sharing
-   Asset analytics

### Benefits

Accelerate documentation workflows by quickly surfacing the Guidde content you need directly from Glean.
