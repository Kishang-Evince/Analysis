### Overview

Glean's integration with Microsoft 365 brings permissions-aware enterprise context to the surfaces you already use. Search across your company's knowledge, then take action — create Word documents, draft and send Outlook emails, build Excel spreadsheets, and manage calendar events and contacts without leaving your workflow.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Create Word documents
-   Create and update Excel spreadsheets, worksheets, and ranges
-   Send, draft, reply to, and forward Outlook emails
-   Create and update calendar events and contacts

### Benefits

Create Word documents grounded in company knowledge, draft Outlook emails informed by project context, and populate Excel spreadsheets with company data. Glean connects insight to action across Microsoft 365 in a single flow.
