### Overview

Gamma is an AI-powered visual content platform for slides, web pages, and infographics. Glean connects to Gamma through MCP, letting users turn company context into polished presentations, docs, and social content directly from Assistant or agent workflows.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Presentations, documents, webpages, and social posts
-   Themes to match tone and visual style
-   Folders for browsing, searching, and organizing outputs
-   Generation controls like audience, tone, language, text density, layout, and export format

Glean executes actions across:

-   Generate Gamma presentations, documents, or social media content
-   Create Gamma content directly from Assistant and agent workflows
-   Browse themes, search folders, and save content

### Benefits

Turn company context into ready-to-share visual content faster. Go from research to Gamma deliverables in one flow-reducing copy-paste and grounding content in real company knowledge.
