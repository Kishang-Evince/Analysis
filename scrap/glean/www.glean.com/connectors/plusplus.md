### Overview

PlusPlus is a learning platform that combines the structure of a Learning Management System (LMS) with the flexibility of a Learning Experience Platform (LXP).

PlusPlus for Glean connector seamlessly integrates the PlusPlus Learning Platform so that all your enterprise learning content and events surface in Glean federated search.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Tracks: enterprise learning programs and curriculum
-   Events: workshops, classes, and live sessions
-   Courses: eLearning content in SCORM, AICC, and xAPI formats
-   Videos: most common video formats supported
-   Articles: your knowledge base including integration with Confluence and Google Apps
-   Codelabs: eLearning authored in Google Docs or Markdown
-   Full Support for access-restricted items in PlusPlus

PlusPlus also provides API Endpoint offering Glean bulk indexing data so your IT team can build their own connectors should they choose to.

### Benefits

Get personalized and relevant search results across PlusPlus content, as Glean’s system incorporates department info to provide personalized results for users.
