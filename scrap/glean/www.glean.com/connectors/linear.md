### Overview

Linear delivers issue tracking tool for modern product development. In Glean you can keep your product development lifecycle on track by updating Linear issues, projects, and docs based on the latest context.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Issues
-   Projects
-   Documentation

### Benefits

Accelerate triage, planning, and delivery by bringing Linear issues, projects, and docs together with the rest of your enterprise context.
