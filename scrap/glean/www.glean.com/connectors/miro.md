### Overview

Miro is an online collaborative whiteboard platform that enables distributed teams to work effectively together, from brainstorming with digital sticky notes to planning and managing agile workflows. Connecting Miro with Glean allows Glean to fetch relevant Miro content. The customer can create a custom application to perform per-user authentication.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Miro boards from a designated list of teams.
-   Note: Only boards visible to users who have explicitly authorized Glean to index the content of a list of pre-indicated teams will be indexed.

### Benefits

Find relevant Miro boards easily and quickly using Glean’s powerful search capabilities.

Stay up to date with team brainstorming by using Glean to search through content on Miro boards.
