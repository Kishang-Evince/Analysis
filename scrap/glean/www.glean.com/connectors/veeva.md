### Overview

Veeva Vault is the system of record for controlled quality documentation. By connecting it to Glean, teams can search approved quality documents in one place, alongside the broader company context they need to do their work.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Quality documents
-   Metadata such as document type, subtype, classification, and status
-   Additional custom fields that teams can query

### Benefits

Give quality and compliance teams a single, searchable place to find controlled quality content. Improve access to approved procedures and records while preserving permissions and source-of-truth integrity in Veeva Vault.
