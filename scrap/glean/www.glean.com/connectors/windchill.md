### Overview

Windchill is a product lifecycle management platform for engineering and manufacturing teams, covering product data and processes across the full lifecycle. Directly from Glean, engineers can search specifications, practices, and technical reports with full Windchill permissions enforcement.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Windchill document objects
-   Primary document content, including PDF and DOCX files
-   Engineering specs, practices, and technical reports
-   Document metadata such as context, revision, lifecycle state, and more

### Benefits

Find the right engineering standard, product spec, or technical report faster. Search authoritative Windchill documents alongside the rest of your company knowledge-so engineers can design, troubleshoot, and make decisions with confidence.
