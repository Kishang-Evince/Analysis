### Overview

Google Chat is a messaging platform that offers direct messaging, group messaging, and collaborative spaces. With Glean’s integration with Google Chat, users can quickly find and incorporate the latest chat communications into status updates and decision-making, enhancing productivity.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Spaces
-   Messages
-   Attachments

### Benefits

Teams rely on chat communication to discuss perspectives, make decisions, and stay aligned on initiative progress. By integrating powerful search and assistant capabilities across Google Chat and the broader enterprise knowledge base, teams can quickly access full context and find information faster.
