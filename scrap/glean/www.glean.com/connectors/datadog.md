### Overview

Datadog provides an integrated platform for infrastructure monitoring and security. Connecting Datadog to Glean enables users to query their interactions with DataDog health, performance, and security metrics across on-prem and cloud infrastructure, automation and monitoring tools, and databases and other apps.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Log data from services and apps
-   Error rates and latency metrics
-   Visualizations and alerts

### Benefits

Explore historical infrastructure metrics to understand frontend and backend performance and impacts on the business.
