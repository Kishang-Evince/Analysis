### Overview

TestRail is a comprehensive test management tool used by software development and quality assurance teams to manage, track, and organize software testing efforts. Glean connects to TestRail to enhance visibility over testing workflows by enabling teams to quickly search and review previous testing activities.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Test repositories
-   Test versions and results
-   Test approvals

### Benefits

Boost efficiency and speed the testing process by enabling QA teams to quickly search past testing data.
