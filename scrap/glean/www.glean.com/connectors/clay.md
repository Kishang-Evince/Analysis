### Overview

Clay is a go-to-market platform for research, enrichment, and workflow automation. With Clay in Glean, you can get enriched accounts, contacts, and buyer signals alongside the rest of our enterperise context.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   People and company lookups
-   Live research results returned at runtime
-   Outputs surfaced in Assistant and multi-step agent workflows

Glean executes actions across:

-   Access live Clay research for current prospecting context

### Benefits

Bring GTM research alongside the rest of your sales and marketing context so you can prospect smarter, close deals faster, and build lasting relationships with accounts.
