### Overview

Salesloft Conversation Intelligence records, transcribes, and analyzes sales and customer calls to find deal signals and pipeline insights. Glean indexes transcripts, AI-generated summaries, action items, deal moments, and linked CRM context so teams can search revenue conversations within the broader context of their company knowledge.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

• Call transcripts and full conversation text  
• Participants, host/organizer, date, duration, and call type• Summaries, action items, and next steps from Salesloft Conversation Intelligence  
• Deal moments, signals, and call topics from Salesloft Conversation Intelligence

### Benefits

Locate the right sales calls faster, understand what changed in an account, and bring transcript-backed deal context into prep, coaching, QBRs, and follow-up. Salesloft Conversation Intelligence in Glean connects conversation intelligence with CRM, docs, and team knowledge.
