### Overview

Affinity is a relationship‑intelligence CRM for PE, VC, and other dealmaking teams. Connecting Affinity with Glean lets you search contacts, companies, opportunities, and lists alongside the rest of your knowledge base, with Affinity permissions fully enforced.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Contacts
-   Companies
-   Opportunities
-   Lists

### Benefits

Get a 360° view of deals and relationships so you can prioritize the right opportunities and close more sales, faster.
