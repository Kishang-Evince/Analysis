### Overview

AI-powered on-call and incident response. Trusted by leading companies like NVIDIA, Squarespace, Canva, Figma, and more. The Rootly connector lets Glean users quickly access incidents, alerts, reports, schedules, and escalation policies, enabling faster resolution, trend discovery, and easier reporting.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Search Incidents & Retros: Instantly surface Rootly timelines, RCAs, SEVs, and other response data directly in Glean.
-   Track Action Items: Expose Rootly postmortem tasks and follow-ups across systems for better accountability.

### Benefits

Bring Rootly’s incident data into Glean search for faster resolution, trend insights, and reliable tracking – all from within one interface.
