### Overview

InVision is a digital product design platform that helps teams create, prototype, and collaborate on user interface (UI) and user experience (UX) designs. By connecting to Glean, users can surface past projects to find the planning, ideation, presentations, and reviews they need to drive current deliverables.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Projects
-   Ideation and iteration
-   Presentations and reviews

### Benefits

Empower design teams with the ability to quickly search previous interactions with the InVision app.
