### Overview

ScreenSteps is a knowledge ops platform that enables teams to train and empower every user to act and feel like an expert, regardless of their experience, background, or tenure. Connecting ScreenSteps with Glean allows your end-users to easily find and follow your knowledge resources where they are working.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Create and control knowledge articles and workflows in ScreenSteps and surface to Glean for enterprise search.
-   Real-time sync of content updates and exclusions.
-   Centralize user search to a single source of truth for streamlined knowledge access.

### Benefits

Simplify your knowledge operations by consolidating all knowledge resources into one searchable source, streamling training and operations.
