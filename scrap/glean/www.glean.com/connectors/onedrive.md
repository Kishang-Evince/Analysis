### Overview

Microsoft OneDrive lets you store and protect your files, share your files with others, and get to your files from anywhere on all your devices. Connecting OneDrive with Glean enables Glean to fetch relevant OneDrive content.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Folders
-   Documents (All document types, including Word, Excel, and PowerPoint)

### Benefits

Get Microsoft OneDrive content that is relevant and up-to-date.

Get more context around folders and documents, like date of publishing or last modification and author or owner name, in one place.
