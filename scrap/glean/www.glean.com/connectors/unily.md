### Overview

Unily is an industry-leading employee experience platform that unites and engages the entire organization. By connecting to Glean, team members have permissions-based access to all internal and external communications, employee engagement, HR guidelines, and more.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Internal communications
-   Employee and customer portals
-   Knowledge base content

### Benefits

Keep employees and customers engaged by giving them instant insight into company communications, knowledge, and culture.
