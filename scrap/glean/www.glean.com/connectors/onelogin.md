### Overview

OneLogin delivers cloud-based identity and access management (IAM) for the modern enterprise. By integrating with OneLogin, Glean users can securely search workforce and customer permissions and access data.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   User and group information
-   Identity management and application access
-   Security and compliance data

### Benefits

Enhance Glean's security and access control by enabling comprehensive visibility and accurate permissions enforcement.
