### Overview

Amplitude is a product analytics platform for understanding user behavior, experiments, and feedback. Connecting Amplitude MCP to Glean lets you analyze charts, dashboards, metrics, experiments, and feedback, while honoring your existing Amplitude permissions.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Search Amplitude charts, dashboards, and notebooks
-   Query product metrics, segments, and saved analyses
-   Explore cohorts, experiments, and feature flags

### Benefits

Give every teammate conversational access to Amplitude—ask natural-language questions about usage, retention, experiments, and feedback, then see charts and context alongside docs, tickets, and messages in Glean.
