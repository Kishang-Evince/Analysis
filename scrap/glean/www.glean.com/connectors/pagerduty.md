### Overview

PagerDuty empowers developers, DevOps, IT operations, and business leaders to prevent and resolve business-impacting incidents.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   Escalation Policies
-   Schedules

Glean executes actions across:

-   Manage incidents
-   Manage incident team members

### Benefits

Use PagerDuty data as part of full incident context so you can escalate fast, directly from within Glean.
