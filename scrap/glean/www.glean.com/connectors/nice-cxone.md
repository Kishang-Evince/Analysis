### Overview

Nice CXone, formerly Mindtouch, delivers smart knowledge management to improve the experience of customers and internal teams. By connecting Glean to Nice CXone, users gain permissions-based search of the entire enterprise knowledge base.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   All documents in the knowledge base
-   Document activity

### Benefits

Enable users to search product, service, and knowledge content your organization hosts on the Nice CXone platform directly in Glean.
