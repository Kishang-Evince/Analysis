### Overview

Loopio is a cloud-based RFP response solution that streamlines and automates the process of responding to RFPs, RFIs, and security questionnaires. By connecting to Glean, verified users can easily locate the requests and responses they need to accelerate the sales process.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Requests received
-   Responses sent
-   Dashboards and messages

### Benefits

Enable sales teams to move faster and win more deals with efficient search of Loopio's request and response platform directly from Glean.
