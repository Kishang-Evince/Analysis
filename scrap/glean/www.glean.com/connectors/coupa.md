### Overview

Coupa is a business spend platform that enables a data-driven approach to total spend management. By connecting to Glean, team members gain permissions-based access to organizational spending data and insights.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Supplier profiles
-   Agreements dates
-   Contract details

### Benefits

Provide approved team members with up to date information on sourcing, contracts, and expense management.
