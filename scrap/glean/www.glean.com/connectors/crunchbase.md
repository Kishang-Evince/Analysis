### Overview

Crunchbase tracks funding rounds, investors, acquisitions, and growth signals across millions of private companies. The Crunchbase MCP connector brings that trusted private-market intelligence into Glean, so teams can query funding data, company profiles, and market signals alongside the internal knowledge that gives it context.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

• Company profiles, business descriptions, and firmographics  
• Funding rounds, investors, and acquisition history  
• Growth signals and private-market activity  
• Company and market intelligence

### Benefits

Private-company data is only useful when it's connected to what you already know about an account. Crunchbase in Glean makes that connection automatic.
