### Overview

OneNote is a digital notebook in Microsoft 365 for capturing meeting notes, project plans, and personal ideas. Connecting OneNote with Glean lets you securely search notebooks, sections, and pages, so users can search their notes alongside the rest of your enterprise content while Glean enforces existing M365 permissions.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Notebooks
-   Sections
-   Pages

### Benefits

Quickly find the notes you’ve taken in OneNote and turn them into organized, prioritized to‑dos in Glean—so you always know what to work on next.
