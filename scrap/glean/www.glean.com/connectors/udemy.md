### Overview

Udemy Business is an online learning platform with thousands of expert‑led courses for employee upskilling. Connecting Udemy with Glean lets you find the right courses, labs, and learning paths.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Surface relevant Udemy courses directly in Glean Search and Assistant
-   Power onboarding, upskilling, and “everboarding” agents with Udemy content
-   Jump from Glean into Udemy to start learning in a single click

### Benefits

Give every employee a personalized learning coach that blends Udemy’s depth with Glean’s enterprise context, so onboarding, role changes, and skill growth all happen faster.
