### Overview

Canva is an online graphic design and content creation platform for presentations, documents, and visual assets. Connecting Canva to Glean lets teams search and reuse designs alongside all their work, so new designs start with full enterprise context.

### Features

Glean understands and retrieves:

-   design documents

‍

Glean executes actions across:

-   Generate and create designs
-   Organize designs, folders and assets
-   Give feedback on designs

‍

### Benefits

Search across Canva design docs in Glean, then use AI to spin up new on‑brand designs, organize them in folders, and provide feedback right from Glean.
