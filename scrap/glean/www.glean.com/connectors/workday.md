### Overview

Workday provides companies with cloud-based finance, HR, planning, and analysis tools. Integrating with Glean enables employees to retrieve relevant data from their Workday accounts. Glean understands user access permissions and rigorously enforces them during queries, ensuring users only see results they are authorized to access.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   HR org charts and people data
-   Knowledge base articles
-   Apply for time-off

### Benefits

Give users quick and easy access to relevant Workday finance and HR information. Enable team members to stay focused on primary tasks.
