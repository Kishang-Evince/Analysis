### Overview

Simpplr software helps companies connect members of their workforce by streamlining internal communications and forging employee connections. Glean will understand the Simpplr site membership model and site privacy levels (Public, Private, and Unlisted). For Private Sites and Unlisted Sites, Glean will understand sets of authorized members.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Sites
-   Pages (underlying Sites) Over time, Glean will support Events, Files, and Posts

### Benefits

Make employee communications more effective by helping people find relevant sites and pages.
