### Overview

The eSM (e-SalesManager) Connector streamlines indexing opportunity, activity, and customer data into Glean, helping companies improve efficiency, data visibility, and collaboration across their entire sales process.

‍

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

\* Sales opportunities: status, contact person, sales products and other information about each case

\* Sales activities: activity history of all employees involved for each case

\* Customer: company information and sales history

### Benefits

Give sellers a single, AI-powered place to instantly search opportunities, activities, and accounts, so they spend less time clicking through CRM screens and more time having timely, well-informed customer conversations that drive higher win rates and faster deal cycles.
