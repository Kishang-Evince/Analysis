### Overview

OneTrust provides enterprises with a platform to evaluate and mitigate risk and enforce organizational security controls. Connecting to Glean enables teams with permissions-based search of risk assessments and security protocols across the organization.

### Features

Glean retrieves context and executes actions across:

-   Risk assessments
-   Security and governance controls
-   Consent and preferences

### Benefits

Provide clear visibility into data security, governance, and consent requirements, as well as compliance and risk-reduction policies.
