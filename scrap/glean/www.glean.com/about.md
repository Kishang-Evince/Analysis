Our Journey

## From workplace search to enterprise AI.

Glean began with a simple problem: people couldn’t find the knowledge they needed to do their jobs. We started with enterprise search and grew into an enterprise AI platform built on company context.

[

See the full timeline

](/about/glean-story)

Glean is founded

The team begins building enterprise search in stealth.

Enterprise search

Glean launches publicly with enterprise search already in use at 40+ companies.

Glean becomes a unicorn

Glean raises $100M at a $1B valuation less than a year after its public launch.

Assistant

Glean brings generative AI to enterprise search and launches a conversational Assistant.

Enterprise AI platform

Glean brings enterprise context, models, and systems together in a secure, open platform.

Agents

Glean launches Agents to complete work across systems and reaches a $7.2B valuation.

Enterprise AI coworkers

Glean introduces AI coworkers that proactively take on work across enterprise tools.
