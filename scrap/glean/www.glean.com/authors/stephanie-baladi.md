## About

## Stephanie Baladi

Stephanie Baladi is a Content Marketing Manager at Glean, based in the San Francisco Bay Area. She brings more than 8 years of experience in content marketing, with a focus on B2B technology and enterprise AI. Her work spans product and technical storytelling, industry narratives, and thought leadership about how AI is changing work. Before joining Glean, Stephanie spent nearly three years at Moveworks, working across web, integrated campaigns, and executive thought leadership. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Sociology from Northeastern University.
