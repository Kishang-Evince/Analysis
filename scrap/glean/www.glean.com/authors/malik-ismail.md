## About

## Malik Ismail

Malik Ismail is Head of Applied AI at Glean, where they lead forward-deployed, AI outcomes, and AI success teams to build and launch transformative 0-to-1 products for the world’s largest businesses. Their career spans engineering, strategy, consulting, venture investing, and product leadership, with experience at Glean, X-the Moonshot Factory, Emerson Collective, Boston Consulting Group, Cisco, and Hydro One. Malik brings a distinctive blend of AI, robotics, growth strategy, product development, customer discovery, partnerships, and executive communication, including sourcing an investment in Field AI that later reached a $2 billion valuation and helping launch Hack the Globe, Canada’s first social-impact hackathon. They hold an MBA and an MS in Environment and Resources from Stanford University, along with an Honors B.A.Sc. in Engineering Science from the University of Toronto.
