## About

## Miribel Wu

Miribel Wu is a product leader with 13+ years of experience building zero-to-one products at the intersection of AI, analytics, and customer experience, and currently works on Glean’s Core AI team, focusing on assistant quality, personalization, memory, and skills. Before joining Glean, they led product strategy and roadmap for Analytics & Platform at Apollo.io, one of the company’s core growth and monetization pillars, including the 0-to-1 vision for a structured data analytics assistant that let go-to-market teams query sales data in natural language without SQL, dashboards, or analysts. Previously, Miribel built Calm’s B2B and healthcare product suite from the ground up, serving as interim Head of Product for Calm Business (later Calm Health), and was the first PM for analytics and integrations at Common Room, where they led the B2B analytics product from 0 to 1 and through GA launch. Earlier in their career, they drove international growth and device experiences at Disney Streaming for Disney+ and Hulu-owning launches across key APAC markets and building personalized content feeds and voice experiences-and designed AI-powered healthcare tech during a product fellowship at IDEO. Miribel’s work is grounded in a deep research background, including award‑winning clinical research in cognitive performance and psychiatric diagnosis, and a Stanford University degree in Science, Technology & Society and Symbolic Systems, where they were a Gates Millennium Scholar and founded a student organization to accelerate impact in healthcare and technology.
