## About

## Arun Kumar

Arun Kumar is a product leader building the future of work at Glean, where he focuses on creating products that help people work smarter. Prior to Glean, he held product and growth roles at Eightfold, Google, simsim, and Ola, leading cross-functional work across product strategy, analytics, GTM, and team leadership. At Google, he led the creator vertical for simsim and YouTube Shopping, helping improve weekly creator retention from 35% to 90%+, grow the active creator base 10x organically, reduce cost per content by 60%, and increase order conversions by 8%. Earlier in his career, he helped drive major operational improvements at Ola through incentive redesign, product innovation, and driver-focused problem solving. Arun holds a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from IIT BHU and brings a strong mix of analytical rigor, growth thinking, and people-first leadership to his work.
