## About

## Nikhhar Gupta

Nikhar Gupta is an SEO and organic growth leader with a track record of helping SaaS companies turn search into a durable growth engine. With more than five years of experience across SEO, content strategy, technical SEO, AEO, international SEO, and content-led growth, he has built scalable programs that improve visibility, strengthen authority, and drive meaningful business impact. Before joining Glean as SEO Manager, he led organic growth initiatives at Darwinbox, where he helped grow overall organic traffic by 15% and accelerated regional growth across SEA and MENA, while also building frameworks for AI-driven search visibility. At ZenTrades.ai, he drove more than 300% traffic growth, improved domain authority, and led a major site migration with 95% authority and traffic retention. A Computer Science graduate from Manipal University Jaipur, Nikhar brings a systems-minded, performance-driven approach to modern marketing-blending strategy, analytics, and execution to create programs that scale.
