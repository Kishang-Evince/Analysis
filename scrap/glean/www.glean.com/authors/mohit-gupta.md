## About

## Mohit Gupta

Mohit Gupta is a seasoned software engineer with over 12 years of experience building high-impact systems across ads, search, and large-scale cloud infrastructure at companies like Google, Microsoft, and now Glean. At Google, they led and contributed to products spanning YouTube Ads performance measurement and optimization, Search Ads language quality, and Search Ads advertiser intelligence, giving them a deep understanding of data-driven experimentation, measurement, and how businesses grow through better advertising outcomes. Prior to that, Mohit worked on Azure networking and developer experience at Microsoft, strengthening their expertise in scalable distributed systems and developer-centric tooling. Currently, as a Software Engineer at Glean, they apply this blend of ads, cloud, and NLP experience to build Work AI products that help enterprises find, understand, and act on their data. Mohit holds a B.Tech. in Computer Science from IIT Bombay, where they earned the Institute Academic Prize and explored early interests in sentiment analysis and information visualization through projects and a publication on adjective intensity and fine-grained sentiment analysis-experience that now informs their approach to language quality and user-centric product design.
