## About

## Rebecca Hinds

Rebecca Hinds, is an organizational behavior expert and leading authority on the future of work who blends rigorous academic research with real-world impact across AI, collaboration, and workplace transformation. As Head of the Work AI Institute at Glean, which she founded in 2025, she leads cutting-edge, data-driven research on how AI is reshaping organizations and delivers actionable frameworks that help leaders turn AI investment into meaningful business results. A longtime thought leader in content and research-driven marketing, Rebecca previously founded and led Asana’s Work Innovation Lab and Thought Leadership program, where her insights on work and collaboration consistently appeared in top outlets like Harvard Business Review, The New York Times, Forbes, and The Wall Street Journal. She is the bestselling author of “Your Best Meeting Ever” (Simon & Schuster), a columnist at Inc. and Reworked, and a sought-after keynote speaker represented by United Talent Agency, frequently addressing global audiences on AI at work, fixing broken meetings, and designing smarter hybrid and remote workplaces. Her work has helped dozens of organizations across industries reduce meeting dysfunction, improve collaboration, and harness AI without sparking employee backlash. Rebecca holds a BS, MS, and PhD in Management Science and Engineering from Stanford University, where her award-winning research on technology, AI, and the future of work earned the prestigious Stanford Interdisciplinary Graduate Fellowship and multiple honors for academic distinction and leadership.
