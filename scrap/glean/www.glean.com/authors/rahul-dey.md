## About

## Rahul Dey

Rahul Dey is a Product Manager at Glean, bringing a dynamic background in building and scaling technology products across healthcare, enterprise collaboration, and media security. With experience deploying AI voice assistants in major healthcare systems, leading integrations to unlock data silos, and developing digital tools for over 40 million frontline workers at Microsoft Teams, Rahul has consistently delivered impactful solutions at scale. Their work at Microsoft also included protecting streaming content for providers like Netflix and Disney on billions of devices and advancing deep fake detection technology. Rahul holds a Master of Engineering and a Bachelor’s in Computer Science and Economics from Brown University, and is certified in Agile Foundations. Their journey reflects a passion for leveraging technology to solve complex problems and drive meaningful change.
