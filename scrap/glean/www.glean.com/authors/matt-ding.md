## About

## Matt Ding

Matthew Ding is a Technical Product Marketing Manager at Glean, where he helps shape how Glean’s enterprise AI and search products are positioned, explained, and adopted by customers. In this role, Matthew focuses on translating complex product capabilities such as AI assistants, enterprise search, and agent frameworks into clear value stories and practical guidance that resonate with builders, developers, and business users alike. His work supports Glean’s mission of helping organizations unlock and operationalize knowledge across disparate systems through advanced AI and unified search technology.
