## About

## Megha Jhunjhunwala

Megha Jhunjhunwala works on the software engineering team at Glean. She joined Glean in 2022 and focuses on building secure and effective AI products tailored for enterprise use. Megha has a background in developing AI/ML products with previous experience at Meta and Microsoft. She graduated from Stanford’s prestigious AI/ML program with specialized expertise in natural language processing and deep learning. As part of Stanford's Robotics Lab, she worked on building autonomous robotic agents for military use cases. Megha earlier obtained a B.Tech in Computer Science from IIT Kharagpur.
