## About

## Melissa Albanes

Melissa Albanes is a product marketing and growth leader with 13+ years of experience helping software companies turn complex products into clear value, from early roles in product marketing and product management to consulting engagements with Google, DocuSign, Databricks, IDC, and Sectigo, and now as Co-Founder and COO of Valyr, where she helps tech leaders drive long-term business value through custom web and software solutions. Across her career, she has led go-to-market strategy, customer advocacy, market research, messaging, thought leadership, sales enablement, and demand-driving content programs, with results that include more than doubling MQLs and sales for a newly acquired solution at SumTotal, increasing utilization of vertical marketing assets by 30% for Google, cutting customer acquisition costs while boosting engagement and lead generation at Sectigo, and helping scale customer stories, reviews, analyst input, and keynote speaker programs at DocuSign. Known for pairing strategic rigor with practical execution, she also brings a rare blend of marketing and technical fluency, shaped by experience in presales and solutions architecture, where she earned SumTotal’s global Rookie of the Year award. Melissa holds a B.S. in Business Marketing from Geneva College and an Inbound Certification from HubSpot, grounding her hands-on leadership style in both formal marketing education and modern growth practices.
