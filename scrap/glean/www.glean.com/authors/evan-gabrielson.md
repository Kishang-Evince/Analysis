## About

## Evan Gabrielson

Evan Gabrielson is a software engineer at Glean, where he helps turn ambitious ideas into practical Work AI experiences that are useful for enterprise teams. His background spans startups, enterprise software, and open source contributions, giving him a broad perspective on how products earn trust, scale with customers, and solve everyday problems. Before joining Glean, Evan co-founded a climate tech company, and at the University of Southern California he combined research and graduate study across machine learning, data science, and algorithms, including work at the USC Institute for Technology and Medical Systems. Evan holds a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering and an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering with a focus in Machine Learning and Data Science from USC.
