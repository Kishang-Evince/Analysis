## About

## David Hamilton

David Hamilton is a seasoned software engineer and technical leader who brings deep experience building developer platforms and AI-powered workflows to their role at Glean, where they work on the Context Platform that underpins Glean’s Work AI capabilities. With a strong engineering background showcased through thousands of open-source contributions on GitHub, David focuses on creating reliable, extensible systems that help developers and knowledge workers tap into rich enterprise context and turn it into meaningful outcomes across code, content, and collaboration tools. Colleagues describe David as mature, dependable, and highly skilled-someone who consistently “gets the job done” while continuously learning and elevating the teams around them. At Glean, they play an active role in launching and championing new capabilities-from context-aware desktop experiences to deeper integrations and MCP-based orchestration-that help customers put their company’s knowledge to work in practical, high-impact ways. Fluent in both English and Swedish, David brings a thoughtful, pragmatic approach to modern engineering, blending craftsmanship with a strong bias toward real-world impact and scalable, developer-friendly experiences.
