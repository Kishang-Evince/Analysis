## About

## Harsh Singhal

Harsh is an industry-recognized leader in Machine Learning, Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence with 16+ years of experience across LinkedIn, Netflix, and Palo Alto Networks, now contributing to AI and Data Governance at Glean. Harsh builds and scales high‑performance teams (grew Koo’s ML org from 3 to 20) and delivers impact across NLP, Computer Vision, and MLOps (Python, PyTorch, Keras, Spark; MLflow, BentoML, ONNX, Milvus) with deep cloud expertise on AWS, GCP, and Azure. Harsh’s work spans safety, fraud, and growth-bot and spam detection, recommendations, and account‑takeover prevention-supported by patents and publications (including IEEE SMC). A builder and educator, Harsh founded Dafinchi.ai and MoleculeSearch.ai, publishes at datascience.fm (~5k monthly users), and actively mentors the next generation of AI talent.
