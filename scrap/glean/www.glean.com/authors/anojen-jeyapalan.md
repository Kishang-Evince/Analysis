## About

## Anojen Jeyapalan

Anojen Jeyapalan is a Product Designer at Glean based in the San Francisco Bay Area, with a strong background in user experience, product design, and product management. He brings diverse experience from leading technology companies including Okta, theScore, Bark.com, and Microsoft, as well as cross-functional roles spanning design, growth, and product strategy. With a Bachelor of Mathematics from the University of Waterloo, Anojen combines analytical thinking with user-centered design to build impactful, intuitive products. He is recognized for his leadership and mentorship contributions and is passionate about creating meaningful product experiences that balance business goals with user needs.
