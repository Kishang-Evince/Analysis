## About

## Allan Livingston

Allan Livingston is a seasoned product leader with over 12 years of experience building and scaling products used by millions, including leading Chrome OS and G Suite product management at Google, where they grew a PM organization from roughly 8 to 35 product managers supporting a 350+ person engineering team. Known for combining deep technical expertise with a calm, collaborative leadership style, Allan brings a rare mix of enterprise, infrastructure, and public-facing product experience to every challenge. They are recognized by colleagues and leaders as a trusted partner who leads with integrity, humility, and clear product vision, consistently driving impact across complex, fast-moving environments. Educated at the University of Waterloo and the University of Oxford, Allan now helps shape the future of AI-powered work, drawing on their background in building platforms that make organizations more productive and secure at scale.
