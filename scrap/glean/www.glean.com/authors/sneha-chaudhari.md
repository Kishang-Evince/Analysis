## About

## Sneha Chaudhari

Sneha Chaudhari is an accomplished AI/ML leader at Glean, bringing a wealth of experience from her previous roles as Tech Lead & Engineering Manager and Senior Machine Learning Engineer at LinkedIn, as well as a Software Engineer at IBM Research Lab. With a strong foundation in machine learning, data mining, and unsupervised learning, Sneha has earned recognition such as the Best Paper Award for innovative work in sensor deployment and is a James R. Swartz Entrepreneurial Fellow. She holds multiple patents in areas like customer interaction, content tagging, and online learning propensity modeling, underscoring her expertise in leveraging advanced analytics for impactful solutions. Sneha’s academic background includes degrees in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University and the Indian Institute of Science, further supporting her deep technical and leadership skills that drive Glean’s AI initiatives.
