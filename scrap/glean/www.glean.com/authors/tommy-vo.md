## About

## Tommy Vo

Tommy Vo is a Software Engineer at Glean, based in San Francisco, California. They joined Glean in 2022 and contribute to various product features, including chat and the home page. Previously, Nghia worked at Slack and Quip, with significant experience in accessibility and interface enhancements. They hold a Master’s and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Michigan. Outside of work, you can find Tommy snacking and climbing at local crags.
