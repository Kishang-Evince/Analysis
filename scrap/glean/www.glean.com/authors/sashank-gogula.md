## About

## Sashank Gogula

Sashank Gogula is a software engineer at Glean, where he builds intelligent, user-centric systems that make workplace knowledge easy to discover. Before Glean, he worked at Microsoft and Concentric AI, developing scalable and AI-powered products. He has a strong research background from Carnegie Mellon University’s Language Technologies Institute, with a focus on NLP and human-computer interaction. Sashank earned his degree in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University. He is passionate about applying AI and thoughtful engineering to create products people genuinely enjoy using.
