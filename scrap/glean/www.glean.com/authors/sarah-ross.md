## About

## Sarah Ross

Sarah R is a Software Engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area, currently working at Glean. She has extensive experience across senior and staff engineering roles at companies like Ringy, Affirm, NerdWallet, Intuit, Tumblr, and Yahoo. Her background spans full-stack and backend development, enterprise integrations, and large-scale product engineering. She holds a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, Santa Cruz, and has been actively involved in leadership and mentorship roles through the Society of Women Engineers. She is recognized with multiple awards and certifications, including Google Cybersecurity and AWS Certified AI Practitioner, reflecting strong technical and leadership skills.
