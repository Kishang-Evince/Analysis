## About

## Michael Moore

Michael Moore is a seasoned legal executive with over 20 years of experience leading legal, privacy, intellectual property, and regulatory functions across technology and AI-focused organizations. Currently the VP of Legal at Glean, Michael has a demonstrated track record in product, commercial, open source, and privacy law. He has recently led AI and IP strategy at IQVIA, a global healthcare data analytics leader, and previously served as Chief Privacy Officer and Vice President of Privacy and IP at Lacework, where he steered privacy programs through major acquisition and integration efforts. Michael’s earlier roles include executive and senior in-house counsel positions at Pure Storage (Vice President, Deputy General Counsel of Privacy, Sales Compliance, Product, and IP), Rambus (Vice President, Intellectual Property & Deputy General Counsel), and other tech innovators. Certified across multiple IAPP privacy credentials (CIPP-US, CIPP-E, CIPP-C, CIPM, CIPT), Michael is recognized in IAM300 and has received awards from the Association of Corporate Counsel and The Recorder. He holds a JD from Santa Clara University School of Law and an MBA in Finance from Santa Clara University. Known for practical legal advice, leadership, and mentorship, Michael is highly recommended by former colleagues for his strategic vision and ability to connect legal strategy with business growth.
