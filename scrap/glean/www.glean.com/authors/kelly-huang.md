## About

## Kelly Huang

Kelly Tk Huang is a product marketer with 8 years of experience spanning AI, analytics, cybersecurity, and growth marketing, with a track record of turning complex technical products into clear go-to-market stories that resonate with customers. Now on Glean’s Product Marketing team focused on AI Agents, they bring a cross-functional background shaped by product marketing roles at Snowflake, Splunk, and BitTorrent, plus earlier growth and digital marketing experience at YouTube TV, Blind, and Slickdeals. At Snowflake, Kelly led go-to-market efforts for both analytics and cybersecurity workloads and shared that they helped build and scale the company’s cybersecurity business to $100M ARR, demonstrating strength in launches, pipeline-oriented execution, and category-building in fast-moving enterprise markets. Their profile highlights expertise across product marketing, go-to-market strategy, campaign management, digital marketing, user acquisition, and AI, backed by recommendations praising their ability to synthesize customer and product insights into high-impact plans and materials that rally teams across disciplines. Kelly holds a bachelor’s degree in Psychology from UCLA and has completed LinkedIn certifications in Advanced Product Marketing, Product Marketing Foundations, and Strategic Thinking.
