## About

## Marisa Huff

Marisa Huff is the Head of Core Product Marketing at Glean, where they lead the team responsible for shaping the core experiences across Glean Assistant, Agents, Search, mobile, and embedded apps. With a proven track record at both Glean and Dropbox, Marisa has driven company-wide launches, developed compelling product narratives, and steered strategic decisions through data-driven insights. Their expertise spans product strategy, go-to-market planning, positioning, messaging, and competitive intelligence, honed through leadership roles managing teams and launching innovative features. Marisa’s earlier experience at Oracle included managing enterprise sales teams and achieving significant revenue growth. They hold an MBA from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business and graduated magna cum laude from UCLA, bringing a blend of analytical rigor and creative vision to every marketing challenge.
