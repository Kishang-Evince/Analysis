## About

## Sunil Agrawal

Sunil Agrawal is the Chief Information Security Officer. Sunil is responsible for the overall security of the Glean’s product, infrastructure, and corporate environment. With over 20 years of experience in the field of security, Sunil has a proven track record of driving growth and innovation. Sunil previously served as Bolt's Head of Security and held security leadership roles at Nutanix, Netflix, Adobe, and more. He also was the founder and CTO of Armor5, a Zero Touch Cloud Security company acquired by Digital Guardian. He has experience with both sides of security, conceptualizing and delivering innovative and high-quality security solutions – including Emmy-award-winning solutions for technology. Sunil holds 40+ patents in different areas of security and is well-versed in Cloud/Mobile Security, LLMs/Generative AI, IAM, Video/Document DRM, and PKI. He earned a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Computer Engineering from Gujarat University and an MS in Computer Science from Iowa State University.
