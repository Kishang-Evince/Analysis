## About

## Rob Stets

Rob Stets is a Software Engineer at Glean. He has over 30 years of professional software engineering experience. Prior to joining Glean, Rob worked at Google for 20 years, where he made significant contributions to their large scale search infrastructure, and prior to Google, he held a research position at Compaq Western Research Lab. Rob holds a Ph.D. and a Masters degree in Computer Science from the University of Rochester and a Bachelor of Science and Engineering in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Duke University. His expertise spans distributed systems, code and API design, and product engineering. At Glean, Rob leverages his deep technical background and industry insights to drive impactful engineering solutions, helping teams solve complex problems at scale.
