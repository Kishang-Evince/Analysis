## About

## Kathleen Qin

Kathleen Qin is a data-driven product leader with nine years of experience spanning enterprise software, AI, cloud security, analytics, and user experience. As Product Manager at Glean, she helps shape products that make enterprise knowledge and AI more accessible and actionable. Previously, she led enterprise and Copilot initiatives at GitHub and drove product strategy, customer engagement, retention, and onboarding experiences for Prisma Cloud at Palo Alto Networks. Her foundation in data analysis and business intelligence at UC Berkeley-including building Tableau dashboards, identifying business trends with Python and Excel, and leading cross-functional process-improvement projects-continues to inform her customer-focused, analytical approach. Kathleen complements her product expertise with certifications in AI for Product Management, Enterprise Design Thinking, UI/UX Design Patterns, Pendo Essentials, Tableau, advanced SQL, and AWS Cloud Practitioner.
