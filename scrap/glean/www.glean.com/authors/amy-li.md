## About

## Amy Li

Amy Li is a software engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area with more than seven years of experience building data, ML, and platform systems across high-growth technology companies, and she currently works on the Platforms Team at Glean. Her background spans engineering roles at Nuro, Robinhood, Facebook, Microsoft, and Expedia, where she worked on everything from machine learning infrastructure and large-scale data pipelines to product design specs, dashboards, A/B testing, conversion, and growth-related systems. Notable accomplishments include reducing ML storage size and costs by about 80% at Nuro, improving predicted collisions by 20–50% through a simulator visualization feature, and increasing document recommendation accuracy from roughly 80% to 90% at Facebook. While her profile shows only limited direct marketing experience, it does highlight exposure to growth metrics, competitor trend dashboards, marketing optimization strategy at Robinhood, and conversion-focused experimentation at Expedia. She holds a B.S. in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University and brings a research-driven, analytical approach shaped by work in NLP, human-computer interaction, and AI at Carnegie Mellon’s School of Computer Science.
