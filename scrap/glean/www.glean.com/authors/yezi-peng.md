## About

## Yezi Peng

Yezi Peng is Glean’s VP of Strategy & Operations, and she brings nearly 13 years of experience spanning go-to-market strategy, revenue operations, marketing, and business operations across high-growth technology companies. Before joining Glean, she led GTM and revenue operations at Pendo and held several senior leadership roles at Productiv, including VP of Business Strategy & Operations and Interim Head of Marketing. Her marketing achievements include scaling Productiv’s marketing team from 2 to 10 people, building demand generation, product marketing, and content marketing functions, and leading major brand, website, and messaging rebuilds alongside two product launches. Earlier in her career, she also led global GTM initiatives at LinkedIn and built a strong analytical foundation through roles in private equity and M&A, giving her a sharp commercial lens on growth and strategy. She holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and dual degrees in Business Administration and Neuroscience from the University of Michigan.
