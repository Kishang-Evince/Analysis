## About

## Pradeep Vaghela

Pradeep Vaghela is an accomplished software engineer with over 15 years of hands-on experience in applied research, product development, and large-scale system design. Currently an engineer at Glean, Pradeep previously held a senior engineering position at Google, where he played a pivotal role in building and scaling the company’s Knowledge Graph Indexing and Search Content Platforms. His expertise spans distributed systems, machine learning, data mining, computer vision, and enterprise search. Pradeep holds a Master’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Southern California. His journey includes crafting high-performance software and leading cross-functional teams at organizations like Read-Ink Technologies and Yahoo!, showcasing his strengths in software architecture, big data, and algorithmic problem-solving. A passionate technologist and mentor, Pradeep excels at the intersection of research and scalable engineering. His diverse programming background covers C, C++, Java, C#, Python, web technologies, cloud computing, and advanced data structures. Throughout his career, he has driven several transformative projects that impacted millions of users worldwide.
