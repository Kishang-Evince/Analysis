## About

## Jonas Taylor

Jonas Taylor is a sales enablement leader who helps growth-stage SaaS teams scale with data-driven onboarding and training. As Sr. Manager, Global Sales Enablement at Sigma Computing, Jonas designs programs that lift quota attainment and shorten ramp, and previously led global initiatives at Lattice and Algolia. Recognized as a ’24 SEC Enablement Rising Star Finalist and a ’23 SES Enablement Legend, Jonas champions co-creation, outcome-focused execution, and continuous learning. They hold a B.A. from the University of Georgia and certifications including Databricks Lakehouse Fundamentals and Salesforce Trailhead Superbadges, and actively mentor GTM teams.
