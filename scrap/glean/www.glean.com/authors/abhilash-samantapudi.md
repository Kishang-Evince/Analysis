## About

## Abhilash Samantapudi

Abhilash Samantapudi is a Product Manager at Glean, where he works on building intelligent, scalable products that help organizations access and make sense of enterprise knowledge more effectively. At Glean, he focuses on developing user-centric solutions that leverage AI and advanced data retrieval to surface the right information at the right time across complex systems and workflows. Prior to joining Glean, Abhilash was an Associate Product Manager at Salesforce, where he worked on data platform products including Bulk API 2.0, a REST-based interface designed for efficient, large-scale asynchronous data operations such as create, update, query, and delete. During his time at Salesforce, he collaborated closely with engineering teams and the developer community, gathering feedback to improve product experiences and usability. His background across enterprise platforms positions him well to contribute to Glean’s mission of transforming how teams discover and use knowledge through AI-powered enterprise search.
