## About

## Tamar Yehoshua

Tamar Yehoshua is the President of Product and Technology at Glean. Prior to joining Glean, Tamar was Chief Product Officer at Slack, where she led product, design, and research as the company scaled, including a 10x increase in revenue, its public listing, and an acquisition by Salesforce. Tamar also led product and engineering teams at Google, working on Search, Identity and Privacy, and at A9.com, an Amazon company. Additionally, she was also a venture partner at venture capital firm IVP. Tamar has served on the board of directors for RetailMeNot, ServiceNow, Snyk, and Yext. She earned her BA in Mathematics from the University of Pennsylvania, and her Masters in Computer Science from The Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
