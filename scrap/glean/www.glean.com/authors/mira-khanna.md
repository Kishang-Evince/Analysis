## About

## Mira Khanna

Mira Khanna is a strategic and customer-centric product marketing leader with over nine years of experience driving go-to-market strategies and impactful growth campaigns in the tech industry. Currently Staff Product Marketing Manager at Mozilla, she leverages expertise in positioning, messaging, user experience, and brand risk management to connect innovative products to real users. Mira’s career spans pivotal roles at Grammarly, where she led B2C initiatives and multi-channel campaigns, as well as contributions to Partnerize and RAPP, where she developed B2B resources and award-winning awareness campaigns. Holding a B.A. in History (cum laude) with an English minor from Hamilton College and certifications from HubSpot and CMX Media, Mira is distinguished by creativity, analytical rigor, and a commitment to championing customers and product teams, further underscored by recognitions including Phi Alpha Theta, Dean’s List, and academic sports accolades. Driven and grounded in insights, she embodies the innovation and excellence essential to modern tech brands.
