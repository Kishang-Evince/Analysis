## How Glean’s agent builder works

01

Describe

Provide plain-language instructions for your workflow or autonomous agent, and Glean will build it for you.  
  

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8e8dac1fcd06d1481bb1e2_Agent%20Builder%20Accordion%20Describe%20Modal.png)

02

Connect

Securely link agents to enterprise data sources, systems, and third-party APIs — all with permissions intact.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8e8dac6e73b8e5db4f30c2_Agent%20Builder%20Accordion%20Connect%20Sources.png)

03

Refine

Test outputs, adjust instructions, and tweak creativity settings to ensure every agent performs as intended.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8e8dad9b4e7b20e7a65dfe_Agent%20Builder%20Accordion%20Refine%20Debug%20Run.png)

04

Deploy

Drafts, versioning, and rollback options help teams iterate while keeping agent updates seamless and controlled.
