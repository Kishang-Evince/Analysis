## Put evidence behind your AI roadmap.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8c0325b7b2acbbadb2a5ba_network.svg)

### Understand work at scale

Build a living, privacy-safe view of recurring work by role and function from activity across the systems your teams already use.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8c0325ff6786d45aea4316_target.svg)

### Know where to invest

Focus investment on the workflows with the strongest combination of estimated time savings and business impact.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8c0325def9dac5b88a060e_invest.svg)

### Prove what worked

Map the workflow again after deployment to see how time shifted and whether the change delivered the expected outcome.

Living workflow map

## See the work behind the workflow.

A single workflow can span calendars, documents, messages, CRM, and code. Transform brings that activity together to map how the work gets done, down to each step, handoff, and the time involved.

![Product screenshot](http://assets.glean.com/marketing/Transform/Living%20workflow%20map.webp)

Ranked opportunities

## Put the highest-value work first.

Transform puts the highest-value workflows at the top of the roadmap, based on estimated time savings and business impact. Each one leads to a decision: automate, augment, redesign, connect, or keep human-led.

![Product screenshot](http://assets.glean.com/marketing/Transform/Ranked%20opportunities.webp)

Agent and skill blueprints

## Turn workflow maps into buildable plans.

Once an opportunity rises to the top, Transform drafts an agent blueprint from the workflow map. Admins can review the required systems, permissions, and human review points before sending it to Glean Agent Builder.

ROI ledger

## Measure what changed after deployment.

Transform tests the business case after an agent or process change goes live. The ROI ledger follows the workflow over time, comparing actual impact with the original estimate.

![Product screenshot](http://assets.glean.com/marketing/Transform/ROI%20ledger.webp)
