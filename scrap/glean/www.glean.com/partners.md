## FAQs

What is the Glean Partner Network?

The Glean Partner Network is a global ecosystem of cloud providers, consulting firms, resellers, and technology companies that help enterprise customers deploy and maximize Glean's Work AI platform.

Is there a cost to join?

There is no fee to join the Glean Partner Network. Apply through our [portal](https://partners.glean.com/) and our team will evaluate fit based on your capabilities and market alignment.

How do I find a partner near me?

Use the [Partner Finder](https://www.glean.com/partners/partner-finder) to search by region, industry, partner type, and specialization. You can also contact our team and we'll match you with a partner.

What types of partners does Glean work with?

We work with Cloud Providers, Consulting & GSI Partners, Value-Added Resellers, and Technology Partners. Each has its own program track.

How long does the application process take?

Most applications are reviewed within 1–2 weeks. Once approved, you'll receive [Partner Portal](https://partners.glean.com/) access and onboarding materials immediately.

Can I purchase Glean through a partner?

Yes. Glean is available through our authorized resellers and on cloud marketplaces (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure), allowing you to use existing committed spend.
