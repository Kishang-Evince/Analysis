## Introduction

Zapier is an AI orchestration platform built for teams at organizations of every size, helping teams connect with over 9,000 apps to automate the most tedious parts of the workday. It turns complex integrations into simple, point-and-click workflows that handle everything from lead routing and data synchronization to customer conversations, all without requiring users to write a line of code. Since 2012, millions of users—from scrappy startups to Fortune 500 companies—have trusted Zapier to streamline their operations, reduce errors, and accelerate growth through intelligent automation.

## Before Glean

At Zapier, giving people the freedom to experiment with and adopt the tools they liked has enabled everyone to do their best work. However, that often meant information was scattered across sources, making it challenging to settle on a single system of record. 

Consolidating those tools was one option, but it meant losing resources that teams relied on for important workflows, as well as taking on what would have been a massive, time-consuming project. Zapier needed a way to consolidate and surface the right information at the right time, without asking people to change how they worked or give up the tools that fit their needs.

That’s why they turned to **Glean**.

## With Glean

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6994984fa63c1a21db1e5c8c_zillow-glean-usecases.webp)

### Unifying knowledge across teams and apps

With information at Zapier living across many internal tools, teams like Revenue Operations, who field a constant stream of questions from across the company, turned employees to Glean to find answers on their own. 

‍

Utilizing Glean’s Chrome extension to ensure enterprise context was at every employee’s fingertips, they quickly saw adoption rise. “Glean is a tool that speaks for itself once you start using it,” said Elise Dunham, Knowledge Manager. 

### Ticket deflection powered by Glean

Zapier launched ZapIT, a Slack-native helpdesk built on Zapier’s own platform, with Glean powering AI ticket deflection and knowledge search. With Glean providing the layer supplying full internal knowledge, including Slack history and company documentation, users could get answers directly in the Slack thread where the ticket was created. 

‍

This more than doubled ticket deflection rates, taking an average of 30 minutes to close. 98% of tickets opened at Zapier are now answered with Glean, with a 99% relevance rating. Employees are able to get answers much faster, freeing up their IT team to prioritize more complex organizational initiatives.   

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/69f0c965b9236ca97bad0e8f_motive-static-img.webp)

### ‍Bringing context into agent harnesses via MCP

As Zapier started working directly inside agent harnesses like Cursor, Claude Code, ChatGPT, and Codex, setting up MCP that connects to Glean proved invaluable. Users now gather unified context using Glean directly from those tools instead of leaving their flow of work.

### Customer Brief Agent

Zapier's Go-to-Market team developed a sales agent that pulls together the full context of a customer before a meeting, spanning product usage, back-and-forth in customer Slack channels, and internal notes from the account relationship. 

‍

With a single clear snapshot of a customer’s current state and recommendations on how best to engage them, GTM teams are more prepared for customer conversations, driving faster time to value and immediate impact. The Agent’s impact extends beyond sales as well - Executives, Marketers, and Product Managers also utilize it as a resource to prepare customer briefs before meetings and review cycles.

### Customer Proof Sourcing

Zapier’s Customer Advocacy team maintains a structured library of more than 700 customer stories, quotes, metrics, and reference assets, enriched with metadata such as industry, use case, persona, product, freshness, and usage restrictions. Glean gives Zapier teams a natural-language interface to that knowledge, helping GTM teams find relevant proof without needing to know where the underlying asset lives or how the database is structured. 

‍

The goal is not simply to store more content, but to make customer proof available whenever a salesperson, marketer, or customer-facing teammate needs it, with safeguards for freshness, marketing clearance, and customer-naming restrictions.

## Future

### What’s next for Zapier

With information easy to find and Glean woven into day-to-day work across the organization, Zapier's focus is continuing to enhance their context layer, expanding that foundation so every employee, and the agents they use, can draw on the full context they need to do their jobs—no matter what tool they choose to work in.
