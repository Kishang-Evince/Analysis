## Overview

Founded in 2018, Nile has been transforming how enterprises connect by delivering a truly modern network infrastructure. Nile's platform provides IT teams with integrated zero trust security, guaranteed performance, and AI-powered automation. By delivering networks entirely as a service, Nile helps organizations reduce operational costs while providing users with the reliable, high-performance access they need to succeed.

## Before Glean

As Nile scaled rapidly, teams across the organization were struggling with fragmented information. Documentation and critical insights were scattered across multiple platforms, creating silos across global teams that slowed productivity and collaboration.

Nile knew they needed a unified approach to knowledge management. While they considered building their own enterprise search platform, they saw a greater opportunity to know more about their business and scale faster with a Work AI platform.

**That’s why they turned to Glean.**

Glean’s knowledge graph inspired confidence that the platform could serve as a foundation for Nile employees, providing context behind answers to ensure trust and accuracy. Glean delivered the unified view they required, along with an agent-building platform to optimize high-impact workflows.

## With Glean

### Driving sales and support efficiency with Search and Assistant

Beyond Search providing unified access to accurate information across applications, Glean Assistant gives teams the ability to interact with and ask questions of the insights they pull.

‍

For Sales and Support teams, this has made onboarding, customer meeting prep, and ticket troubleshooting more efficient, while improving Nile’s competitive advantage.

![Diagram showing a connection flow from a blue circle with a white 'g' icon on the left to a white circle labeled 'nile' on the right, with various colorful icons interconnected in the center and two horizontal progress bars above and below the flow.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/69d53256833c88c192c43ab2_with-glean-2.webp)

### Org-wide agent deployment

Glean has enabled teams across Nile to build customized agents for their most important workflows, as well as bring other applications and AI tools into one agentic ecosystem.

Product Team

Documentation Agent

Nile's product team built this agent to transform PRDs and technical data into polished, external-facing documentation that follows a standardized format. What once took PMs up to 4 hours per document now takes just minutes – eliminating a massive backlog and saving the company from hiring a technical writing team.

GTM Team

Meeting Prep Agent

Nile's most popular agent among sales teams, this agent utilizes Glean’s Deep Research feature to research target companies and infuse insights with Nile’s sales strategy and value proposition. The output is discovery questions, key insights, and tuned messaging to prepare sellers going into meetings.

Product Team

Newsletter Agent

Run monthly, this agent compiles features released, recent customer success stories, and the latest thought leadership content into comprehensive customer newsletters that account teams share out. This was a task that previously took hours for PMs to develop each month, but now just minutes to review.

Engineering Team

Incident Insights Agent

Engineers are using Glean APIs with tools like Cursor to intelligently search across tools, code, and incident reports to develop quick summaries and troubleshoot.

### AI-driven cultural shift

Nile is seeing a cultural shift, where employees instinctively turn to a single source of truth for information and workflow acceleration, rather than relying on tribal knowledge or navigating scattered systems.

‍

Glean has streamlined workflows, reduced time spent searching for answers, and empowered teams to work more efficiently and collaboratively across the organization through AI-powered workflows.

Glean's knowledge graph gave us confidence in an AI tool with minimal hallucinations – it stands out compared to the others. We view Glean as a foundational element that is helping us build better products.

Karthikeyan Nathillvar

Head of Product for AI Data and Cloud Infrastructure, Nile

## Future

### What’s next for Nile

Nile is continuing to refine its content strategy, as well as test and publish new agents. One in-progress agent is a Lead Generation agent for sellers, which will draft targeted emails based on activity data – turning website visits into meaningful touchpoints, while maximizing prospecting time. Another, a Sentiment Analysis agent, will uncover and summarize daily ticket and sentiment themes for executives.

‍

Nile has rolled out an AI certification program internally, and leadership is encouraging employees to take outside courses on prompt engineering and agent building to continue refining their skills.

‍

Company-wide, the impact is clear: by redirecting time savings to higher-value initiatives, Nile is empowering employees to achieve more ambitious results with the support of AI – and expects these successes to continue growing.
