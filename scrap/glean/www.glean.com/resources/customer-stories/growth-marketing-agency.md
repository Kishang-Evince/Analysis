## About

This marketing consulting firm has accelerated growth for leading brands since its founding in 2010. It has more than 70 clients who count on its fully remote workforce for experiment-led marketing services.

## The challenge

This leading marketing agency has a fully remote team, which generates a vast amount of documentation across multiple tools. As the company grew, employees found it harder to locate key resources, making it difficult to stay informed. Whether they were reviewing martech due diligence or [onboarding new hires](https://www.glean.com/solutions/people), searching for information slowed them down and pulled focus from high-impact work.

## The solution

With Glean, their employees get the knowledge they need, without the struggle. Instead of sifting through scattered documents, teams can instantly find martech evaluations or onboarding materials.

‍

For a fast-growing company, this means:

-   **Faster onboarding**: New hires quickly get up to speed with instant access to company knowledge.
-   **Confident decision-making**: Employees easily find due diligence reports, ensuring they select the best tools for clients.
-   **Uninterrupted workflows**: Whether working in Slack or a browser, employees can search from anywhere without breaking focus.

Glean keeps keep employees informed and aligned, helping them scale efficiently without losing momentum.

More and more information is going to be created as we grow, and it’s going to continue to be in disparate sources. With Glean, employees are finding information they may not have otherwise known existed and using that to provide better experiences for our clients, which is highly advantageous to us.

Anonymous

VP of Operations
