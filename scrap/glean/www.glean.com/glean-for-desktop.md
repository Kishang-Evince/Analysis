## Quick Chat

### Quick Chat follows your work.

The Quick Chat window floats above your apps as you move between them. Draft a new doc, check your inbox, and continue the conversation.

## Screenshot-to-chat

### Share what you see with screenshot-to-chat.

Attach a screenshot directly in chat to show Glean what you’re looking at and get a more relevant answer, whether you are reviewing a UI, debugging an issue, or asking about a document.

## Keyboard shortcut

### Open Glean with a keystroke.

A single keyboard shortcut brings up Glean from whatever app you're in, so you can search, chat, or take action in the moment, without losing your place.
