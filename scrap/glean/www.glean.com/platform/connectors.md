### Invoke actions using natural language

Tell Glean what you want to get done and it takes action using its knowledge of how you like to work. ### Automate work by combining triggers with actions

Trigger agents based on events or schedules and have them take action on your behalf. 

### Take action with enterprise controls

Set clear boundaries for what agents can and can’t do, with actions that respect user permissions and human-in-the-loop controls that let you require approval before execution.

With 275+ native and MCP-based connectors, Glean taps into your app ecosystem, so you can start handing off work to AI.

### Work securely, within the right guardrails

Users only see the data and take the actions they're permitted to, with granular access controls, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, and AI security models that keep your enterprise secure.

### Bring deeper context to every workflow

Glean builds indexes and knowledge graphs across your data, so it can understand how your business works and automate work reliably.

## FAQs

How many connectors does Glean support?

Glean supports 275+ out-of-the-box connectors across enterprise apps and ecosystems.

Do Glean connectors preserve permissions?

Yes. Glean inherits and enforces source-system permissions, so users only see data they’re already allowed to access.

How fresh is data from connected apps?

Glean supports real-time sync, so updates to data and permissions are reflected as soon as they happen in the source system.

Can Glean do more than read data from connected tools?

Yes. Glean also supports actions, allowing users to describe a task in natural language and trigger work across connected systems.

What if a company needs an app that is not listed?

Teams can request new connectors or bring custom data and actions into Glean with custom connectors, the Indexing SDK, MCP servers, and OpenAPI-based actions.

Why do connectors matter for enterprise AI?

Connectors give Glean the business context it needs to search, reason, automate work, and take action across the tools employees already use.
