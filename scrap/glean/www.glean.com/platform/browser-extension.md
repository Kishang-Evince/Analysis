## How Glean’s browser extension works

### Surface

![Surface - mobile](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/691dc0adae3a7fbba14ca7e4_Surface%20-%20mobile.webp)

The Discover tab brings you personalized prompts and content suggestions based on what’s on your screen – turning information into insight, in real time.

### Ask

![Ask - mobile](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/691dc0a9e562256775126f4d_Ask%20-%20mobile.webp)

Use natural language to get instant answers from Glean, grounded in both your document and your company’s broader knowledge.

### Search

![Search - mobile](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/691dc0a9d716253a81f1de75_Search%20-%20mobile.webp)

Find documents, teammates, and conversations across your tools, right from your browser. All results respect permissions and context.

### Create

![Create - mobile](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/691dc0a98046173b25bcadf0_Create%20-%20mobile.webp)

Generate summaries, improve clarity, and connect to relevant knowledge – all from a sidebar that understands your workflow.

## Simplify

![Simplify - Mobile](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/691dc0a90ff820f4cc2f0868_Simplify%20-%20Mobile.webp)

Turn long documents into short answers with Glean. AI pulls key points, insights, and takeaways – so you can act faster, with confidence.
