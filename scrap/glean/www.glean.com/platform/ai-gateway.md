## How Glean AI Gateway works

01

Connect

Connect 40+ models, 275+ connectors, enterprise context, memory, and skills via MCP through a governed layer (skills coming soon).

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8eba7513b65433c7018fd5_connect-tabs_gateway.png)

02

Govern

Protect LLM and MCP traffic from prompt injection, filter harmful LLM content, and apply restricted topic controls to LLM interactions (beta).

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8eba75f045e985d97b13bb_govern-tabs_gateway.png)

03

Deploy

Roll out models and tools across AI surfaces to the whole organization through MDM, with no per-app setup.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8eba768631fab4aebf6308_deploy-tabs_gateway.png)

04

Observe

Track AI like the rest of your infrastructure: request volume, latency, and errors by model, plus token and tool consumption by user and app.
