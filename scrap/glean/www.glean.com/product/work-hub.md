## Announcements

Share and discover important news. Streamline communication and cut through the noise of other communication platforms. Never miss an important announcement.

Front and center. The announcements carousel appears at the top of the Glean home page, so nobody misses important company news.

**Precision targeting**. Share announcements by department and location, so people only see the information that is relevant to them.

**Saved for later**. Find past announcements by searching for them and organizing them in collections.

![Product Illustration](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6401f7d224f84dca6c23fa04_1.webp)

## Directory

Search for and understand who people are, what they’re working on, and how they can help.

**Find who you need**. Filter and find by department, role, and location.

**Get to know your team**. See what your colleagues are working on.

![Product Illustration](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6401f7d293d3b857a1f68755_2.webp)

## Resources

Maintain a single source of truth for useful resources.

**One click to company policies**. Get easy access to documents like employee handbooks, expense policies and more.

**Stay on the same page**. Share company OKRs, planning documents and analytics so everyone understands where things stand.

**Benefits information without the hassle**. Never forget where information is for 401k, insurance and other benefits.

![Product Illustration](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6401f7d2ee8fa707de2ea3b3_3.webp)

## Calendar and events

Stay on top of your day.

**Never miss a meeting**. Upcoming events on your home page, personalized to your calendar.

**Join with a click**. Easy access to videoconferencing details without digging through event invitations.

![Product Illustration](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6401f7d2baa66216dc62dc6e_4.webp)

## Recommendations

Your personalized feed of suggested and recent content.

**Saves you a search**. Quickly access the files you need.

**Trending content**. Browse the documents that are getting the most activity among your team and the company.

**Mentions**. See where teammates have tagged you across apps.

![Product Illustration](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6401f7d283de4f8298400c8b_5.webp)
