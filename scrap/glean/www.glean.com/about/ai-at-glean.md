### Stay ahead of what matters to customers

Every morning brings new AI headlines, product claims, and competitor news. Our field needs to know what changed and what it means for customers.

‍

Glean turns those signals into a daily brief for Solutions Engineering, connecting the news to customer implications and the next actions that matter in the field.

### Resolve connector issues faster

Connector escalations are hard to untangle when evidence lives across support tickets, Slack threads, Jira issues, logs, metrics, code, and past cases.

‍

Our connector team built an agent that brings that context together and reasons through the issue. Resolution time fell from more than 100 hours to roughly 50 hours, while support containment rose from 70% to 90%.

### Make product decisions with interactive artifacts

Product decisions often start with messy questions about who a feature is for and what needs to change before the next iteration.

‍

Our design team uses Glean to turn internal examples, product metrics, and copy explorations into an interactive artifact the team can test together.

### See what a deal needs next

Deal context is spread across customer conversations, the CRM, internal discussions, and opportunity history.

‍

Our Sales team uses Glean to review the opportunity against the team's sales methodology, see what's missing, and understand what could move the deal forward.

### Turn company context into stronger content

Strong marketing starts with understanding the customer and knowing the product. 

‍

Our Marketing team uses Glean to bring sales calls, Jira tickets, technical docs, and brand guidance into their work, then create strong drafts and verify claims against internal sources.

### Turn complex questions into reviewable analysis

Finance questions can take days when data is spread across staffing sheets, CRM records, and other systems.

‍

Our Finance team uses Glean to work across those sources in one conversation and produce an interactive artifact tied to the underlying data.

## What changes when teams use Glean

Using Glean changes where teams spend their time. More time goes to judgment and execution, with less spent rebuilding context or repeating the same work.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8676687d5445830197d3c5_glongl-feedback-bg.png)

### Context is easier to reach

Teams can start with the information they need, instead of rebuilding context from scattered systems.

### Decisions get clearer

Glean helps teams compare signals, uncover patterns, and understand what changed before deciding what to do next.

### Expertise becomes reusable

The best processes can become a Skill, artifact, or agent that other teams can use.
