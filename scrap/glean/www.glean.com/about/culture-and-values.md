## The valuesthat shape our work.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a85f52ef3a6b8f304a8fd70_component-icon-dot-grid.svg)

### Make it customer-driven

Stay close to real customer problems

Prioritize the issues that matter most

Follow through to create lasting value

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a85f52ef3a6b8f304a8fd70_component-icon-dot-grid.svg)

### Make it happen

Take ownership and communicate early

Simplify the path forward

Debate openly, then commit and move

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a85f52ef3a6b8f304a8fd70_component-icon-dot-grid.svg)

### Make it better

Question the status quo constructively

Give and seek direct feedback

Keep learning and improve what you touch

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a85f52ef3a6b8f304a8fd70_component-icon-dot-grid.svg)

### Make it together

Share context openly

Respect different perspectives

Support one another through hard moments

## How we put our culture into practice

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8407490b895e304bf53554_icon-placeholder.svg)

Start strong

New hires learn how Glean works, meet people across teams, and are encouraged to bring a fresh perspective from day one.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8407490b895e304bf53554_icon-placeholder.svg)

Share what you know

We share context openly, making it easier to understand decisions and work together without silos.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8407490b895e304bf53554_icon-placeholder.svg)

Stay connected

Team events, all-hands, and public recognition help Gleanies stay connected and celebrate progress together.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/6a8407490b895e304bf53554_icon-placeholder.svg)

Make better decisions with full context

Get proactive answers, recommendations, and next steps grounded in your company’s full context, so every decision starts with the right information.
