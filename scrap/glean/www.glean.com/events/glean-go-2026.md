## FAQs

What is Glean:GO?

Glean:GO 2026 is Glean’s flagship event for customers, prospects, partners, media, and analysts.  
  
It is where leaders, builders, and operators come together to see what’s next in enterprise AI, learn from the teams already putting AI to work at scale, and get practical guidance they can bring back to their organizations.  
  
From strategic keynotes and customer stories to technical deep dives, hands-on workshops, and certification-style learning, Glean:GO is designed to help attendees move from experimentation to real business impact.

Is Glean:GO a hybrid event?

Yes.  
  
Glean:GO 2026 is planned as a hybrid event with both in-person and virtual experiences, so teams can join in the format that works best for them.  

Where is the in-person Glean:GO event taking place?

Glean:GO 2026 is planned for San Francisco, with Fort Mason as the working venue for the in-person experience.  
  
We’ll share final venue details, logistics, and arrival information with registered attendees as details are finalized.

When is Glean:GO happening?

Glean:GO 2026 is planned as a multi-day event happening on August 26th to 27th, 2026.

Who should attend Glean:GO?

Glean:GO is built for a broad enterprise AI audience, including customers, prospective customers, admins, developers, IT and security leaders, functional business leaders, and executives shaping how AI gets adopted across their organizations. Whether you want strategic guidance, practical implementation advice, product depth, or hands-on learning, there will be programming designed for your role.

What kinds of sessions can I expect?

Glean:GO 2026 is shaping up to include:

-   Keynote moments focused on Glean’s product vision and the future of enterprise AI
-   Customer stories and practical transformation sessions centered on adoption, ROI, governance, and change management
-   Technical builder sessions on agents, MCP, APIs, platform primitives, continual learning, and proactive context
-   Admin-focused sessions on deployment, connectors, adoption, governance, security, and AI cost management
-   Roadmap and product sessions that go deeper on the platform areas announced on the main stage
-   Invitation-led executive programming for senior leaders
-   Partner programming and showcase moments
-   Hands-on onboarding and workshop-style learning for users who want to build skill quickly

Will there be hands-on workshops or training?

Yes.  
  
In addition to breakout sessions, the 2026 program is expected to include hands-on workshops, guided build sessions, and onboarding experiences designed to help attendees learn by doing.  
  
That includes working sessions for builders and admins, as well as a user onboarding bootcamp experience for attendees who want practical, step-by-step enablement.

Will sessions be available on-demand?

Yes.  
  
Keynotes and many sessions are expected to be recorded and made available after the event so attendees can catch up on the programming they missed and share relevant content with their teams.

Will there be networking opportunities?

Absolutely. Glean:GO is being designed to create meaningful opportunities for attendees to connect with peers, customers, builders, admins, executives, and partners through curated discussions, breakout conversations, and community moments across the event.
