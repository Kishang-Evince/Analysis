## What is a search assistant for cross-team collaboration?

A search assistant is an AI-powered tool that helps employees find information, people, and resources across multiple teams and departments within an organization. Unlike traditional search tools that only index documents, modern search assistants understand context, permissions, and relationships between people, content, and workflows.

In many organizations, valuable information often remains isolated within specific teams or tools, creating communication barriers. These silos can impede collaboration and delay decision-making. University of North Carolina [research](https://www.kenan-flagler.unc.edu/perspectives/breaking-barriers-how-to-free-your-organization-from-the-silo-mentality/) found 83% of survey respondents indicated silos exist within their companies. An overwhelming 97% saw these organizational silos as having a negative effect on performance. Search assistants address this challenge by providing a cohesive platform that connects data across departments such as sales, engineering, marketing, and HR.

The evolution from basic keyword search to intelligent assistants marks a significant leap in enterprise technology. Where traditional search engines rely on exact matches and Boolean operators, [AI-powered search assistants](https://www.glean.com/blog/what-is-ai-search) employ machine learning models that understand intent, context, and organizational relationships. They learn from user interactions, improving result relevance over time and adapting to company-specific terminology and workflows. This intelligence layer transforms raw data into actionable insights, enabling employees to make informed decisions quickly and collaborate more effectively across departmental boundaries.

## How search assistants break down communication silos

In many organizations, valuable information often remains isolated within specific teams or tools, creating communication barriers. These silos can impede collaboration and delay decision-making. Search assistants address this challenge by providing a cohesive platform that connects data across departments such as sales, engineering, marketing, and HR.

When employees seek project updates, search assistants gather information from various sources like Slack, project management tools, and shared documents. This integration allows employees to access comprehensive data without toggling between platforms. By understanding the context of inquiries, search assistants eliminate the need for precise file locations, enabling users to ask questions naturally.

By revealing hidden insights and expertise, search assistants empower employees to uncover valuable knowledge they might not have known existed. This capability not only boosts individual productivity but also cultivates a culture of [knowledge sharing](https://www.glean.com/blog/community-ai-success-2) and innovation. Continuous updates ensure that teams work with the most recent information, minimizing the risk of outdated data leading to errors.

## Key features that enable better cross-team collaboration

### Unified search across all workplace apps

Search assistants seamlessly integrate with a wide range of tools, from cloud storage like Google Drive to communication platforms such as Slack and [Microsoft Teams](https://www.glean.com/blog/glean-microsoft-teams). This integration enables employees to search within a single interface, saving time and reducing the need to switch between multiple applications. The system maintains security by aligning with existing permissions, ensuring users access only the data they're meant to see.

### AI-powered context understanding

With sophisticated AI, search assistants recognize the underlying intent of queries, delivering results that match the user's unique context. This involves tailoring responses based on individual roles, departments, and previous interactions, enhancing the relevance of the information retrieved. By offering both specific answers and broader insights, these tools empower users to make well-informed decisions quickly.

### Smart people discovery

Search assistants also enhance team connectivity by identifying colleagues with the necessary skills or knowledge across the organization. Users can easily view organizational structures and relationships, facilitating effective collaboration. This feature supports cross-functional initiatives by ensuring the right expertise is readily available when needed.

## Real-time insights for improved team communication

Search assistants revolutionize team interactions by offering dynamic insights into organizational activities. With up-to-the-minute visibility into project progress and team initiatives, these tools keep everyone aligned and informed. Interactive dashboards highlight key discussions and emerging themes, enabling teams to address pressing issues efficiently.

Proactive notifications ensure timely responses. These alerts inform team members of crucial updates or the need for their expertise, guaranteeing that no vital detail is overlooked. By identifying the most impactful resources, search assistants guide teams in prioritizing essential knowledge creation.

Understanding global team dynamics is crucial. By recognizing team members’ locations and schedules, search assistants facilitate seamless collaboration across time zones. Additionally, they ensure that meeting outcomes and decisions are easily accessible, supporting effective implementation and follow-through. This approach to communication fosters a culture of transparency and accountability throughout the organization.

Onboarding becomes a seamless experience, with new hires able to explore resources and gain insights independently. New hires in these companies are 30% more likely to stay past their first year.

Moreover, these assistants automatically gather insights from various communications, turning them into a comprehensive knowledge base. This not only preserves essential context but also triggers automated processes across departments. By doing so, organizations can eliminate repetitive tasks and facilitate smooth information flow.

The adoption of search assistants results in notable gains in project completion rates across different teams. By accessing information directly, the volume of emails decreases, streamlining communication and minimizing disruptions. This improved workflow leads to greater employee satisfaction, as daily operations become more fluid and cohesive. In fact, [Glean's survey](https://www.hcamag.com/us/specialization/employee-engagement/employees-waste-at-least-two-hours-a-day-searching-for-what-they-need-to-work/324737) found 43% of employees would consider leaving their job if there wasn't an efficient way to access needed information, rising to 51% for workers aged 18-34 and 57% for those aged 35-44.

## Best practices for implementation

To successfully deploy a search assistant, begin with a targeted pilot program. Focus on teams that depend heavily on collaboration, such as those in product development or support, to demonstrate the tool's effectiveness. This strategy not only illustrates immediate advantages but also fosters excitement and adoption across the organization.

Ensure precise setup by aligning permissions with the company’s hierarchy to maintain security and relevance. Define clear guidelines for which data should be included in the search index, ensuring accessibility to essential information. This clarity aligns the search assistant with organizational goals and standards.

Training is crucial for maximizing the tool's benefits. Provide employees with guidance on using natural language queries effectively, enhancing their ability to leverage the assistant fully. Regularly review search analytics to identify and address information gaps. This continuous evaluation helps refine the tool's functionality and optimize its use.

Encourage a feedback-driven culture by allowing employees to suggest improvements or report missing data. Seamlessly integrate the search assistant into existing workflows, ensuring it enhances rather than complicates daily tasks. This approach helps employees view the assistant as a vital resource, boosting productivity and collaboration across teams.

## Getting started with search-powered collaboration

To begin leveraging search assistants, start by evaluating your organization's current collaboration dynamics. Gather insights from teams to pinpoint major information challenges and understand their specific needs. This assessment will help identify which platforms are essential for seamless integration and connectivity.

Choosing the right solution is crucial. Look for an AI platform that aligns with your tech landscape, ensuring it connects effortlessly with existing systems. Begin with a specific objective, such as enhancing project transparency or expediting team onboarding. This approach allows you to track meaningful outcomes and adjust strategies for maximum impact.

Define clear metrics for success. Consider goals like reducing information search time or boosting cross-departmental efficiency. Building a network of champions within your organization can foster enthusiasm and facilitate implementation. By scaling thoughtfully and collecting ongoing feedback, you can refine your strategy to better serve your teams and fully harness the benefits of search-powered collaboration.
