## What is AI search?

AI search represents a significant evolution from traditional keyword-based search systems. At its core, AI search uses artificial intelligence - including machine learning, natural language processing, and deep learning - to understand the context and intent behind queries, delivering precise, personalized results across an organization's entire knowledge ecosystem.

Unlike conventional search that relies on exact keyword matches, AI search understands natural language queries. When an employee asks, "What's our parental leave policy?" the system comprehends the intent and surfaces relevant information, even if the actual documents use different terminology. This semantic understanding, powered by deep learning models, enables employees to find what they need using their own words, not predetermined keywords.

Modern [AI search platforms](https://www.glean.com/product/workplace-search-ai) go beyond simple retrieval. They continuously learn from an organization's unique language, terminology, and context. Through techniques like vector search and [knowledge graphs](https://www.glean.com/product/knowledge-graph), these systems map relationships between people, content, and activities across the enterprise. This creates a living, breathing information network that adapts to how teams actually work and communicate.

The technology indexes content across 100+ [enterprise applications](https://www.glean.com/connectors) in real-time, ensuring employees always access the most current information. More importantly, it maintains strict [security protocols](https://www.glean.com/security), respecting existing permissions so users only see what they're authorized to access. This unified, secure approach to information retrieval forms the foundation for reducing the cognitive load that contributes to burnout.
