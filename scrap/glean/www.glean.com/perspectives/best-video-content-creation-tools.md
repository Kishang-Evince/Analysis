# The best AI video editing tools for marketers in 2025

Marketing teams face unprecedented pressure to produce video content that captures attention and drives engagement. The demand for video has exploded across every platform, yet traditional production methods remain time-intensive and resource-heavy.

Artificial intelligence has fundamentally transformed how marketers approach video creation. These new tools eliminate technical barriers and compress production timelines from weeks to hours, enabling teams to focus on strategy rather than execution. Today, [89%](https://wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/) of businesses use video as a marketing tool, and 95% of video marketers see video as an important part of their overall strategy, with 68% of marketers who don't currently use video planning to start using video in 2025.

The convergence of machine learning, computer vision, and natural language processing has created a new category of video editing software. This technology shift empowers marketing professionals to produce professional-quality content without specialized training or dedicated video teams.

## What are AI video editing tools?

AI video editing tools represent a fundamental shift in how marketing teams create and produce video content. These platforms harness artificial intelligence to automate complex editing tasks, generate content from [text prompts](https://www.glean.com/blog/what-is-an-ai-prompt), and make intelligent decisions about pacing, transitions, and visual effects. Unlike traditional editing software that requires manual frame-by-frame adjustments, AI-powered solutions understand context and intent — they analyze your content goals and automatically apply professional editing techniques.

Mid-tier solutions such as Runway Gen-4 and Adobe Firefly integrate AI features into more traditional editing workflows, providing creative professionals with intelligent assistance while maintaining manual control. At the [enterprise level](https://www.glean.com/blog/top-9-gen-ai-tools-2025), platforms like Veo 3.1 and Sora 2 push the boundaries of what's possible — creating cinematic sequences with realistic physics, emotional nuance, and coherent multi-scene narratives that rival human-directed productions.
