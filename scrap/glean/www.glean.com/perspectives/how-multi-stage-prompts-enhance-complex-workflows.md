# How multi-stage prompts enhance complex workflows

Complex workflows often overwhelm traditional AI systems when presented as monolithic tasks. Breaking these workflows into sequential, interconnected stages transforms how AI processes information and delivers results.

Multi-stage prompting represents a fundamental shift in AI architecture: from single-shot interactions to orchestrated sequences. This approach mirrors how humans naturally tackle complex problems — through systematic decomposition and progressive refinement.

Enterprise teams across [engineering](https://www.glean.com/blog/glean-ai-prompting-guide-engineers), sales, and [support](https://www.glean.com/blog/glean-ai-prompting-guide-support) increasingly rely on this technique to automate sophisticated processes. The methodology enables AI to maintain context across steps while adapting its approach based on intermediate outputs, creating more reliable and transparent workflows.

## What is multi-stage prompting?

Multi-stage prompting is an advanced AI technique that decomposes complex tasks into a series of sequential, interconnected prompts where each stage builds upon the outputs of previous ones. Unlike traditional single-prompt approaches that attempt to handle entire workflows in one interaction, this methodology creates a chain of specialized prompts — each focused on a specific subtask within the larger workflow.
