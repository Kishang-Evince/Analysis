## FAQs

What makes Glean different from Microsoft 365 Copilot?

**Glean** is a company-wide AI platform that connects to all of your enterprise data, not just an in-app assistant. It builds enterprise context across every system, learning from content, activity, and people to deliver relevant, trustworthy answers and automate work in seconds. **Microsoft 365 Copilot**, on the other hand, is embedded within Microsoft 365 apps to help users work faster inside that environment, summarizing emails, generating documents, or analyzing spreadsheets. **The key difference:** Glean helps you find and understand information across your company, while Copilot helps you create and process information within Microsoft apps. Glean is ecosystem-agnostic; Copilot is ecosystem-bound.

Does Glean integrate with Microsoft tools?

Yes. Glean integrates deeply with Microsoft through native Microsoft connectors as part of **100+ turnkey, permission-aware integrations** that keep access controls enforced end-to-end. Inside **Microsoft Teams**, Glean brings search and chat into the flow of work, allowing employees to ask questions and get source-backed, AI-generated answers without leaving Teams. Glean also connects to **GitHub** to unify developer knowledge alongside Microsoft content, ensuring enterprise-wide context. And with Glean Agents and a library of 100+ actions (including MCP host actions), teams can run permission-aware Microsoft and cross-stack automations to retrieve, reason over, and execute workflows on company data, securely and at scale.

Which tool works best across multiple data sources (e.g. Google Workspace, Slack, Confluence)?

Glean excels at multi-source integration, offering pre-built connectors for hundreds of enterprise tools including Google Drive, Slack, Confluence, Jira, Zendesk, Salesforce, and even custom data sources. Microsoft Copilot performs best with Microsoft-owned products. Integrations with non-Microsoft systems often require additional configuration or custom connectors, and the experience is less seamless.If your organization uses both Microsoft and non-Microsoft applications, **Glean delivers a more unified, cross-platform experience**.

What is the best alternative to Microsoft 365 Copilot?

If you’re looking for an enterprise-grade Copilot alternative, **Glean is built to go beyond Microsoft 365**. It connects to your entire tech stack, from Slack and Salesforce to SharePoint and ServiceNow, giving employees AI that works across every tool, not just one suite. With **enterprise-grade security, data governance, and cross-platform connectivity**, Glean provides a single, trusted AI layer for all your company knowledge.
