### Turn project insights into follow-through.

Turn project meetings, messages, and updates into draft RFIs, tasks, summaries, and change descriptions that are ready to act on.

Compile contracts, RFIs, directives, emails, and change events into a single timeline to support claims and audits without reconstructing history by hand.
