## FAQs

How does Glean help retailers give store associates fast, accurate answers on products, policies, and promotions?

Glean connects product, policy, promotion, and operational knowledge across the systems retailers already use. Store associates can ask a question and get a trusted answer grounded in current context, instead of checking multiple tools or waiting on a manager. That helps teams respond faster, stay consistent, and deliver a better customer experience in the moment.

How can Glean reduce onboarding time for retail employees across stores and corporate teams?

Glean gives new hires a faster way to find training materials, product information, operating procedures, and role-specific guidance. Instead of depending on ad hoc handoffs or tribal knowledge, employees can get answers from the sources their teams already use. That helps store associates, support teams, and corporate employees ramp faster.

How does Glean connect to the retail tech stack while keeping permissions and security intact?

Glean connects to the applications where retail knowledge lives, from productivity and collaboration tools to service platforms, data sources, documents, and team systems. It preserves source-system permissions, so employees only see information they’re allowed to access. With enterprise governance and security controls built in, retailers can make answers easier to find without creating new access risk.

What are the highest-impact use cases for Glean across merchandising, supply chain, store operations, e-commerce, and support?

High-impact use cases are the ones where teams need fast context from many places. Common examples include store and support responses, onboarding, merchandising research, policy lookup, cross-functional issue resolution, customer service drafting, and operational decision support. Glean helps teams reduce repetitive prep, spot relevant context faster, and make product, service, and execution decisions with more confidence.

How is Glean’s system of context different from Copilot, Gemini, or custom GPTs for retail teams?

Glean’s system of context gives AI the business context it needs to work across retail teams, not just inside one app or workflow. It connects company knowledge, understands relationships between people, data, and processes, and applies permissions throughout the experience. For retail teams, that means answers can be grounded in the real context behind product, customer, store, and operational questions.

Can Glean support an in-house GPT or AI assistant by adding enterprise context instead of replacing it?

Yes. Glean can provide the governed enterprise context behind an existing GPT, assistant, or agent experience. That helps teams avoid rebuilding connectors, retrieval, permissions, and ranking from scratch. If a retailer already has an internal interface, Glean can help ground it in trusted company knowledge so responses are more accurate, secure, and useful in day-to-day work.

How do retailers measure Glean’s success, from hours saved to sales lift or improved CSAT?

Retailers should measure Glean across workflow efficiency and business impact. Useful metrics include time saved, faster onboarding, fewer repeated questions, quicker response times, and higher productivity for store and support teams. Retailers can also connect Glean to business outcomes, such as stronger customer experience, improved CSAT, and faster execution across merchandising, operations, and support.

What change management and training are needed for retail teams to adopt Glean at scale?

Adoption works best when Glean is tied to real retail workflows. Start with a few role-specific use cases, such as store policy lookup, customer support responses, merchandising research, or onboarding. Then show employees how to use Glean in the tools and moments where they already work. Practical examples and early wins build trust faster than broad AI training.

How does Glean combine structured retail data with unstructured docs, emails, and wikis so teams can chat with their data?

Glean brings structured data and unstructured knowledge into one governed experience. Teams can ask questions that connect metrics from data systems with the documents, emails, policies, and team knowledge that explain what the numbers mean. That gives retail teams more context for decisions, not just isolated data points.

What implementation options does Glean offer for global retailers with strict residency, governance, and compliance needs?

Glean offers deployment options for enterprises with strict security, residency, governance, and compliance needs. Retailers can choose Glean-hosted environments or customer cloud deployments, with controls for permissions, sensitive content, and model data handling. That gives global teams flexibility to adopt AI while meeting internal and regional requirements.
