## FAQs

How does Glean help legal teams move faster without increasing risk or losing control?

Glean brings together the contracts, policies, negotiation history, approvals, and matter context legal teams need to do their work. Instead of chasing information across systems, teams can quickly find trusted context, reuse approved language, and move routine work forward with more consistency.  
  
Glean also helps legal teams stay in control. It respects source-system permissions, grounds answers in company knowledge, and shows where information came from, so teams can move faster without losing oversight.

What security, privacy, and audit controls does Glean provide for legal work?

Glean is built for enterprise use, with controls that help legal teams work securely across sensitive information. It preserves source-system permissions, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and supports access controls, hosting options, logging, and monitoring.  
  
For organizations with strict governance needs, Glean also supports auditability through access logs, security documentation, and trust resources such as SOC 2 reports.

How does Glean verify that AI-generated answers are grounded in approved sources, current policies, and the right permissions?

Glean grounds answers in your company’s own content and respects the permissions already defined in source systems. Users only receive answers from information they’re allowed to access.  
  
Legal teams can also guide Glean toward approved sources for sensitive workflows, such as current policies, standards, and curated knowledge sets. Citations back to source material make it easier to review answers and confirm they’re based on the right information.

Can Glean compare contract drafts against legal playbooks, fallback clauses, prior redlines, and approval history?

Yes. Glean can help surface the legal context around a contract review, including relevant playbook guidance, fallback clauses, prior redlines, approval notes, and related matter history.  
  
That gives legal teams a faster way to review drafts, reduce repetitive research, and make more consistent decisions based on what has already been reviewed, negotiated, and approved.

How does Glean help legal teams complete security, privacy, and vendor questionnaires faster with source-linked answers?

Glean helps legal teams draft questionnaire responses using approved internal sources, such as security standards, privacy documentation, and prior answers. Instead of starting from scratch, teams can create a grounded first draft with citations back to the underlying source material.  
  
That makes it faster to complete security, privacy, and vendor questionnaires while giving reviewers a clear way to validate answers before anything is sent.

How does Glean enable self-service legal answers for employees without sacrificing accuracy or nuance?

Glean helps employees get cited, permission-aware answers to common legal questions, so they can self-serve without relying on guesswork or adding more routine requests to the legal queue. Employees get faster access to policy-backed guidance, while legal teams keep more control over consistency and accuracy.  
  
For nuanced or higher-risk issues, Glean can also support structured intake and routing workflows, helping requests move to the right legal review path.

How should legal teams measure ROI from Glean beyond time savings?

Legal teams should measure ROI across speed, quality, protection, and savings. That can include cycle time, playbook consistency, policy adherence, deflected requests, reduced risk of missed actions, and lower outside counsel spend.  
  
The strongest ROI story is not just that legal works faster. It’s that legal becomes more consistent, more scalable, and better able to support the business without adding the same level of overhead.
