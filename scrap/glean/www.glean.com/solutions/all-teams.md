## How teams use Glean

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/69d44a731bdbafe5428a11dd_allteams-how-1.webp)

### Onboard new hires quickly

Turn company context into usable answeres, drafts, and next steps so new hires can contribute without chasing down tribal knowledge.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/69d44a7d6de4f7a2c0dd6efd_allteams-how-2.webp)

### Give every team sharper context

Connect people, docs, tickets, messages, meetings, and systems of record so work starts with the full picture instead of a scavenger hunt.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/69d44a86f426012090a02b80_allteams-how-3.webp)

### Move work forward across the business

Help teams create deliverables, coordinate follow-through, and act across tools with the governance, permissions, and controls enterprises need.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/69d44a91a63df7e3e69f341e_allteams-how-4.webp)

### Access tribal knowledge

Discover the insights your team already knows, answers that have already been shared, and people who know even more.
