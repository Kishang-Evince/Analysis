## How Glean’s enterprise search software works

### Prompt

Type your query into Glean’s unified search bar, whether you’re looking for an insight stored in a document, a Slack message, or a Jira ticket. Glean searches across all your company’s apps to find exactly what you need. 

![Glean Search Graphic ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/67975d24d9876efe5786b001_Frame%202083935132.webp)

### Understand

Deep learning LLMs enable semantic understanding for natural language queries. Glean continuously trains on your company’s unique language and context, delivering highly relevant results without manual fine-tuning.

![Glean Understand Graphic ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/67991c37d5f650c88d7f5cac_Understand.webp)

### Generate

Glean’s AI understands your query’s context and retrieves the most relevant answers, drawing from up-to-date information across your tools. 

![Glean Generate Graphic ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/67991ea7c75dc88380a5349b_Generate.webp)

### Personalize

Glean builds a knowledge graph of your company’s people, content, and interactions. Results are personalized to each user, ensuring employees see the most relevant information for their role. 

![Glean Personalize Graphic ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6127a84dfe068e153ef20572/67975d24cf2d965660705390_Frame%202083935133.webp)

Glean enforces real-time indexing and respects existing permissions, so users only access what they’re allowed to see.

## FAQs

What is enterprise search software?

Enterprise search software helps organizations find and access information across apps, documents, and systems. It centralizes knowledge, making it easier for teams to stay productive and informed.

How does enterprise search software work?

Enterprise search software connects an organization's tools, apps, and data into a single platform. It uses AI to analyze queries, retrieve relevant results, and surface insights, helping teams quickly find the information they need.

What are the benefits of using enterprise search software?

It cuts down on the time spent searching through documents, conversations, or answers by providing a unified search experience. Teams can quickly access what they need and stay focused on higher-value work.

How does Glean make search faster?

Glean's AI understands your query's context - your role, projects, and tools - and instantly delivers the most relevant results. You'll find what you need in seconds with proactive recommendations and intuitive filters.

What tools does Glean integrate with?

Glean integrates with over 100 apps, including Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Salesforce, Jira, and ServiceNow.

What kind of insights does Glean provide?

Glean delivers concise document summaries, key takeaways from meetings, and proactive recommendations based on your work.

What makes Glean different from other enterprise search tools?

Glean doesn’t just search for information - it understands your team’s context, surfaces proactive recommendations, and connects knowledge across silos. Its intuitive design makes it easy for teams to adopt and love.
