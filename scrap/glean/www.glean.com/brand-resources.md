## Logo

01

The Glean wordmark is our brand’s anchor. It signs off everything-from product UI to global campaigns.  
  
Look closely and you’ll see a search icon and a smile, nods to what drives us: curiosity, clarity, and delight. Simple on the surface, thoughtful by design.  

## Clear space & sizing

Give the logo room to breathe. Keep at least one “N” width of space around it.    
  
Minimum size:  
20px for digital, 3mm for print.

## Logo icon

When there’s not room for our wordmark, use the logo symbol instead. Allow for ample whitespace around the avatar.  
  
As a general rule we use the inner counter of the ‘g’ to define our clear space when locking up the avatar.  

## Trademark Usage

Use the ™ symbol on first or prominent mentions of “Glean” in written text-such as documentation, legal disclaimers, or footers.  
  
Do **not** add ™ directly to the Glean logo itself. Like Apple and other leading brands, we keep our logo clean and unobstructed in all visual applications.

## Typeface

03

PolySans is a fresh take on mid 20th century classics, visually distinct from its predecessors by a subtle soft-edge ink trap feature.  

## Fallback typeface

The Fallback typeface is DM Sans which is a Google font to be used only in cases where PolySans cannot be used, such  as Google Slides.
