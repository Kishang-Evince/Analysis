On this page

Glean is proud to partner with [Fellow](https://fellow.ai/) to provide a seamless experience for your team. Refer the [Fellow documentation](https://help.fellow.ai/en/articles/11003216-glean-fellow) or contact their support team with any questions.

The Fellow connector in Glean automatically syncs meeting agendas, notes and AI recaps from Fellow into Glean so teams can search and access them alongside other work content.

## **Features**[​](#features "Direct link to features")

-   **Unified AI-powered search:** Index Fellow’s AI-powered meeting summaries and action items into Glean, combining them with your entire enterprise knowledge base. This empowers you to generate comprehensive summaries and automate work across your organization.
-   **Controlled access and privacy:** Glean strictly mirrors the access controls set in Fellow, ensuring only the people who already have permission to view meeting data will see it in Glean. Private conversations and sensitive notes remain secure.

## Benefits[​](#benefits "Direct link to Benefits")

Make all Fellow meeting data—including notes, action items, and decisions—instantly searchable and secure within Glean, streamlining knowledge discovery.

## Pre-installation considerations[​](#pre-installation-considerations "Direct link to Pre-installation considerations")

-   You must have administrator access to Fellow and Glean.
-   In Glean, you must configure an [Indexing API Authentication](https://developers.glean.com/api-info/indexing/authentication/overview). The Indexing API only supports Glean-issued tokens.

## Installation instructions[​](#installation-instructions "Direct link to Installation instructions")

Visit the [Fellow integrations documentation](https://help.fellow.ai/en/articles/11003216-glean-fellow) for instructions on setting up Glean for Fellow.
