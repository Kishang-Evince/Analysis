On this page

Manage your connectors from the Glean admin console. Use this section to monitor sync progress, control who can access indexed content, and review connector health.

From **Admin console** > **Platform** > **Connectors**, you can:

-   Monitor initial sync and ongoing updates
-   Review connector health signals and alerts
-   Control visibility during rollout and testing
-   Update connector settings for supported connectors
