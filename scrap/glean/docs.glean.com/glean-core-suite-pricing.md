On this page

Glean Core Suite is the foundation for every user, workflow, and AI experience across your company. It provides every organization with core capabilities for Assistant and Agents, APIs, Enterprise Search, workplace management solutions, infrastructure, and all the surfaces where work gets done. Each element of Glean Core Suite is powered by Glean's unique approach to enterprise context, openness and extensibility, and security and governance.

Glean is always-on and working for you. Whether that's indexing and connecting your connectors, enforcing permissions, or intelligently and proactively drawing connections across enterprise data, Glean provides your teams with the core foundations they need to improve productivity, drive efficiencies, and power your business.
