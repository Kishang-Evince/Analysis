Clear your browser cache in Glean to troubleshoot a bug or performance issue. Follow these steps in Chrome:

1.  Click the lock icon in the left of the URL bar and choose **Site settings**.

2.  In the window that opens, click the **Clear data** button.

3.  Log into Glean again.
