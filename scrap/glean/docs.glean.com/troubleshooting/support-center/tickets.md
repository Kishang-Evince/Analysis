On this page

Create a support ticket for issues that require formal tracking, follow-up, or escalation. Ticket creation is available to **Designated Support Contacts** as defined in the [Glean Support Agreement](https://glean.com/support-agreement). Tickets are best for bug reports, outages, and requests that need a documented resolution.

Please reach out to your Glean account team if you need to be set up as a Designated Support Contact.

## Prerequisites[​](#prerequisites "Direct link to Prerequisites")

Before you create a ticket:

-   Confirm that you qualify as a **Designated Support Contact**, as defined in the [Glean Support Agreement](https://glean.com/support-agreement).
    -   If you do not qualify, [start a conversation](/troubleshooting/support-center/conversations) with Support or ask your Glean Administrator to submit a ticket for you.
-   Confirm that you can sign in to the [Gleaniverse](https://community.glean.com), where the Support Center widget is available.
-   Have the details of your issue ready. See [What to include](#what-to-include) for the full list.

## What to include[​](#what-to-include "Direct link to What to include")

A clear and complete ticket helps our support team resolve your issue faster. Include these details so we have the full context:

-   **Issue description** — A clear, detailed explanation of the problem, including what you were attempting to do when the issue occurred and any steps to reproduce it.
-   **Error messages** — The exact text or error code that was displayed.
-   **Timing information** — The date and time when the issue occurred, along with your timezone, so support can correlate it with system logs.
-   **User impact** — Whether the issue affects just your account or multiple users.
-   **Document reference** — For issues related to specific content, such as missing search results or permission problems, the relevant URL.
-   **Visual evidence** — Screenshots or recordings that demonstrate the issue, when possible.

## Priority levels[​](#priority-levels "Direct link to Priority levels")

Choosing the right priority level helps our support team allocate resources and manage response times.

### Low priority

Choose this level for non-critical issues that don't impact your daily operations. Examples include:

-   General product inquiries
-   Feature requests
-   Questions about future functionality

### Normal priority

Select this level for issues that affect some aspects of your operations but don't significantly hinder your workflow. These issues require attention but aren't time-critical.

### High priority

Use this level when facing issues that seriously impact your ability to use the product or service effectively. These problems need prompt attention to restore normal operations.

### Urgent priority

Reserve this level for critical issues that:

-   Cause a complete halt to your operations
-   Severely impact your business functionality
-   Require immediate resolution, such as system outages

note

Accurate priority classification helps our support team triage and address all customer needs. Over-escalating priority levels can slow down resolution for genuinely critical issues.

## Steps to create a ticket[​](#steps-to-create-a-ticket "Direct link to Steps to create a ticket")

If you are a Designated Support Contact and need to formally track an issue:

1

Open the support widget

Sign in to the [Gleaniverse](https://community.glean.com) and select the support widget in the bottom-right corner of the page.

2

Select Create a ticket

Choose **Create a ticket** from the support widget home screen.

![Support widget home showing Send us a message and Create a ticket options](/img/troubleshooting/support-widget-home.png)

3

Fill out the ticket form

Follow the prompts to describe your issue, select a priority level, and submit.

## View your ticket history[​](#view-your-ticket-history "Direct link to View your ticket history")

After you create your first ticket, a **Tickets** section appears in the support widget. This section lets you view the status and history of your support tickets.

note

The Tickets section only appears after you have created your first ticket. If you do not see it, you may not have submitted a ticket yet, or ticket creation may not be available for your account.

## View all company conversations and tickets[​](#view-all-company-conversations-and-tickets "Direct link to View all company conversations and tickets")

If you are a Designated Support Contact, you can view all support conversations and tickets submitted by anyone in your company through the **customer portal**.

To access the customer portal, open the support widget from [Gleaniverse](https://community.glean.com) and look for the **Go to customer portal** banner. This banner appears at the top of both the **Messages** and **Tickets** sections of the support widget.

Once in the customer portal, use the dropdown at the top of the page to switch between **Conversations** and **Tickets**.

Important

You **must** access the customer portal through the link inside the support widget. Don't bookmark or navigate directly to `ticket.glean.com` — the portal will not recognize your identity unless you go through the authenticated Gleaniverse path. Always start from [Gleaniverse](https://community.glean.com), open the support widget, and select **Go to customer portal**.
