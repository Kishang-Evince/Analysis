On this page

Start a conversation to ask a question or get quick help from Glean's support assistant. Conversations are available to **all users** and are the best starting point for most requests. Think of a conversation as chatting with support, not filing a formal ticket.

## Prerequisites[​](#prerequisites "Direct link to Prerequisites")

To start a conversation with Support:

-   Have a [Gleaniverse](https://community.glean.com) account. Conversations are available to **all users**, but you must sign in to reach the support widget. Previous logins from the legacy support portal were not migrated, so you must [create a new account](/troubleshooting/support-center/).
-   Confirm access to the Support Center widget in the bottom-right corner of Gleaniverse after signing in.

## Steps to start a conversation[​](#steps-to-start-a-conversation "Direct link to Steps to start a conversation")

1

Open the support widget

Sign in to the [Gleaniverse](https://community.glean.com) and select the support widget in the bottom-right corner of the page.

2

Select Send us a message

Choose **Send us a message** to begin a conversation with Glean's support assistant.

![Support widget home showing Send us a message and Create a ticket options](/img/troubleshooting/support-widget-home.png)

3

Describe your issue

The assistant helps triage your request and connect you with the right resources. If the assistant can't resolve your issue, it guides you through submitting a support ticket.

You can view your past conversations at any time by opening the support widget and selecting **Messages**.

If your issue needs formal tracking or escalation, see [Tickets](/troubleshooting/support-center/tickets).
