## Issue[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue")

Gitlab Personal Access Token provided may be incorrectly formatted.

## Resolution[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution")

-   Ensure you are using a personal access token. You can find more information here [https://docs.gitlab.com/user/profile/personal\_access\_tokens/](https://docs.gitlab.com/user/profile/personal_access_tokens/)

For additional assistance, please contact Glean Support at [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
