## Issue:[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue:")

There was an unexpected issue during setup.

## Resolution:[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution:")

If you were configuring a connector:

-   Please ensure that the email used to sign into Glean matches the email used to sign into the connector.
-   Ensure that there are no IP allowlists in place that would prevent Glean from accessing the connector.
-   Ensure that the admin authorizing the Glean integration has full priviledges to do so across the entire connector.

Please reach out to Glean Support for additional assistance: [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
