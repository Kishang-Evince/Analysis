## Issue:[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue:")

-   The proxy is reachable and the hostname resolves, but a connection to the endpoint could not be established.

## Resolution:[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution:")

-   Verify that the endpoint is up and accepting connections on the expected port.
-   Check firewall rules to ensure the proxy VM is allowed to reach the endpoint.
-   Confirm that the port number configured for the connector instance is correct.

If you are still having difficulty please reach out to us: [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
