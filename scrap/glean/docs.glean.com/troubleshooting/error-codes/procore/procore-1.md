## Issue[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue")

We are unable to fetch permission templates from your Procore instance.

## Resolution:[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution:")

1.  Please ensure that the service account has been configured properly and the credentials provided are valid.
2.  If the credentials are valid, please ensure that the service account has 'Admin' privileges to the Company Directory. See [Edit a user account in the Company Directory](https://support.procore.com/products/online/user-guide/company-level/directory/tutorials/edit-a-user-account-in-the-company-directory) for more information.

If you are still facing issues, please reach out to us: [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
