## Issue:[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue:")

-   We could verify that the Glean Slack App is installed in any workspaces.

## Resolution:[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution:")

-   Please verify the Glean Slack App is installed in all workspaces in your Slack org.

1.  Open [Slack](https://app.slack.com/client) on web.
2.  Find your organization in the top left. Click the organization name, then **Tools & settings → Organization settings**.
3.  Click Integrations → Installed apps.
4.  Find and click the Glean app you created earlier.
5.  In the top right, click **Manage → Approve for organization**. Approve the scopes.
6.  In the top right, click **Manage → Add to workspaces**. Select all workspaces by clicking the top-most checkbox. Also select **Default for future workspaces**.
7.  Click Next to approve the scopes.

For additional assistance, please contact Glean Support at [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
