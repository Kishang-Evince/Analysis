## Issue[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue")

-   ServiceNow knowledge article template fetches are enabled and Glean is unable to fetch the knowledge article template table as it likely does not exist.

## Resolution[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution")

-   If your organization should have knowledge article templates, ensure they exist. See the [ServiceNow knowledge article templates documentation](https://www.servicenow.com/docs/bundle/washingtondc-servicenow-platform/page/product/knowledge-management/concept/knowledge-article-templates.html) for more details.
-   If your organization should not have knowledge article templates, uncheck the setting `Enable fetching template-based knowledge articles`.

For additional assistance, please contact Glean Support at [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
