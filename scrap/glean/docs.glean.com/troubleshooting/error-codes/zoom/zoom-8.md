## Issue:[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue:")

-   The Zoom recordings fetch failed.

## Resolution:[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution:")

-   The Glean application must be installed to the Zoom instance as the owner or admin of the Zoom account.
