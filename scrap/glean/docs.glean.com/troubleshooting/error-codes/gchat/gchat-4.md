## Issue:[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue:")

At least one of the Space red lists is populated when at least one of the green lists is also populated.

## Resolution:[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution:")

-   Edit the config such that at most one type of lists is populated: Green Lists or Red Lists.

If you are still having difficulty please reach out to us: [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
