## Issue[​](#issue "Direct link to Issue")

-   OAuth token generation failed during Atlassian connector setup.

## Resolution[​](#resolution "Direct link to Resolution")

-   Verify that audience scopes and authorization URLs are correctly configured in your Atlassian application settings.
-   Otherwise, contact Glean support for assistance.

If you are still having difficulty please reach out to us: [https://support.glean.com](https://support.glean.com)
