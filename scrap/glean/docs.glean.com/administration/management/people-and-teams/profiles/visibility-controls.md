Go to **Admin console → Users and permissions → People data → Manage settings**.

You can hide user profile attributes and social profiles to suit your company policy or preferences. Toggle the visibility of profile attributes and social icons shown in the People directory and on the Org Chart.

-   **Profile attributes**: Choose which details appear on a person's profile. You can control Bio, Title, Start date, Tenure, Number of direct reports, Department, Location, Phone number, and Alias emails.
-   **Social icons**: Hide some or all of the social profile links shown on a person's profile. The available options depend on which connected sources provide profile links, such as Slack and Microsoft Teams.
