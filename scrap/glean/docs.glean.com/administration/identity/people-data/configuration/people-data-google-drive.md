On this page

To sync people data from Google Drive, follow the steps at [Connect to Google Drive](/connectors/native/gdrive/setup).

When you connect Google Drive to Glean, you also connect documents and folders in Google Drive to Glean. The people data setup and the connector setup are the same process.
