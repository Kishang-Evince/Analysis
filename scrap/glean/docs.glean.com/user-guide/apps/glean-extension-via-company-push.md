On this page

Your company may install the Glean browser extension for you. When that happens, Glean can open as your homepage when you create a new browser tab. This page explains why that happens and how to change it.

## Why Glean opens in new browser tabs[​](#why-glean-opens-in-new-browser-tabs "Direct link to Why Glean opens in new browser tabs")

Your organization force-installed the Glean extension after reviewing security and usefulness for your workplace. Force-installing can also make Glean the page that appears when you open a new tab.

To learn more about Glean, see [What is Glean?](/user-guide/about/what-is-glean).

## Restore your previous homepage[​](#restore-your-previous-homepage "Direct link to Restore your previous homepage")

After you sign in to Glean, go to [Settings](https://app.glean.com/settings) and under **Preferences** toggle off **Use Glean as new tab page**.

### Restore a blank browser tab[​](#restore-a-blank-browser-tab "Direct link to Restore a blank browser tab")

Use **Home page settings** as described earlier to stop Glean from opening on new tabs.

### Restore another homepage extension[​](#restore-another-homepage-extension "Direct link to Restore another homepage extension")

If you preferred a previous homepage extension (for example, Momentum, Scratchpad, or Earth View), uninstall that extension, then reinstall it. The previous homepage experience should return.

## Other ways to open Glean[​](#other-ways-to-open-glean "Direct link to Other ways to open Glean")

Even after you change the homepage setting, you can still use Glean in these ways:

-   Type **`gl`**, then Space or Tab, then your query in the browser address bar
-   Press **Command + J** (Mac) or **Alt + J** (Windows) to open the [extension sidebar](/user-guide/apps/extension-sidebar)
-   Go to [app.glean.com](https://app.glean.com/)
-   Use [Glean for Desktop](/user-guide/apps/glean-for-desktop)
