On this page

Install the Glean browser extension to access the sidebar, Glean homepage, and companion from your browser. You can install from Glean setup or from your browser's extension store.

info

If your team is moving from embedded support integrations in Zendesk, ServiceNow, or Service Cloud, installing and signing in to the browser extension is the first step toward the replacement experience. Then follow [Extension sidebar](/user-guide/apps/extension-sidebar#agents) to use Chat, Search, and Agents in the browser.

## Downloading browser extension[​](#downloading-browser-extension "Direct link to Downloading browser extension")

You have two options to install the extension:

-   Visit [https://app.glean.com/setup](https://app.glean.com/setup) and follow the guided installation process for your browser.
-   Install the extension through your browser's extension app store:
    -   [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/glean/cfpdompphcacgpjfbonkdokgjhgabpij)
    -   [Firefox Browser Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/glean/)
    -   [Safari](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/glean-for-safari/id6444195239)
    -   [Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/glean/lkhkfgdfpoijiiinpjgcgacdkgjeieak)
    -   [Brave](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/glean/cfpdompphcacgpjfbonkdokgjhgabpij)

The browser extension supports Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, and Brave. After installation, sign in to Glean to use the sidebar.

Your admin may have installed the extension

If your organization manages browser extensions centrally, the extension may already be installed on your device. Open it and sign in instead of installing it again. Admins can see [Deploy the browser extension to managed devices](/administration/management/features/browser/deployment) for centralized deployment.

For an explanation of the permissions requested by the extension, see [Browser extension permissions](/user-guide/apps/why-does-the-chrome-extension-ask-for-those-permissions).
