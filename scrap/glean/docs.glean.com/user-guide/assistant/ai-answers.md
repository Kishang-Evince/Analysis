On this page

AI Answers shows a direct answer to your query. When appropriate, answers appear at the top of your search results. Each answer draws from your organization's documents and includes citations, so you can check where the information came from.

### Permission-aware

Answers use only content you already have permission to see. They never surface information from documents you can't access.

### Consistent answers

Similar questions return consistent answers, so you can rely on what you get back.

## Ask in natural language[​](#ask-in-natural-language "Direct link to Ask in natural language")

AI Answers works best when you ask a full question rather than type keywords. Phrase it the way you'd ask a colleague, and Glean has more to work with when it builds the answer.

### Example

Instead of searching `expense policy`, ask `How much can I expense on a hotel?`
