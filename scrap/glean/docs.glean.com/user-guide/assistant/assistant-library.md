On this page

Library is a dedicated place where you can browse, search, and revisit the content you create in Glean, as well as discover those your colleagues have shared with you.

![The Library page on the Artifacts tab, with a Created by me filter, a Type filter, and a sort menu set to Recently viewed.](/img/user-guide/assistant/library-created-by-me.png)

Library opens on the Artifacts tab, showing what you created.

You can use Library to:

-   Reopen a document you created last week
-   Find a slide deck or image you want to reuse
-   Browse interactive artifacts colleagues across departments have shared

## Open Library[​](#open-library "Direct link to Open Library")

To open Library, select **Library** in the left navigation.

## Browse by content type[​](#browse-by-content-type "Direct link to Browse by content type")

The tabs at the top of the page organize your content.

| Tab | What you'll find |
| --- | --- |
| **Artifacts** | Documents, slides, interactive artifacts, and everything else you create in a conversation with Glean. |
| **Projects** | [Projects](/user-guide/knowledge/projects/how-projects-work) that keep related chats, documents, and artifacts together in one workspace. |
| **Meeting notes** | [Meeting notes](/user-guide/assistant/meeting-notes) captured from your calls, so you can revisit decisions and follow-ups. |
| **Go Links** | [Go Links](/user-guide/knowledge/go-links/how-go-links-work), the short **go/link** bookmarks people use across your company. |
| **More** | [Answers](/user-guide/knowledge/answers/what-are-answers-and-how-do-they-work) and [Announcements](/user-guide/knowledge/announcements/how-announcements-work). |

Which tabs you see depends on the features enabled in your organization.

Click any artifact to open it alongside the conversation where it was created. From there you can keep editing it, share it, or export it.

## Find artifacts[​](#find-artifacts "Direct link to Find artifacts")

The **Artifacts** tab opens on **Created by me**, so your own work comes first. Use **Type** to narrow the list to **Audio**, **Documents**, **Emails**, **Images**, **Interactive**, **Messages**, **Slides**, or **Spreadsheets**.

Sort the list by **Recently viewed**, which is the default, or by **Created date**.

To find one specific item, use **Search library** at the top of the page.

## Find artifacts your teammates created[​](#find-artifacts-your-teammates-created "Direct link to Find artifacts your teammates created")

Switch **Created by me** to **Shared with me** to see what other people have published. Two more filters appear: **Creator**, which narrows the list to specific people, and **Department**, which narrows it to a team.

![The Library page on the Artifacts tab with the Shared with me filter selected, showing additional Creator, Department, and Type filters.](/img/user-guide/assistant/library-shared-with-me.png)

Shared with me adds Creator and Department filters.

A few use cases:

-   Filter to **Slides** from the Sales department to find a customer deck you can adapt for your own account.
-   Filter to **Interactive** from Data Science to see the dashboards that team has built.

note

**Shared with me** shows only the artifacts someone shared with you directly and the artifacts marked **Visible across your entire org via Search and Library**. Anything shared by link alone stays out of Library.

## Share content across your organization[​](#share-content-across-your-organization "Direct link to Share content across your organization")

When you share content, you choose who can open it and whether people can discover it in **Library** and Glean search.

### Change who can access your content[​](#change-who-can-access-your-content "Direct link to Change who can access your content")

1.  Open the content you want to share.
    
2.  Click **Share**.
    
3.  Under **People with access**, add the groups, departments, and teammates who need the content. You keep the **Owner** role on anything you created.
    
    ![The People with access section of the Share dialog, with a field to add a group, department, or teammate, and the creator listed below as Owner.](/img/user-guide/assistant/share-people-with-access.png)
    
    Add a group, department, or teammate under People with access.
    
4.  Under **General access**, choose one of the following:
    
    -   **Restricted to only people with access** — only the groups, departments, and teammates you added can open the content.
    -   **Anyone at \[company name\] can view** — anyone at your company can open the content.
    
    ![The General access menu in the Share dialog, expanded to show two options: Restricted to only people with access, and Anyone at your company can view.](/img/user-guide/assistant/share-general-access.png)
    
    The General access menu shows your own company name in the second option.
    
5.  If you chose **Anyone at \[company name\] can view**, select **Visible across your entire org via Search and Library** to make the content discoverable. Leave it cleared to keep the content unlisted, so people can open it only if they have the link.
    
    ![The Share dialog with General access set to allow anyone at your company to view, showing an Advanced section with the checkbox Visible across your entire org via Search and Library.](/img/user-guide/assistant/share-visible-across-org.png)
    
    The Advanced checkbox appears only after you allow anyone at your company to view the content.
    
6.  To send the content to someone directly, click **Copy link** and share the link.
    

### Sharing options[​](#sharing-options "Direct link to Sharing options")

Everyone you add under **People with access** finds the content in their own Library under **Shared with me**, whichever **General access** option you choose. The **Visible across your entire org via Search and Library** checkbox controls whether the rest of your company can discover it too.

| General access | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Restricted to only people with access** | Only the groups, departments, and teammates you add under **People with access**. |
| **Anyone at \[company name\] can view**, with **Visible across your entire org via Search and Library** cleared | Anyone at your company who has the link. |
| **Anyone at \[company name\] can view**, with **Visible across your entire org via Search and Library** selected | Anyone at your company. |

tip

Select **Visible across your entire org via Search and Library** when the content is useful across your organization, such as a reusable document, a team message template, or a reference asset. Leave it cleared when you want to share content more narrowly, without making it discoverable across your organization.

## FAQ[​](#faq "Direct link to FAQ")

### Can other people see my artifacts in Library?

No, not by default. Content you create in Glean is private by default. Other people can only see it if you share it directly with them, share it with a group they belong to, or make it visible across your organization.

### Why can't I find an artifact a teammate created?

The creator either kept it private or shared it by link without selecting **Visible across your entire org via Search and Library**. Ask them to share it with you directly or to make it visible org-wide.

### What happens if I don't select Visible across your entire org via Search and Library?

The content stays unlisted. Anyone at your company can open it if they have the link, but it doesn't appear in their **Library** or Glean search. The people you shared with directly still find it under **Shared with me**.

### What happened to Content in the left navigation?

Library replaces *Content* in the left navigation.

It gives you a single place to browse, filter, and search the content you create and save in Glean.

### How long does content stay in Library?

Content created with Glean stays in Library until it is deleted.

Standard chat responses follow the chat retention settings of your organization.
