On this page

The celebrations widget on the Glean homepage highlights new hires and work anniversaries so teammates can recognize each other. It shows a carousel of people celebrating during the current week.

## What is the celebrations widget?[​](#what-is-the-celebrations-widget "Direct link to What is the celebrations widget?")

The celebration widget appears on the Glean homepage and displays a carousel of users who have reached a new work anniversary or recently joined the team.

## How can I congratulate my teammate?[​](#how-can-i-congratulate-my-teammate "Direct link to How can I congratulate my teammate?")

Click **say welcome/congrats** to send a message to the celebrated teammate through your organization's chat application, such as Slack or Teams. If the teammate does not have a Slack or Teams profile, clicking on the button will launch your organization's email application instead.

## Who shows up on my widget?[​](#who-shows-up-on-my-widget "Direct link to Who shows up on my widget?")

Glean displays up to 10 celebrations in the widget at any time. You see a personalized selection of celebrations for users with whom you have a relationship. Cards are visible for the entire week of the celebration.

## What determines the order of the celebrations shown on the widget?[​](#what-determines-the-order-of-the-celebrations-shown-on-the-widget "Direct link to What determines the order of the celebrations shown on the widget?")

If you are being celebrated, your card will always appear first. Next, the widget will show users who have anniversaries or new start dates today, followed by users who have upcoming anniversaries, and then users whose anniversaries have passed.

## I don't want others to know about my celebration. How do I opt out?[​](#i-dont-want-others-to-know-about-my-celebration-how-do-i-opt-out "Direct link to I don't want others to know about my celebration. How do I opt out?")

1.  Select "Your settings" from the avatar dropdown menu.
    
2.  Under "Privacy," toggle off the option to feature your celebrations on the home page.
    

## Where does the celebration data come from?[​](#where-does-the-celebration-data-come-from "Direct link to Where does the celebration data come from?")

The celebrations widget uses start date data from your company's people connector (HR or IT system) to determine when to showcase particular users. If that source data is incorrect, you will see celebrations appear on incorrect days.
