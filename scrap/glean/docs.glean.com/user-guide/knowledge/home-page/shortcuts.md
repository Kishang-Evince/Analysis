On this page

Shortcuts are quick links to websites or pages you visit frequently. They appear on your Glean homepage so you can open common destinations without searching.

## Turn on shortcuts in your profile settings[​](#turn-on-shortcuts-in-your-profile-settings "Direct link to Turn on shortcuts in your profile settings")

1.  Click your profile icon and select **Your settings**.
2.  Under **Preferences**, go to the **General** section.
3.  Find **Show shortcuts** and toggle it on.

## How to add shortcuts[​](#how-to-add-shortcuts "Direct link to How to add shortcuts")

Once shortcuts are turned on, you can add personal shortcuts from your homepage.

1.  Go to your Glean homepage, and below the search bar click the **Manage shortcuts** menu (`⋮`).
2.  Select **My shortcuts**, then click **Add shortcut**.
3.  Enter the site name and URL, then click **Add**. The shortcut appears in the **Shortcuts** section on your homepage.

### Top sites[​](#top-sites "Direct link to Top sites")

**Top sites** automatically displays sites you visit most frequently. When enabled, these sites appear on your homepage for quick access.

note

While both Shortcuts and Go Links provide quick access, they are distinct features. Shortcuts are personal links to frequently visited sites. Go Links are company-wide, easy-to-remember URLs for internal resources.
