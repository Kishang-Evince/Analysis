On this page

Pets are optional interactive companions that live on your Glean page. Each pet is tied to a Glean feature, so using that feature can unlock a new pet.

Pets follow your cursor when it's nearby and wander around on their own. Depending on your available features, a pet can also alert you when a chat response, agent run, or meeting needs your attention.

## Before you start[​](#before-you-start "Direct link to Before you start")

Pets are part of Glean's [delight features](/administration/management/appearance/delight-features), which are turned on by default. If you don't see pets or can't unlock them, your admin may have turned off **User delight features** for your organization.

## Unlock Orbit first[​](#unlock-orbit-first "Direct link to Unlock Orbit first")

![Orbit pet](/img/pets/orbit.svg)

Orbit is the starter pet. You need to unlock Orbit before you can unlock any other pets.

To unlock Orbit, do either of these:

-   Click the greeting on your Glean home page.
-   Open **Settings → Pets** and select **Orbit**.

Actions you perform before unlocking Orbit don't count toward other pets. Unlock Orbit first, then go exploring.

## Unlock more pets[​](#unlock-more-pets "Direct link to Unlock more pets")

After you unlock Orbit, use Glean normally to discover more pets. When one appears, choose **Switch** to use it immediately, or dismiss it and select it later in **Settings → Pets**.

| Pet | Unlock by |
| --- | --- |
| ![Cat pet](/img/pets/cat.svg) **Cat** | Finish a chat |
| ![Duck pet](/img/pets/duck.svg) **Duck** | Run an agent |
| ![Fish pet](/img/pets/fish.svg) **Fish** | Share a chat |
| ![Frog pet](/img/pets/frog.svg) **Frog** | Run a Deep Research prompt |
| ![Dachshund or Gleaner Dog pet](/img/pets/gleaner-dog.svg) **Dachshund or Gleaner Dog** | Create an artifact |
| ![Glippy pet](/img/pets/glippy.svg) **Glippy** | Search |
| ![Goldie Dog pet](/img/pets/goldie-dog.svg) **Goldie Dog** | Visit your profile page |
| ![Sloth pet](/img/pets/sloth.svg) **Sloth** | Create an agent |
| ![Spooky pet](/img/pets/spooky.svg) **Spooky** | See an error page |
| ![Sprout pet](/img/pets/sprout.svg) **Sprout** | Share an artifact |

The dog can appear as **Dachshund** or **Gleaner Dog**, depending on your organization's branding and locale. **Glippy** is available only in Glean-branded experiences.

## Manage your pets[​](#manage-your-pets "Direct link to Manage your pets")

### Choose an active pet[​](#choose-an-active-pet "Direct link to Choose an active pet")

Open **Settings → Pets** to see which pets you've collected.

![User Delight settings with Orbit selected and pet unlock notifications turned on](/img/pets/pet-settings.webp)

From there, you can:

-   Select a pet to display it on your Glean page.
-   Select **No Pet** to hide your active pet.
-   Turn on **Notify me when I unlock a pet** to get an alert each time you find a new pet.

![Pet unlock screen for Orbit with Switch and Nah buttons and a notification setting](/img/pets/pet-unlock-screen.webp)

When you unlock a pet, select **Switch** to use it immediately. If you select **Nah**, you can turn it on later from **Settings → Pets**.

### Pet settings[​](#pet-settings "Direct link to Pet settings")

Click or hover over the pet, then click the **gear icon** to open **Settings**.

![Pet settings menu with Pet, Do not disturb, Hide, Alerts, and Reduced Motion controls](/img/pets/pet-settings-menu.webp)

From there, you can:

-   Switch out your active pet.
-   Snooze alerts from your pet.
-   Hide your pet for a set amount of time.
-   Configure available alerts for chats, agent runs, and meetings.
-   Configure **Reduced Motion**.

Enabling **Reduced Motion** keeps your pet in one spot. The pet stays visible but won't follow your cursor or pace around the page.

## Turn off pets[​](#turn-off-pets "Direct link to Turn off pets")

To hide your own pet, open **Settings → Pets** and select **No Pet**. To turn off pets for everyone in your organization, an admin can open the **Admin console**, navigate to **Settings → Appearance**, and turn off [**User delight features**](/administration/management/appearance/delight-features).
