On this page

Once your crawls are in progress, Glean kicks off the machine learning pipelines that build your company's knowledge graph. The **Prepare your company's knowledge graph** task is automatic — no admin action is required.

## What you'll see[​](#what-youll-see "Direct link to What you'll see")

In the **Prepare your workspace for better results** section, the **Prepare your company's knowledge graph** task is marked complete on your behalf as soon as the crawls and ML pipelines are running.

![Prepare your company's knowledge graph](/img/get-started/prepare-knowledge-graph-task.webp)

Prepare your company's knowledge graph

While Glean prepares your knowledge graph, you can:

-   Configure [Manage LLM and Assistant settings](/get-started/review/manage-llm-assistant)).
-   Review the [Crawling & learning process](/get-started/review/crawling-and-learning) for what's happening in the background.

note

Glean is not ready for end users until the ML pipelines have completed. Until then, search relevance, spellcheck, autocomplete, and Assistant responses are degraded. See [About machine learning](/get-started/review/crawling-and-learning#about-machine-learning) for impact and expected timelines.
