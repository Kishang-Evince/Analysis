# 4.9.7 Cost Comparison Position - Independent Research Test Guide

**Purpose:** Hands-on tenant verification of every claim in the companion research doc [V2/Cost Comparison Position.md](../../../../Glean/Combined/4.9.7%20Pricing%20&%20TCO/V2/Cost%20Comparison%20Position.md). This field is inherently analytical/competitive - Glean does not publish its own tier positioning, so desk research relied entirely on third-party procurement data. This guide's job is to benchmark that third-party picture against your organization's own actual quoted price, which is the only way to genuinely confirm where Glean sits relative to alternatives for your specific deal.
**Tenant entry:** Your organization's actual Glean quote/Order Form · any competing quotes obtained during procurement (Microsoft Copilot, Moveworks, Guru, or others) · your Glean account team
**Companion research doc:** [V2/Cost Comparison Position.md](../../../../Glean/Combined/4.9.7%20Pricing%20&%20TCO/V2/Cost%20Comparison%20Position.md)
**Sr No mapping:** Sr No 1-7 below map 1:1 to the same Sr No in the companion research doc's claims table.
**How to record a result:** Pass/Fail/Partial/Blocked + Notes. "Pass" means you personally confirmed it against your own quote/procurement data - not that a third-party site says so.
**Effort column:** time estimate + Easy/Hard tag - `Easy` (a quick page/quote check) or `Hard` (needs a written account-team answer or access to competing quotes).
