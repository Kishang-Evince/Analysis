# 4.9.7 Training & Enablement Cost - Independent Research Test Guide

**Purpose:** Hands-on tenant verification of every claim in the companion research doc [V2/Training & Enablement Cost.md](../../../../Glean/Combined/4.9.7%20Pricing%20&%20TCO/V2/Training%20%26%20Enablement%20Cost.md). Desk research found this cost is genuinely undocumented anywhere public - Glean's own contract terms are silent on training as a line item, and no customer-facing certification program was found to exist yet. This guide's job is to close that gap with a direct answer from Glean's own account team and your own signed Order Form, not to re-verify public pages a second time.
**Tenant entry:** Your organization's signed Glean Order Form/contract · your Glean account team (CSM/Solutions Architect, per the now-confirmed contract clause) · Admin Console → any Training/Resources section if present
**Companion research doc:** [V2/Training & Enablement Cost.md](../../../../Glean/Combined/4.9.7%20Pricing%20&%20TCO/V2/Training%20%26%20Enablement%20Cost.md)
**Sr No mapping:** Sr No 1-7 below map 1:1 to the same Sr No in the companion research doc's claims table.
**How to record a result:** Pass/Fail/Partial/Blocked + Notes. "Pass" means you personally confirmed it against your own contract/account team - not that the docs say so.
**Effort column:** time estimate + Easy/Hard tag - `Easy` (a quick console/page check) or `Hard` (needs a written account-team answer or contract-owner access).
